The second video is here! Today we have an overview and run through of the second 5-man dungeon currently available on Alpha, Atal'Dazar. It is set in a troll temple with various undead, and plauged mobs, along with a dinosaur or two mixed in. Of the two dungeons available, this is by far my favorite, so please have a look and let us know what you think of the dungeon!
