**This article is about using Serenity during the fight. It's about maximizing cooldown reduction and minimizing ability delay. It is NOT about the opener, and it is NOT about a strict rotation and purposefully delaying abilities that are off their cooldown.**

So with [Pandanaconda's fantastic post](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/08/17/serenity-theorycrafting/) covering [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) and all of its intricacies, I decided that it may benefit people to provide an abridged version.

#### What it does:

[Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is a 90s talented cooldown, lasting 8 seconds, that reduces the cost of all Chi spending abilities to 0, reduces their cooldowns by 50%, and increases damage done by 40% during its duration. It formerly refunded  the Chi cost, which is an important change from Warlords to Legion. The cooldown reduction works on spells that already are on cooldown, which is important for what follows. For example, if [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) has 10s left on its cooldown, when you pop [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), you suddenly have 5s left till you can cast it again.

#### How you think you use it:

This ability seems pretty straightforward, you want to use your hardest hitting abilities so that you can benefit from the 40% increased damage, and use them as often as possible so that you can maximize the benefit of the 50% reduced cooldowns. [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) is our biggest hitter; so, of course, you'd want to use it at least once during [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), and twice if possible.

Using [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) twice during [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) requires it to have less than an 8s cooldown, which means it needs a 16s cooldown when reduced by haste. With the 30% haste from [Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust), and during [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656)'s cooldown is reduced to 8.3s, so you need some additional haste, in order to make it possible to clip the end of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) with [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) in order to reduce the cooldown of the second [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) by 50%. This is achievable, but only during [Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust), and even then it was impossible to get enough haste for the second [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) to be covered by the 40% increased damage.

#### Why this is wrong:

Pandanaconda showed that, although it may seem important to try to fit two casts of [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) into the duration of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), and that *obviously*, you would want the full duration of [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) to benefit from 40% increased damage, the real answer may not be quite as obvious.

Although using [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) during [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) increases its damage by 40%, the channeling aspect of [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) means that for the 3-4s you're channeling, you're not casting anything else that can benefit from the bonuses of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173). Channeling a full duration [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) during the 8s window you have only leaves you with 4-5s for anything else. By channeling [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) at the very end of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), you snapshot the cooldown reduction which adds half a [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) per [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) use; so, it's roughly the same as 50% more [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) damage. This also allows you to use the 3-4s that you would be channeling [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) for increased damage and CD reduction on abilities like [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428), [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord), and [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick).

#### How you should use it:

The first step to maximizing it is counter-intuitive. You want to maximize the cooldown reduction of major abilities like [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656)and [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord). To do this, you want to take advantage of the fact that [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) reduces any cooldowns remaining in half as well. As I said above; " if [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) has 10s left on its cooldown, when you pop [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), you suddenly have 5s left till you can cast it again." This is the same for [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord).

This means that you want [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) and [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) to be on cooldown so that, once [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) cuts their remaining CD in half, they will still be usable during [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173). You want both to have less than 15s left on their cooldowns, and in order to use both, [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) should be at less than 13s left on its cooldown so that you have a GCD to use it before you start to channel [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) with the last second of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) for the cooldown reduction snapshot. Once you pop [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) these cooldowns will drop to less than 7.5s for [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) and less than 6.5s for [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord), allowing you to use them. So the last two GCD of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173)'s 8s buff should be [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) > [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656). **If either ability is up earlier in the Serenity buff, use them when they're available. Don't purposefully delay them to the last GCDs.**

So we've covered the last two globals, what about the preceding 6? [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) is the only other major ability we haven't mentioned that has a cooldown to be reduced. Right now, fitting two [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) into [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is easy, fitting a 3rd requires quite a bit of haste, and 4 is nearly impossible no matter how hard you try. So that takes care of two more globals, leaving us with 4 to use on [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) and [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick). [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) does more damage per cast, but does spin for 1.5s before haste, meaning that using two of them, with 0 haste, will use up 3 GCD.

**To copy directly from [Panda's post](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/08/17/serenity-theorycrafting/):**

An example of a [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) rotation is as follows:

[Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) -> [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) -> [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) ->[Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) -> [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656)

You can also modify this to have [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) towards the end if you started [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) with 12 seconds remaining on it; this is especially relevant for second/third uses of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173).

[Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) -> [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) -> [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) ->[Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) -> [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656)

#### When to use it:

This is the hardest question to answer. As was said in [Panda's post](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/08/17/serenity-theorycrafting/):

- [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is a moderately difficult talent to use optimally and needs to be used moderately well to be worth talenting into it. If you’re not comfortable with it or skilled enough to use it well, you may see it as a DPS loss compared to [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). Use at your own risk.
- It is not hard for [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) to compete with [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) due to the [bugs in SEF](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/10/11/what-is-up-with-sef-and-serenity/).
- On 2-5 targets cleave, [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) can dish out crazy amounts of damage, probably way more than its counterpart if played right, as you can manually tag all targets. Be aware of this possibility

This means that using [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is up largely to personal choice, situation, and skill. There will be players across all difficulties of content who will find [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) to be an increase to their performance. However, there will be others who will find that it is a detriment. This will not be restricted to "lesser skilled" players, there will be highly skilled Windwalkers who are not comfortable using it, or do not find themselves in a situation to benefit enough from it frequently, or simply do not enjoy using it.

Basically: Basically unless you can go "oh, this would be perfect for [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), and I know exactly how to use it." then go with [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). If you need the WHEN, HOW, or WHY answered for you, then don't use [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173).

This talent is the definition of counterintuitive. Thankfully, it's just one talent rather than half of the spec as it was in Warlords. Hopefully, this answers more questions than it creates.
