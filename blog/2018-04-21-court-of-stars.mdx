*This guide assumes that you have a knowledge of the mechanics of both bosses and trash in the dungeon and will take a more monk focused approach. For more information on the mechanics of all the different mobs and bosses in the dungeon check out the guide over at [Wowhead.](http://www.wowhead.com/court-of-stars-dungeon-strategy-guide)*

## Talents

The recommended talents for Court of Stars are:\
-[Chi Wave](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave)\
-[Tiger's Lust\](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust)\
-[Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir)\
-[Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep)\
-[Healing Elixirs](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122281/healing-elixir)\
-[Hit Combo](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo)\
-[Whirling Dragon Punch.](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch)

The damage talents selected are taken due to being the current best setup in all scenarios, there is no real deviation in talents for aoe/st.

[Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can be used to dispel roots in this dungeon and as such is superior to [Chi Torpedo](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115008/chi-torpedo) in this dungeon.

There is no real huge spike damage in Court of Stars and as such [Healing Elixirs](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122281/healing-elixir) are best suited to help combat the mostly ticking damage.

## Boss Tips

#### Patrol-Captain Gerdo

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips:**\
-You can use [Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) to dispel [Arcane Lockdown](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=207278/arcane-lockdown) from yourself or anybody else, it can be useful to dispel one of the ranged so they don't have to move or jump and they can focus on their dps more.\
-Other than this just focus on maximizing your ST damage and cleave any adds down that spawn.

#### Talixae Flamewreath

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips**:\
-Building up 10 [Transfer the Power](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195300/transfer-the-power) stacks for the imp spawns can be incredibly helpful to burst them down quickly, depending on the comp you're running as well you can considering saving a [SEF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) stack for them. If your group has very high ST damage and maybe slightly less AoE (Assa rogue or Enhance Shaman for example) that'd be a good time to save it.

#### Advisor Melandrus

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269) / [Prydaz, Xavaric's Magnum Opus](http://www.wowhead.com/item=132444/prydaz-xavarics-magnum-opus)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips**:\
-In terms of damage there isn't much here other than just play your normal ST rotation. However, on higher keys [Slicing Maelstrom](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=209676/slicing-maelstrom) does very high damage and surviving can be pretty hard. You want to maximize your healing as much as your possibly can, using [Touch of Karma](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) when on half health or lower to maximize the healing from the trait as well as using [Transcendence for healing](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) and being smart with [Healing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122281/healing-elixir) usage.

## Trash Tips

#### Leg Sweep

**Notable packs to be ready to** **stun:\** -Stunning the Mana Sabers so that they don't cast [Mana Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=209516/mana-fang) will ensure that everyone stays alive on packs that include them.\
-The Watchful Inquisitor's Eye Storm cast does heavy damage to players and should be interrupted with [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep).\
-The large packs of imps cast too many things at once to interrupt them all separately and therefore [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) and [Arcane Torrent](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=129597/arcane-torrent) will be very helpful for dealing with them cleanly.\
-Having [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) ready for add spawns on Talixae can help kill them before they do much damage to any players.

#### Kick

**Important casts to look out for and prioritize kicks on:\** -[Sound Alarm](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=210261/sound-alarm)\
-[Nightfall Orb](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=209410/nightfall-orb) (If you do the harbor pull)\
-[Supress](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=209413/suppress)\
-[Charging Station](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=225100/charging-station)\
-[Hinder](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=215204/hinder) (If you don't disable all alarms)\
-[Searing Glare](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=211299/searing-glare)\
-[Withering Soul](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=208165/withering-soul) (on Talixae)

#### Paralysis

You can use [Paralysis](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) on the front mob of any patrol packs to stop the entire patrol from moving, a good use for this is the large imp packs so that you don't have to deal with a bunch of them while you're doing another pack at the same time.

#### Tiger's Lust

As mentioned earlier you can dispel [Arcane Lockdown](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=207278/arcane-lockdown) on the first boss using [Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust).
