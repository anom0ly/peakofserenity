So since I started doing the video guides, and spent more time finishing the spreadsheet (we'll get to that), so I've been neglecting some of the changes to the 7.2 PTR builds. So lets catch up.

##### 7.2 PTR Build 23578

- **Monk T20 Windwalker 2P Bonus:**  [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428) applies Rising Fist to the target for 8 sec, increasing the critical strike chance of your [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) against the target by 25%.
- **Monk T20 Windwalker 4P Bonus:**  [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) increases the critical strike chance of your next [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428) by 50%.

These are the new tier bonuses (boni?) for T20. These bonuses are both relatively passive and single target focused, although the 2pc bonus could, theoretically be placed on two different targets for double the crit increase. This does require a certain amount of haste and/or the t19 2pc bonus that reduces the cooldown of [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428).

Early napkin math has them slightly stronger than current t19 Bonus, and there's the added benefit of being able to mix and match with 2pc of one tier and 2 or 4pc of the other tier. Its possible, if not likely, that this will be the preferred method until a certain item level breakpoint, which I'll figure out as we get closer to 7.2's release.

##### 7.2 PTR Build 23623

Many legendaries received some tweaks to their stat distributions, so I won't go about listing them since they really won't effect much.

- **[Drinking Horn Cover](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=209256)**   The duration of ~~Storm~~ Serenity / Storm, Earth, and Fire is extended by ~~0.6~~0.4 sec every time you cast a Chi spender / 0.6 sec for every Chi you spend.

This seems to be a relatively confusing thing for many people to read. Basically all it does is add [Serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173) to [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097), the **functionality between [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097) and [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) isn't changed at all**. When [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097) is equipped, [Serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173)'s buff will be increased by 0.4s per Chi spender you cast. This puts the maximum duration of [Serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173) at 13s, assuming you cast one Chi spender every GCD. If you use [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) during [Serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173), which will very likely be best, then [Serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173) will max out at 11s. Its unclear what, if any, effect it will have on the balance of talents, but I am relatively confused about the reasoning behind it, but I'll leave that discussion for a podcast rant. I can't wait to implement this into my spreadsheet... not.

- **[Thunderfist](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238131)**   Strike of the Windlord grants you Thunderfist for each enemy struck. Thuderfists discharge upon melee strikes, dealing 0 Nature damage.
  Name changed from Cestus of Storms to Thunderfist

Obviously just a name change, nothing to see here, move along.

- **[Concordance of the Legionfall](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=239042)**   ~~Increases damage dealt by 1%. [PH]~~Your abilities have a chance to trigger Concordance of the Legionfall, increasing your primary stat by 2000 for 10 sec.

This is the new trait that replaces the previous [Windborne Blows](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=214922/windborne-blows) in 7.2. This scales from 2000 Agility at rank 1 to 11800 Agility at rank 50, with an average proc per minute set at 1.37. Raise your hand if you're excited for another buff to monitor and react to... anyone? For more information on how this changes from the current system, there are ample resources on Wowhead discussing the topic, so I wont reiterate it here. Short version is that its meant to be an AP dump after you hit all the other traits, and the "grind" is much longer and harder (thats what she said) than it is currently.
