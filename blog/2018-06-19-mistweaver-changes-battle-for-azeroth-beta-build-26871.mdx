This week's build brought some slight retuning of abilities.

### Changes This Build

- [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) healing reduced from 175% Spell power over its duration to 140% Spell power.
- [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) mana cast reduced from 3.0% mana per cast to 2.8% mana per cast.
- [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) healing reduced from 360% Spell power over its duration to 320% Spell power.
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) targeted heal reduced from 120% Spell power to 105% Spell power.
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) heal on [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) targets reduced from 80% Spell power to 70% Spell power.
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) mana cost increased from 3.2% mana per cast to 3.5% mana per cast.

### Speculation

All this build really does is punish Mistweaver's dungeon healing. The [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) changes were half expected since it was brought in at the time that [Uplifting Trance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197206/uplifting-trance) was removed and [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) was given another charge. The [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) and [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) changes aren't strong enough to shake anything up in terms of spell priority since you cast mainly cast each spell for a reason other than their healing. [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) for [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) healing and [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) as a vehicle for [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) and [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) casts to be funneled into a single target. However, they are fairly strange in that they hadn't really been an overpowered spell like [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) had been since the last build.

In terms of raid healing, however, this build throws a bit of a wrench into kickweaving, the talent build that revolves around [Spirit of the Crane](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=210802/spirit-of-the-crane) and [Rising Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist). If you're in a situation where [Refreshing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196725/refreshing-jade-wind) looks appealing, you're likely to just not worry about casting [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on cooldown and go all in on Player-Based AoE healing (PBAoE) with [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font), [Refreshing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196725/refreshing-jade-wind), and [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) healing via [Rising Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) and just fill with damage to regain mana through [Spirit of the Crane](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=210802/spirit-of-the-crane), dumping mana through [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) on any target low enough that they're in danger of dying.
