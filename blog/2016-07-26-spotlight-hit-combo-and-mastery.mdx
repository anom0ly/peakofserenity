Just a few days into the new class changes and both [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) and [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) are already causing a ton of discussion; is it OK to drop it? Should I delay X until Y stacks? (Hint: No) and so on. Well, rather than talk it out I thought I'd go through and make a list. I assume this will be the first of eventually several posts about [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) and [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) over the next few months.

##### What they do:

Both [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) and [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) provide a flat damage increase to all abilities, provided that you don't use the same ability twice in a row. [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) stacks up to 8 stacks, with each providing 2% increased damage per stack, and [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) provides the flat bonus with no stacks involved. If you use the same ability twice in a row, [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) will drop all its stacks and you'll have to start from 0, whereas [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) only looks at the ability just used and the one preceding it.

##### What works:

Here is a comprehensive list of Windwalker abilities. I tested each ability for 3 things, Does it benefit from [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes)? Does it generate a stack of [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740)? Does it count as a separate filler ability, ie, can you [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) > X > [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) with gaining the Mastery bonus on the second [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm). Many of these seem redundant and obvious that they wouldn't' work, but for the sake of being thorough, I'm trying them all.

As you can see, it is pretty straightforward. Nearly all damaging abilities benefit from [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) and if they benefit from [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) they generate a stack of [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740), with the exception of [Rushing Jade Wind](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=116847), which obviously doesn't generate a stack of [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) since they're on the same talent tier.

##### What this means:

Obviously it doesn't mean a whole lot. What it does mean is that some abilities that wouldn't necessarily be used in a rotation, like [Flying Serpent Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101545) and [Crackling Jade Lightning](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning), can be used in dire circumstances to keep up [Mastery: Combo Strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) or not drop [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740). Situations like this shouldn't happen often and are more likely to be in your head than in reality. The truth is that sometimes you'll have to just sit and wait for your other abilities to come off cooldown rather than spam [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) into [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kickhttp://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) to fill GCDs.

There will, on occasion, come a point where you'll be about to cap Energy, so you need to [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) to prevent from capping, but if you use [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) then you'll over cap Chi. In this situation using [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kickhttp://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) followed by [Flying Serpent Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101545) or [Crackling Jade Lightning](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning) enables you to [Blackout Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kickhttp://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) again, and drop 2 Chi so you can [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) again without overcapping\*\*. This assumes you have 3 GCDs available before you'll cap energy, and at least 4 GCDs before anything valuable comes off cooldown. An alternative to this situation is also to just use [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) to dump Chi as a faster, but less damaging, alternative which only requires 1 GCD to drop 3 Chi and free up space for more [Tiger Palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) before another major ability, like [Fists of Fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656) is available.

\*\*Note: its not recommended to try and actively weave [Flying Serpent Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101545) or [Crackling Jade Lightning](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning) into the rotation to increase your damage regularly as they both need to be canceled immediately or you'll lose more damage than you gain, making this incredibly difficult for a very small gain.\*\*
