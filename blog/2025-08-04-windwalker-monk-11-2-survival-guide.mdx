**As always, information on this page may change over the first days and weeks of the new patch as new things are discovered or tested.**

## Class Changes

- Large 13% aura increase ([Windwalker Monk](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=137025/windwalker-monk)).
- You can use non-channel and non-cast abilities during [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) (undocumented).

## What To Do

#### Tier

The Season 3, the tier set is VERY strong, so you will likely want to **get it ASAP**, perhaps even dropping item level to do so. Sim yourself for the best answers, but don't be shocked if it happens.

#### Talents/Rotation

**Shado-Pan for basically everything.** Conduit can be used for short single target fights (sub 5-minutes), but Shado-Pan is best all the other times.

- If playing Shado-Pan, do what you can to not cap energy, overcapping Chi is less than ideal, but much less important.
- If playing Conduit of the Celestials, use [Slicing Winds](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1217413/slicing-winds) right after your [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) to stack the [Heart of the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent) buffs together.

#### Stats

No change: **Agility >> Mastery = Haste >> Versatility = Critical Strike**

#### 2H vs 1H

**2-handed weapons are best, get the one from [Ara-Kara, City of Echoes](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/zone=15093/ara-kara-city-of-echoes), [Harvester's Interdiction](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=221159/harvesters-interdiction?bonus=657:12052:5877:10390:12361).**

#### [Durable Information Securing Container](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=245964/durable-information-securing-container)

**You will replace this with a higher item level belt when you get one.** Sim yourself to be sure, but that is the most likely path. Best chance is that it will be replaced with your first 11.2 embellishment recraft.

#### Embellishments

[Duskthread Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=222873/duskthread-lining) or [Dawnthread Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=222870/dawnthread-lining) to Bracers and Boots until you can craft a higher item level Belt than you [Durable Information Securing Container](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=245964/durable-information-securing-container) Belt then **Bracers and Belt**. This frees up your Cloak for [Reshii Wraps](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=235499/reshii-wraps) and [Interloper's Reinforced Sandals](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=243306/interlopers-reinforced-sandals).

#### Trinkets

Ideally you want [Araz's Ritual Forge](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=242402/arazs-ritual-forge?bonus=7981:12052:5877:12361) and an equip trinket like [Astral Antenna](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=242395/astral-antenna?bonus=7981:12052:5877:12361). As always, **sim your options** to see what’s best for you in particular.

#### Consumables

- You will likely want to use the "gain X of your highest secondary stat for 1 hour" feasts like [The Sushi Special](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=222720/the-sushi-special) or food like [Beledar's Bounty](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=222728/beledars-bounty).
- [Enchant Weapon - Stonebound Artistry](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=223765/enchant-weapon-stonebound-artistry) to your weapon.

#### Macros

With the recent change to [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick), we can finally cast abilities that have no cast or channel time at the end of its GCD without interrupting the channel any more. [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) buffed by [Vivacious Vivification](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388812/vivacious-vivification) still interrupts the channel of [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) even though it has no cast time.
Updated macros can be found [here in the PvE Guide](/tww/windwalker/pve-guide/#macros).
