### Changes

- Shado-pan

  - Added an energy tracker aura. This aura shows the player how much energy they have spent towards their next set of [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes).
  - Flurry Charges are no longer cancelable and no longer have a duration.
  - [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) unleash energy trigger threshold is now 240 energy (was 300).
  - [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) damage reduced by 8%.
  - [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) Accumulator Threshold increased by 47%.
  - [Wisdom of the Wall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450994/wisdom-of-the-wall) bonus effects duration reduced to 16 seconds (was 20%).
  - [Against All Odds](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450986/against-all-odds) duration reduced to 5 seconds (was 6 seconds).
  - *Developer's note: The goal of these changes is to allow  Shado-pan to activate [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) more frequently and have a better understanding of when the [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) are going to to be unleashed on their foes.*
- Windwalker

  - [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)'s damage increased by 20%.
  - [Flurry of Xuen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452137/flurry-of-xuen) damage increased by 40%.
  - Fixed an issue that caused [Courageous Impulse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=451495/courageous-impulse) to incorrectly buff the damage of [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) by more than intended when multiple stacks are active.
  - Fixed an issue that caused [Courageous Impulse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=451495/courageous-impulse) to incorrectly buff [Teachings of the Monastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery)'s [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)s.

### What They Mean

There isn't any big changes for Windwalker. This amounts to about 3% more ST damage for  Shado-pan but less for Conduit of the Celestials, and a 4-5% increase in AOE damage.

### "Bugs"

**The bug with SEF and [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) is no longer working.**

The Shado-pan interaction where you can desync your [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) charges for some additional damage is still in the game, you just have to set it up before doing any content as you cant use the Cancelaura method anymore (good).

1. Log out
2. Log back in
3. Go to a target dummy
4. Cast [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm)s on the dummy. Either 4 with [Inner Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=397768/inner-peace) or 3 without [Inner Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=397768/inner-peace)
5. Auto-attack dummy until you have 9 stacks of [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes)
6. Cast another [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) on the dummy to trigger [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes)
7. Don't leave melee range until the animation fades
8. Leave combat and enjoy a small boost to your [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) damage
9. Ideally get summoned into any instance for less chance that it resets

### Anniversary Blackrock Depths

With the 11.0.5 patch you can do Blackrock Depths. Much of the loot isn't currently in sims yet, but should be over the next few days. All of the gear caps out at Hero track, so it will almost certainly lose to Myth track gear, but it can be useful for lower geared characters and alts.

**If you get something, sim it.**
