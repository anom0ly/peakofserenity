## Stats

While we search for new stat gaps, using a variety of methods, follow these steps.

1. Agility is nearly always king. Equip the highest ilvl gear. If its within 5 ilvl or 10 for necks/rings, then you should sim them to be sure.
2. Follow the stat priority; Mastery > Crit > Vers > Haste for single target and Mastery > Haste > Crit > Vers for multi target.

##### Gems vs Item Level

This is always a difficult question to answer because each slot has its own stat budget. So I'll make a list. This list assumes that secondary stats you would use for gems are worth 60% of Agility. This is how many item levels a gem socket is worth for each slot, assuming a 150 secondary stat gem:

- Helm/Chest/Legs - 3 ilvl (7 ilvl for Agi gem)
- Neck/Ring - 7 ilvl (16 ilvl for Agi gem)
- Shoulders/Gloves/Waist/Boots - 4 ilvl (9 ilvl for Agi gem)
- Cloak/Bracers - 6 ilvl (13 ilvl for Agi gem)
