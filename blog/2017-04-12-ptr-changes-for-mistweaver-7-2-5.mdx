The early data-mining sweep from the 7.2.5 PTR build has thrown up two MW changes:

- **[Essence Font](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=191837)**   Unleashes a rapid twirl of healing bolts at up to 6 allies within 25 yds, every 1.002 sec for 3 sec. Each bolt heals a target for (110% of Spell power)
  Mistweaver (Level 58)
  plus an additional (~~36%~~48% of Spell power) over ~~6 sec.~~8 sec.
  Gust of Mists will heal affected targets twice
  .
  Cost changed from 8.0% of base mana to 7.2% of base mana
  Cooldown changed from None to 12 sec cooldown
- **[Essence Font](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=231633)**   Each bolt of Essence Font heals a target for an additional (~~36%~~48% of Spell power) over ~~6 sec.~~8 sec.
  Gust of Mists will heal affected targets twice.

##### Honor Talents (1)

- **[Ancient Mistweaver Arts](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=209520)**   Soothing Mist is now an active spell, and no longer is automatically applied through casting Effuse, Enveloping Mist or Vivify.

  Soothing Mist Honor Talen t0.0% of base mana, plus ~~0.3%~~0.2% per second 40 yd range Channeled 1 sec cooldown Requires Monk\
  Requires level 10 Heals the target for (3600% of Spell power) over 20 sec.\
  While channeling, Effuse and Enveloping Mist are instant cast, and will heal the soothed target without breaking the Soothing Mist channel.

**Obviously this is a very early iteration and when further information is known about the direction of MW in 7.2.5, we will post about it on here in-depth. Until then, sit tight and wait for more updates!**
