**As always, things on this page are subject to updates as we discover new things or things change.**

## Class Changes

- [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) damage increased by 10%.
- [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) damage increased by 5% and additional damage to primary target increased by 10%.
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) damage increased by 10%.
- Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible (2) Set Bonus: [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) bonus damage reduced to 5% (was 10%).
- Aberrus, the Shadowed Crucible (4) Set Bonus: [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) primary target damage reduced to 20% (was 30%).

These mean we do more ST damage once we're no longer using the Aberrus tier, nothing else changes.

## Other Changes

There aren’t any other functional changes. Most Embellishments received nerfs, so see below for that that means.

Talents stay, mostly, the same; with every talent setup making sure to grab [Dance of Chi-Ji](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325201/dance-of-chi-ji) to benefit the Amirdrassil tier set. [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is a reasonable option in AOE if you'd like to play with it, but you don't have to. Default ST and Cleave setups stick with [Faeline Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388193/faeline-stomp), and AOE uses [Faeline Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388193/faeline-stomp) or [Fatal Flying Guillotine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=394923/fatal-flying-guillotine).

## What To Do

#### Embellishments

With all the changes to embellishments, we're back to the same recommendation we were at for Season 2.

We have two pretty much equal options here: double [Shadowflame-Tempered Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204710/shadowflame-tempered-armor-patch), or [Slimy Expulsion Boots](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193451/slimy-expulsion-boots) with a [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch). They are simming very closely. [Slimy Expulsion Boots](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193451/slimy-expulsion-boots) with a [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch) is likely the easier to use and more flexible option between the two.

**If you want to go the Toxic route:** If you do not have one from Season 2 then you should craft a Season 2 item with [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch) ASAP before/as you use [Slimy Expulsion Boots](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193451/slimy-expulsion-boots). Common slots for this are belt or bracers.

1. Season 2 item with [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch) **if needed**
2. [Slimy Expulsion Boots](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193451/slimy-expulsion-boots)
3. Season 3 item with [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch) on another slot, like Bracers or Belt
4. Season 3 item with [Alchemical Flavor Pocket](https://www.wowhead.com/item=200652/alchemical-flavor-pocket) (does not count towards the 2 embellishment limit)

**If you want to go the Shadowflame route:** You'll simply craft [Shadowflame-Tempered Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204710/shadowflame-tempered-armor-patch) onto any two pieces. Any slot that will not be covered by tier is a good option due to higher item level.

1. [Shadowflame-Tempered Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204710/shadowflame-tempered-armor-patch)
2. [Shadowflame-Tempered Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204710/shadowflame-tempered-armor-patch)
3. Any free slot with [Alchemical Flavor Pocket](https://www.wowhead.com/item=200652/alchemical-flavor-pocket) (does not count towards the 2 embellishment limit)

**As always, sim yourself to see exact numbers for your own character.**

####

#### Tier

- The tier from Amirdrassil is strong enough to use, but likely a lower priority than other specs who get more out of it.
- Ideally you use the [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) with [Blackout Reinforcement](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=424454/blackout-reinforcement) once all the other abilities are on cooldown, it should be as simple as that.
  - You shouldn't run into the problem of potentially overwriting a proc with another due to the PPM nature of the proc, but safest choice is to use it before potentially generating another.

####

#### Trinkets

- As a general rule of thumb, our best trinkets for all content seem to be coming from raid: [Cataclysmic Signet Brand](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=207166/cataclysmic-signet-brand?bonus=7977:8807) and [Bandolier of Twisted Blades](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=207165/bandolier-of-twisted-blades?bonus=7977:8807).
- Dungeon trinkets worth mentioning are [My'das Talisman](https://www.wowhead.com/item=158319/mydas-talisman?bonus=657:9367:5881:7981) and [Mirror of Fractured Tomorrows](https://www.wowhead.com/item=207581/mirror-of-fractured-tomorrows?bonus=657:9367:5881:7981)

#### Consumables

- Food - You may reach the point of stat food being better than Agility food, sim your options to know for sure, but any difference is doing to be nearly negligible.
- Weapon Enchants - [Enchant Weapon - Sophic Devotion](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=199970/enchant-weapon-sophic-devotion) is likely best
- [Incandescent Essence](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=210494/incandescent-essence) is new, use it

**Sim your gear set up again to double check any changes that are needed.**
