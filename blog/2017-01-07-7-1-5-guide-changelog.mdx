Rather than split things into multiple posts, or into one post that is made irrelevant by the next, I will use this post to show the changes to the guides and articles, and continually update it as time goes on. When reading this article understand that few, if any, things are final as we continue to refine one thing, it may change something else.

#### Wordy Guide

###### Stats, Gems, and Enchants

###### Stat Summary

- No change

###### Stat Priority

- Added link to [How to SimCraft guide](http://www.walkingthewind.com/guides/how-to-simcraft/) to get stat weights.
- Split Stat Priority into Single Target and Multi Target, updated to current information

###### Gems

- No change

###### Enchants

- Added Multi Target Neck enchant - [Enchant - Soldier](https://www.wowhead.com/item=141909/enchant-neck-mark-of-the-trained-soldier)

###### Consumables

- Changed Potions to "Potion of the Old War with [Serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173), Potion of Prolonged Power without."
- Changed Food to "Follow the stat priority for your character."

##### Talents

###### Tier 3 (level 45)

- Changed to "*[Power Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121817/power-strikes) is close behind **on single target** and can be used **on single target** if you're not comfortable using [Energizing Elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288)."*

###### Tier 4 (level 60)

- Added Dave ([Summon Black Ox Statue](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115315))
- Changed to "*[Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381) will be the default for almost all situations. [Summon Black Ox Statue](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115315) can be used to group enemies up, should a grip not be available. [Ring of Peace](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844) can be used to knockback enemies that need to be kept from a certain location."*

###### Tier 5 (level 75)

- Updated [Dampen Harm](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122278) to "Reduces all damage you take by 20% to 50% for 10 sec, with larger attacks being reduced by more."

###### Tier 6 (level 90)

- Added to RJW: "Applies Mark of the Crane to up to 5 nearby targets."
- Changed Recommendations: "*[Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847) should be used when there are consistently 5 or more targets with [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) or many more targets with [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) to cut down on AOE ramp up time. [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904) is useful for very  high priority burst that lines up with its uptime and cooldown. [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) for all other situations.**"*

Tier 7 (level 100)

- Changed Recommendation to: "*Use [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) except when there is more than 5 consistent targets, until you get [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806/convergence-of-fates&bonus=3514), then use [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173). If you have [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover), then continue with [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) even after [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806/convergence-of-fates&bonus=3514). [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) has its niche with very high targets and [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847). [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) if you want to be lazy."*
