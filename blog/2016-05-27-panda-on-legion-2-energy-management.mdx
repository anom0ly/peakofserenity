#### Energy management

Not completely unrelated to my first point of comparing [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) and [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784). How do we spend energy in legion? At least let's have a look at single target.

Base energy regen is 10 per second, amplified by haste %. All major spells ([RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) , [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656), [SotW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205414)) see their cooldown reduced by haste by the following rule: Actual cooldown = base cooldown \*( 1 / ( 1 + haste%) ). With 25% haste, you'll gain 25% energy regen and reduce cooldowns by 20%, therefore maximum number of casts is amplified by 25% too.

First, I'll note that I'm aware that with lower cooldowns, it becomes harder to stick to them perfectly. It's easier to have [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) kept on cooldown and [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656) kept on cooldown when they have 10 seconds and 25 seconds cooldowns than when, during [bloodlust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust)+Trinket+[berzerk](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=26297/berserking), they could have respectively 5 and 13 seconds cooldown. Let alone during [serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity)... Therefore, with more haste, I'm aware we'll be missing out on some [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) cooldowns by a bit.

Lets add it up:

- 10 energy per second, 600 per minute; that's 12 [jabs](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/jab), so 24 chi generated per minute if no energy goes to waste.
- [Energizing elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) will grant you 4-6 chi depending on how well you manage to use it, not amplified by haste.
- [Ascension](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) will grant you 90 energy per minute, let's round it up to 4 chi generated.
- Since [Energizing elixi](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) doesn't get affected by haste, let's assume the average player gets [Ascension](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) and gets 4 chi + haste value.

At 0% haste, you're sitting on 28 chi per minute. What about spenders?

- [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) can be cast 6 times per minute
- [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656) can be cast 2.4 times per minute
- [SotW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205414) can be cast 1.5 times per minute.

That's a grant total of 22.2 chi used per minute, leaving you with less than 6 chi if you waste few resources. That means with little/no waste, you're casting 6 [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428)s per minute, and 5-6 [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)s per minute if we assume [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) >[BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784).

What about [T19 2-Piece bonus](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=211427/item-monk-t19-windwalker-2p-bonus)? It reduces [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) cooldown by 2 second before haste is applied (if it works like the similar MOP bonus on [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656) cooldown). This means we can do 7.5 [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) per minute, and use 25.2 chi on [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428)/[FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656)/[SotW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205414).

**Here's were I find it awkward:** add in [Combo Breaker](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137384/combo-breaker)'s 8% chance times 12 [jabs](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/jab) and you get your average minute of fight (at 0% haste):\
7.5 [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428), 2.4 [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656), 1.5 [SotW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205414), and 3 [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) (one from [combo breaker](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137384/combo-breaker)). 12 [jabs](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/jab), 4 [chi waves](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) come in to fill this rotation. That's about 30 actions per minute with no haste, with 20% haste and taking [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656)'s cast time into account we're up to 45 seconds of activity, which leaves some room for [bloodlust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) and so on. That's fine.

What bugs me though is the spot [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) has in the rotation. With perfect gameplay, on 20% haste, you'd use [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) 4 times per minute and use [chi waves](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) to ensure you keep [Mastery: Combo strikes](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) up. With some messing up, not casting [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) perfectly on cooldown, you end up using [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) half as much as [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428). That's a new thing for WW's.

First issue is that [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) becomes sort of a "trap" button. You removed [tiger palm combo breaker](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=118864/combo-breaker-tiger-palm) because you don't want people to have a proc they don't want to use because it's weaker than the rest of the kit? Well, that's what [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) has become. You can be sure 99% of windwalkers testing it at the moment, and 99% of those who'll play it on launch, will not be completely aware of this little calculation unless a couple people advertise it a LOT, and will assume [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) is their bread and butter again. Truth is, each time they press that button, chances are they'll be delaying either [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) , [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656) or [SotW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205414), within 15 seconds, losing some damage. How many players testing WW's have I seen go [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)->TP->[BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)->TP to build up some chi for [FOF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656)/other cooldowns? How many have I told "you've just used half the [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)'s you are allowed to use this minute, don't do that unless you're setting up for [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) or something".

TL;DR: if [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) >[BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784), correct rotation uses [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) 4-5 times per minute, depending on haste. People using [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) without consideration will not have enough chi to cast their relevant spells. Also, we're using [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) twice as much as [BOK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) with T19 2PC.
