##### New Stuff: Gear Compendium

Rather than constantly update trinkets and adding new posts for people to find, I've added a [Gear and Trinket Compendium](http://www.walkingthewind.com/gear/gear-trinket-compendium/) to the nav bar, under Gear.  This should serve as a one-stop-shop for all your trinket and gear needs.

##### Sims: Races

I have been meaning to do this for awhile, but other things kept hopping ahead in line. These sims were done using SimulationCraft's Mythic profile (T19M), 1 Boss Patchwerk with an average fight length of 300 seconds.

[![10-22-races](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/10-22-Races.png)](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/10/23/legion-sims-10-22-races-potions-and-gear-compendium/10-22-races/)

As you can see, although Blood Elf and Draenei are at the top, the difference from top to bottom (not counting Pandaren - Fishbrul) is less than 1%. This means that you can literally play whatever you want and see little to no difference. So the obvious choice is Pandaren since they're the only true Monk race.

##### Sims: Potions

The long awaited potion simulations including the new Potion of Prolonged Power are finally in, so you can stop asking me how it compares.

[![10-22-potions](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/10-22-Potions.png)](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/10/23/legion-sims-10-22-races-potions-and-gear-compendium/10-22-potions/)

Basically, Old War is still very very very very(...) slightly ahead (0.2%) in single target, where as Prolonged Power pulls ahead with multiple targets. So if Prolonged Power is cheaper on your server, as it likely will be, then you'll be perfectly fine.

##### Bonus Sims: Potions + Talents

A [few weeks ago](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/10/02/legion-sims-10-1-trinkets-food-potions-and-talents/) I ran simulations testing the current potions and the difference, with them, between [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) and [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). It showed that it was beneficial to, after the opener, use Potion of Old War at a different time from [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) when talented into [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). Since there was a new potion on the block, I thought I'd resim them to show how the new potion changes things, if at all.

[![10-22-potion-talents](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/10-22-Potion-Talents.png)](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/10/23/legion-sims-10-22-races-potions-and-gear-compendium/10-22-potion-talents/)

As expected, Prolonged Power is better than Old War with [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch), this is largely because you can have the potion buff up for both charages of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) and then some. Old War is slightly stronger than Prolonged Power with [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173)  is because of the interaction between Old War and [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) all piling into [Gale Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=195403/gale-burst).
