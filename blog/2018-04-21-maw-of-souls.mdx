*This guide assumes that you have a knowledge of the mechanics of both bosses and trash in the dungeon and will take a more monk focused approach. For more information on the mechanics of all the different mobs and bosses in the dungeon check out the guide over at [Wowhead.](http://www.wowhead.com/maw-of-souls-dungeon-strategy-guide)*

## Talents

The recommended talents for Maw of Souls are:\
-[Chi Wave](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave)\
-[Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust)\
-[Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir)\
-[Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep)\
-[Diffuse Magic](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic)\
-[Hit Combo](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo)\
-[Whirling Dragon Punch.](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch)

The damage talents selected are taken due to being the current best setup in all scenarios, there is no real deviation in talents for aoe/st.

[Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can be used to dispel a debuff in Maw of Souls and as such is the best option.

[Diffuse Magic](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) is the most helpful of the 3 in Maw of Souls due to the fact that the heaviest hitting abilities in the dungeon are magic damage, such as: [Lantern of Darkness](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=192019/lantern-of-darkness), [Brackwater Blast](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=201397/brackwater-blast) and [Fire Blast](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=202365/fire-blast). It can also help with dispelling [Taint of the Sea](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=197264/taint-of-the-sea) on Helya.

## Boss Tips

#### Ymiron, the Fallen King

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269) / [Drinking Horn Cover](http://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips**:\
-You can use [Transcendence](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) to get back to the boss quickly after he casts [Screams of the Dead](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=193364/screams-of-the-dead) and you have to run out.\
-Saving some damage for the [Arise, Fallen](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=193566/arise-fallen) cast can be necessary depending on affixes and level, if left alive for too long they end up doing a lot of damage or on affixes like [Necrotic](http://www.wowhead.com/affix=4/necrotic) and [Explosive](http://www.wowhead.com/affix=13/explosive) they can be a pain when left alive. In these situations saving a stack of [SEF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) for them can be very helpful, and equipping [Drinking Horn Cover](http://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover&spec=269) due to the massive extension you'll get by spamming [SCK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick).

#### Harbaron

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips**:\
-Making sure you have [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) and [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) up and ready for each [Fragment](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=194325/fragment) cast will make it significantly easier to deal with.\
-If your group is having trouble killing Shackled Servitors you can hold onto 1 [SEF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) charge for when it spawns and burst it down very quickly.

#### Helya

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips:\** -Depending on the affixes you can change your [ToD](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death) timing. On [Fortified](http://www.wowhead.com/affix=10/fortified) you should hold it until Helya herself comes out to try and kill her with only 1 or 2 submerged phases. However, on [Tyrannical](http://www.wowhead.com/affix=9/tyrannical) using it on the Destructor Tentacles can be very beneficial, as if you have 2 up at the same time it will likely be a wipe and on high enough keys you'll have your 2nd [ToD](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death) up for Helya herself anyway.\
-Remember that if you get the [Taint of the Sea](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=197264/taint-of-the-sea) debuff you can use [Diffuse Magic](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) to dispel yourself and save your healer some mana.

## Trash Tips

#### Leg Sweep

**Notable packs to be ready to stun:**\
-If you do a double pull at the start before Ymiron it can be difficult to pick up every Fire Blast cast, using [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) or [Arcane Torrent](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=80483/arcane-torrent) can help a lot here.\
-The dogs in the underbelly of the boat can be very deadly to any players that they jump at so killing them in a stun can save a lot of deaths.\
-If you pull multiple packs after Harbaron it is recommended that you assign one player to kicking the [Bone Chilling Scream](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=198405/bone-chilling-scream), if you do that you can still get a lot of the other casts with [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) and [Arcane Torrent](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=80483/arcane-torrent) if you're Blood Elf.

#### Kick

**Important casts to look out for and prioritize kicks on**:\
-[Fire Blast](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=202365/fire-blast)\
-[Soul Siphon](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=194657/soul-siphon)\
-[Bone Chilling Scream](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=198405/bone-chilling-scream)\
-[Whirlpool of Souls](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=199589/whirlpool-of-souls)\
-[Debilitating Shout](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195293/debilitating-shout)\
-[Torrent of Souls](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=199514/torrent-of-souls)

#### Paralysis

In Maw of Souls there are no casts that can only be interrupt be [Paralysis](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and no mobs that can be skipped via its use either, therefore you should just use it to interrupt any casts that go through while your interrupt is on cooldown.

#### Tiger's Lust

If you pull any Seacursed Slavers at the start of the dungeon then [Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can be used to remove [Barbed Spear](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=191960/barbed-spear).
