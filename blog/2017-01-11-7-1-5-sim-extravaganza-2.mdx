So, because of the change to how [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) works, we needed to look again at when is the best time to use it in terms of the cooldowns of other abilities. Since things like the 2pc and [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806/convergence-of-fates&bonus=3514) will effect the timings and how cooldowns line up, I looked at combinations of both and none.

- [Convergence + 2pc](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Delaying-Serenity-CoF-2pc.html)
- [Convergence](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Delaying-Serenity-CoF-No-2pc.html)
- [2pc](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Delaying-Serenity-2pc-No-CoF.html)
- [None](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Delaying-Serenity-No-2pc-CoF.html)

"No Delay" is obviously using Serenity roughly on CD, whereas FoF &lt;15 or >20 is using Serenity if FoF has less than 15s OR more than 20s on its cooldown. "Previous Usage" was how we used Serenity prior to 7.1.5, FoF at &lt;15, SotW at &lt;14, and RSK at &lt;2s

**TLDR: Despite them being in different orders, the common theme is that "No Delay" is always within less than the margin of error from the top. So when using [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), you can delay it a little if something is about to come up, but don't delay it too long. And if you don't have [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806/convergence-of-fates&bonus=3514), the sims say that you almost cannot use [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) incorrectly.**

Notes:

- To assess the power of [Cenedril, Reflector of Hatred](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137019), add 30k DPS to the simulation’s DPS, this is equal to the bonus provided to the cloaks’ damage if used on cooldown.
- SimC is also not using [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080) correctly, nor a second time when using [Hidden Master's Forbidden Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137057), so this legendary is not modeled correctly in the sim.
- Lastly, [Kil'jaeden's Burning Wish](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=144259/kiljaedens-burning-wish) was recently buffed to do 55% more damage, so to compensate for that, add 4.4k DPS per target since its AOE.
