Here's what you need to know for playing a Windwalker in 10.0.7:

## Class Updates

#### Changes

- You can now play as a Lightforged Draenei, Worgen, or Goblin Monk.
- [Inner Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=195243/inner-peace)’s maximum Energy Bonus increased to 30 (was 10).
- [Inner Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=195243/inner-peace) now also increases [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=100780/tiger-palm) damage by 10%.
- [Rising Star](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=388849/rising-star) now increases [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) damage by 3/6% (was 5/10%) and critical strike damage by 6/12%.
- [Faeline Harmony](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=391412/faeline-harmony)’s damage and healing bonus increased to 10% (was 8%).
- New Talent: [Skytouch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=405044/skytouch) – [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=100780/tiger-palm) applies an effect which increases your critical strike chance by 50% for 6 seconds on the target. This effect cannot be applied more than once every 1 minute per target.
  - Developers' note: This has been added as a choice node on [Skyreach](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=392991/skyreach) for players that still want the benefit additional critical strike benefit but do not want to dash to their target.

#### Results

I don't know why you'd play anything but Pandaren, but the option is there.

Windwalkers will do a little more damage in Single Target and you can now keep the crit buff from [Skyreach](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=392991/skyreach) without needing to worry about the charge if you're on a fight where it will cause problems.

No other talent or rotational tweaks come from the other above changes.

## [Onyx Annulet](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=203460/onyx-annulet) & Primordial Stones

This is a new ring added in 10.0.7 that allows you to add up to 3 Primordial Stones to it. You can check out more information on Wowhead's guide: [here](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/guide/forbidden-reach/primordial-stones).

For Windwalkers, the ring is generally very strong in all situations. You’ll pretty much want to pair [Onyx Annulet](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=203460/onyx-annulet) with [Seal of Diurna's Chosen](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=195480/seal-of-diurnas-chosen) (using [Flaring Cowl](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193494/flaring-cowl) to proc that).

**As a general recommendation u****se [Freezing Ice Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=204011/freezing-ice-stone), [Prophetic Twilight Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204029/prophetic-twilight-stone), and [Desirous Blood Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=204027/desirous-blood-stone).**

This is the best simming combination for single target. For dungeons, you may want to replace [Freezing Ice Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=204011/freezing-ice-stone) with either [Pestilent Plague Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204022/pestilent-plague-stone) or [Storm Infused Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204000/storm-infused-stone).

Some notes:

- It’s looking like an, **at most, ~1% overall dps difference between the “best” dungeon setup and just running the single target stones in dungeons**, so up to you if that matters. (And a wider gap if you run dungeon stones in single target, so if you just want one build, do the single target one).
- [Pestilent Plague Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204022/pestilent-plague-stone) is slightly below [Storm Infused Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204000/storm-infused-stone) in some sims and ahead in others, but it procs [Resonant Fists](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=389578/resonant-fists) in a way that sims may not be fully reflecting, so it’s probably the real winner.
- [Storm Infused Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204000/storm-infused-stone) is also known to ninja pull in dungeons sometimes, so watch out.
- [Echoing Thunder Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204001/echoing-thunder-stone) is oversimming by a lot, so don’t trust that one in sims for now.
- If you do want separate setups, you can get a 2nd ring at the vendor for portability.
- The effects of the stones scale based on your ring’s overall item level, not the item level of the individual stone itself, so upgrading [Prophetic Twilight Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204029/prophetic-twilight-stone), for example, does actually increase the overall damage of the ring.
- The families at the vendor for each are: Frost ([Freezing Ice Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=204011/freezing-ice-stone)), Shadow ([Prophetic Twilight Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204029/prophetic-twilight-stone)), Necromantic ([Desirous Blood Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=204027/desirous-blood-stone)), Nature ([Pestilent Plague Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204022/pestilent-plague-stone)), and Nature ([Storm Infused Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=204000/storm-infused-stone)).

## Conclusion

That's it, upgrade your ring and stones as fast and far as you can, keep playing the way you have been.
