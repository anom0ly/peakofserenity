Legion is finally winding down, with many of us finishing up our progression in Antorus and starting to turn our attention toward Battle for Azeroth. Before we do, we have some final additions to the site to finish things out.

### Mythic Plus Guides

Talby has been working hard with other Windwalkers to create a resource for Mythic+. This will continue to grow and be updated by top M+ Windwalkers.

You can find it here: [http://www.peakofserenity.com/windwalker/mythic-plus/](/tww/windwalker/)

### Antorus Guides

Pandanaconda has started uploading the encounter guides that he's been hard at work on. The first 5 are up, and more should be following in the next few days and weeks. Thankfully Antorus is going to last a LONG time, so there will be plenty of time for everyone to use it.

You can find it here: [http://www.peakofserenity.com/windwalker/encounters/antorus/](/tww/windwalker/)

### Battle for Azeroth Prep

As we anxiously await the opening of Alpha/Beta so we can start seeing what the next expansion has in store, we are turning our attention to seeing how we can improve the site. To get feedback from you, the users, I've created a google survey that will help us know what we should focus on, and what we can do without.

You can find it here: [https://goo.gl/forms/EUhR3kCoOY7Ht4rN2](https://goo.gl/forms/EUhR3kCoOY7Ht4rN2)
