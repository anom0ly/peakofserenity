## What covenant should I go?

It says it in the guide but they are all similar. We suggest Kyrian but all covenants are viable at all levels of content. Yes, that means mythic raiding too!

## I'm playing RM but I keep having to hit healing buttons.

[Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909)’s direct healing isn’t going to keep people up. The opportunity it gives to have more Renewing Mists up at one time allows you to more easily handle AoE damage through Vivify. TLDR: the healing part of RM = meh; the HoT extension = easier healing later 😎

## Which lego to go for and which slot?

Tear of Morning in the head slot. As a general rule of thumb you will normally want to craft a legendary in a high stat slot. These are the rough breakdown in stats per slot:

Chest, legs and head are all 1.00

Shoulders, boots, belts and gloves are all 0.75

Bracers and cloak are 0.56

## What should my DPS rotation be?

**Standard Rotation**

[Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) >  [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780) to 3 stacks of Teachings of the Monastery >  [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

**Rotation with  [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909)**

[Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) >  [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) >  [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780)

**Spirit of the Crane Rotation (DPS or Mana Rotation)**

Max DPS (If you also have  [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909), use the above  [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909) rotation)\
 [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) >  [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780) to 3 stacks of Teachings of the Monastery >  [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

**Or**

Max Mana

[Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780) to 3 stacks of Teachings of the Monastery >  [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

**Chi-ji Rotation (While active)**\
 [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) >  [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780) >  [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

If [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) is on cooldown replace it with a [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780).
