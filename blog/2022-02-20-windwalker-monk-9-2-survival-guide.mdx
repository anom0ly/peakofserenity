## Disclaimer

**Everything on this page is subject to change at any point due to theorycrafting, tuning, or us simply missing something or discovering something new that we didn't know before. React to this information on your own volition.**

**If there is anything that you feel was missed in this guide, that is because it is best done through the tools on [Raidbots](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot) once 9.2 comes out.**

## Changes

- [Bonedust Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325216/bonedust-brew) can no longer crit, but had its damage increased from a 35% bonus to a 40% bonus. This results in a slight nerf to Necrolord damage.

## What this means

#### Covenants

- **Raiding:**
  - Kyrian for single target
  - Either Kyrian or Necrolord for small target cleave
  - Necrolord for frequent or large target cleave.
- **Mythic+**: Necrolord

#### Legendaries

###### Single Legendaries

- Night Fae will use [Faeline Harmony](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=356705/faeline-harmony) in Belt for all situations.
- Venthyr can use [Sinister Teachings](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=356818/sinister-teachings) in Wrist/Finger.
- Necrolord will use [Invoker's Delight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) for everything. In Cloak if you need Helm for tier bonus, on Helm if not.
- Kyrian will use [Invoker's Delight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) for everything. In Cloak if you need Helm for tier bonus, on Helm if not.

###### Double Legendaries

- [Memory of Unity](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=190590/memory-of-unity) in Belt and [Invoker's Delight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) for everything. In Cloak if you need Helm for tier bonus, on Helm if not.

#### Tier Set

###### Gear

- [Crown of the Grand Upwelling](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=188910/crown-of-the-grand-upwelling) **(Helm - [Anduin Wrynn](https://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=181954/anduin-wrynn))**
- [Tassets of the Grand Upwelling](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=188914/tassets-of-the-grand-upwelling) **(Shoulders - [Lords of Dread](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=26533))**
- [Cuirass of the Grand Upwelling](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=188912/cuirass-of-the-grand-upwelling) **(Chest - [Rygelon](https://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=182777/rygelon))**
- [Grips of the Grand Upwelling](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=188916/grips-of-the-grand-upwelling) **(Gloves - [Lihuvim](https://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=182169/lihuvim))**
- [Legguards of the Grand Upwelling](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=188911/legguards-of-the-grand-upwelling) **(Legs - [Halondrus](https://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=184915/halondrus))**

**Ideal** **Pieces**: Shoulders, Chest, Gloves, and Legs with [Invoker's Delight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) in the Helm slot.

###### Bonuses

- **2-Set** - [Fists of Primordium](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=364418/fists-of-primordium) - Increases [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) damage by 40%.
- **4-Set** - [Primordial Potential](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=363734/primordial-potential) - After 10 offensive abilities, your next 3 offensive abilities deal an additional 22% damage.

Neither cause any change in how you play. You can delay [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch), and [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) to try and fit them into the window of 3 abilities from [Primordial Potential](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=363734/primordial-potential), but can potentially lose damage by doing so too frequently. A good rule of thumb is if you have to delay by more than 1/5 of the abilities CD, it won't be worth doing so.

#### Everything Else

There's no other changes to any aspect of Windwalkers other than what is above. Damage should be fine, low in pure single target but high in AOE.
