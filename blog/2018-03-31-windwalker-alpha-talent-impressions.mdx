I finally was able to get into the Alpha for a few hours to run some tests and see how Windwalker feels with the changes. I focused primarily on the individual talents and changes, rather than the overall feel of the spec with a variety of builds as some important mechanics aren't currently working correctly, making any testing irrelevant. I wasn't concerned with looking at the damage of any of the abilities, since those are the things that will change much later in the cycle.

This did end up being more comprehensive than I originally anticipated, so please consider supporting the site and the work that I/we do by clicking the buttons on the right. --->

If you'd like to watch my stream from last night, you can find it on my Twitch here: [https://www.twitch.tv/videos/244837471](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/244837471)

### Talents

##### Tier 1 (lv15)

- [Eye of the Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196607/eye-of-the-tiger) - No change
- [Chi Wave](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) - No change
- [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) - Extra chi per target hit is AWESOME. It fits well into the single target rotation as well as provides a ton of resources for AOE.

This tier doesn't feel much different, but the extra chi from [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) is noticeable. Currently the extra chi puts [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) at the top of this tier for all situations.

##### Tier 2 (lv30)

- [Celerity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115173/celerity) - Same as current, when combined with [Chi Torpedo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115008/chi-torpedo) its 60% movement speed for a looooong time if used correctly, nearly 40 seconds. I can't think of a time this will be useful other than foot races and battlegrounds, but its a lot of speed for a long time.
- [Healing Elixirs](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122280/healing-elixir) - Definitely an odd position, but not a talent that I took often, so I don't mind being able to take it with [Diffuse Magic](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic). Windwalker has a ton of movement anyway, so Tiger's Lust isn't as missed, even though I love it.
- [Tiger's Lust](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) - Same as current

I didn't mind having a little bit of a mix of movement and survivability on this tier. It allows you to take either two movement talents, or two survival talents, or a mix. This feels like you'd have more flexibility in what you'd use depending on the situation.

##### Tier 3 (lv45)

- [Ascension](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) - No change, still a garbage talent. The energy cap and regen increase is not noticed, nor is the 6th chi slot.
- [Power Strikes](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=121817/power-strikes) - No functional change, just more damage. However, with the nerf to [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir), [Power Strikes](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=121817/power-strikes)'s 4 chi per minute max is more reasonable compared to [EE](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir)'s 6 chi max, if used perfectly, which is more typically a little less than 6. Having one less thing to worry about, for a little loss in single target feels pretty nice. In AOE when you can more easily dump chi, [EE](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) will still reign supreme.
- [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) - Feels good, easy to use, much less thought required, much less waste. You can, and often should, use [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) right after [EE](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) as long as you won't cap your chi, in order to waste less energy.

Of all the tiers, this is the one I was most excited about changing. [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) being down to 2 Chi, while technically a HUGE nerf, balances this talent tier soooooooooo much better. The fact that [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) maxes out at 6 chi, compared to [Power Strikes](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=121817/power-strikes)'s 4 is still a notable difference, but no where near as big as it is currently where the difference is more like 11 vs 4 in perfect use. It will take some getting used to [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) being so easily used, but overall a fantastic change all around.

##### Tier 4 (lv60)

- [Tornado Kicks](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261683/tornado-kicks) - No functional effect
- [Combo Breaker](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=262107/combo-breaker) - This appeared to be bugged as the effect was happening regardless of talent. It feels really good, as requiring a [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) to proc means that you automatically have the 2 chi needed. It is a bummer when you get the proc right as [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) is coming off CD, but getting it the GCD after you just used [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) into [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) feels amazing.
- [Fist of the White Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947/fist-of-the-white-tiger) - Was not providing the extra chi, will revisit later

This tier is, more or less, all new; so I was interested in checking out. Unfortunately [Fist of the White Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947/fist-of-the-white-tiger) wasn't working correctly, not providing the Chi, and [Combo Breaker](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=262107/combo-breaker) appeared to be on at all times, whether you had the talent or not. However, the one thing I can comment on with this tier is that [Combo Breaker](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=262107/combo-breaker) feels exactly how that proc should be; its impactful but not intrusive.

##### Tier 5 (lv75)

- [Inner Strength](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261767/inner-strength) - Doesn't do anything functional, stacks to 5, definitely something that is nice to have, but in my opinion, requires too much attention to use fully. Nice to have if you're not overthinking it and you don't need the other talents in the row. Cannot be maintained indefinitely currently. Not a worthy replacement for [Dampen Harm](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122278/dampen-harm), in its current form, in my opinion.
- [Chi Torpedo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115008/chi-torpedo) - same as [Celerity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115173/celerity)
- [Diffuse Magic](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) - Doesn't feel terrible having it in the row with [Chi Torpedo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115008/chi-torpedo) as I'd use [Diffuse Magic](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) most of the time, and never felt the need to use Chi Torpedo

Similar to my impression of Tier 2, I don't mind having a mix of survival and movement on the same tier. [Inner Strength](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261767/inner-strength) does feel like a half idea right now, as it neither provides enough damage reduction in the best situation to make it worth thinking about and playing around, nor does it have a high enough uptime to make a noticeable difference with its lower damage reduction. One of those two things need changing, either more uptime, or more damage reduction.

##### Tier 6 (lv90)

- [Hit Combo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo) - Same feel, just less damage, less punishing for mistakes
- [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) - Holy forking shizz... this talent feels awful. Potentially the worst gameplay I've ever played in WoW. The fact that it drains some energy feels fine, there's no problem with that. The problem, and it sticks out like a thorn, is that if you are waiting for energy to regen so you can use [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm), using [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) as soon as you have enough energy will, depending on how much you're spamming the button, mean that you don't have that much energy for [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) for that fraction of a second, and [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) will turn off, meaning that you'll have to wait till your next free GCD to turn it back on. Basically, to not automatically drop [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) the instant that you use [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm), you need to have at least 54 energy when you hit [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm). This means that you have to wait an additional 0.4s past hitting 50 energy, if you have no haste. This is only an issue if you're spamming [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) waiting to have enough energy to use it, which is basically hardwired into any person who's ever played an energy class. This can be played around, simply by waiting a smidge, and planning your resources so that you are always above 4 energy, but its enough of a change to feel jarring. Talents like [Ascension](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension), [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity), and others will help reduce the frequency of this happening, but not fix the fact that if you are spamming [Tiger Palm](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) waiting for energy, 99% of the time you will drop [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) and have to put it back up when you can, losing damage. It is visually very cool though, but I am genuinely disappointed with how it feels.
- [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) - Same feel, just a shorter duration and cooldown so less overall uptime. Right now, based on my 8.0 spreadsheet, he is WAY overtuned, unless I have put something wrong into the sheet, which is definitely plausible. Does not look like he feeds into the new [Touch of Death/Gale Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death), so the fact that they line up with the same cooldown length isn't super important beyond the fact that you'd want to use [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) when you have stat buffs, which you'd also want to do for [Touch of Death](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death), so that you get more benefit from the 10% damage addition.

I hope my disdain for [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) was adequately conveyed through that paragraph. If not, there's definitely some choice words somewhere on the [VOD from last night's stream](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/244837471). This tier is mostly disappointing as on [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) provides any meaningful change to how you play, and it plays like hot garbage. Something more needs to be done about this tier, but the potential is there.

##### Tier 7 (lv100)

- [Wind Strikes](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=262112/wind-strikes) - Pretty passive, nothing happens. It does have some interesting play in a situation where you're dropping [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) from the rotation, as it adds AOE damage to your TP, where [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) would still require you to [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) to use. Its probably not a meaningful interaction, but one that is theoretically possible.
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) - Still my favorite talent, still feels just the same. The fact that [BoK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) reduces the CD of [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [FoF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) but not [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is really not noticeable.
- [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) - No legendary bracers is groooooossss. Other than that, nothing special. It does not look like it was currently working correctly as the cooldown reduction didn't seem to be happening. It does feel weird without [Strike of the Windlord](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) to use. Honestly the only time I missed [Strike](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) when testing.

There's not much to be said about this tier as it really isn't much different from live, which is mostly OK.

### New Changes

- No [Strike of the Windlord](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) - I didn't notice it was gone except for during [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity). Because of the CD reduction mechanic of [BoK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), there is enough to press for the resources you have without [Strike of the Windlord](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord).
- [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) reducing cooldown of [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [FoF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) - Feeels goooooood. Not major enough to be game changing, but enough that its noticeable.
- [SCK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) costs 2 Chi - Feels more natural, just harder to "dump" chi. It feels much better when you drop [RSK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [BoK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) from the rotation. Also feels very nice with [Power Strikes](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=121817/power-strikes) as it helps you have enough Chi for [FoF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) without having to think about it much. You just alternate [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) and [SCK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) and [FoF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) when its up. Combined with [EE](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir), [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst), [Fist of the White Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947/fist-of-the-white-tiger), and other things, you'll have a ton of resources for using [SCK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) often. Although I wasn't overly miffed with 3 chi [SCK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick), this just feels right.
- Offensive abilities being on the GCD - Yeah, this doesn't feel great. It will take some getting used to and planning around. [Touch of Karma](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) remains off the GCD since its "technically" a defensive.
- [Ring of Peace](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) and [Leg Sweep](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) being baseline is quite nice. [RoP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) got its time in the sun during Antorus, so its nice to be able to play around with it in the future without it taking up a talent slot.

### Builds

This makes any testing for how the builds "feel" impossible to truly do right now. ### What can still be done

Although I really enjoyed my time on the Alpha, there are still some things that can be changed to improve the spec. I still maintain that how diverse our stat interactions are with our abilities will lead us into trouble later on; right now Vers affects 100% of our damage, Crit around 90%, Mastery 80%, and Haste 70%. This leads to inevitable inequity between the specs, and tends to just be a hassle.

[Ascension](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) needs buffing or its still terrible. It needs to provide at least 4 chi per minute, which would be another 100 energy per minute or 1.667 energy per second. At the current 10% increase to energy regen, (assuming my math is correct, which is no guarantee) you'd have to have like 15 energy per second regen before taking [Ascension](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) into account, which requires 50% haste, an unreachable number outside of huge buffs. If its un-nerfed to 20% then that requirement falls to 38%, at buffed to 30% more energy regen you'd need 28% haste, which is a MUCH more reasonable number, but still pretty high for how Haste will likely remain lower in our priority. This just shows that [Ascension](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) has really no chance.

[Chi Wave](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) needs buffing to offset [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) giving chi if it wants a chance to compete in that tier. There could also be a change to [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) so that it only provides 1 Chi per target hit if it hits more than 1 target. This would insure that [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) is the go-to pick for anything with multiple targets, and [Chi Wave](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) remains preferable for single target.

[RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) needs to be seriously looked at, along with the other two talents in that row.

- [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) idea 1 - Cost 5 energy to start and maintain, every target hit reduces energy cost by 1 for 5 seconds.
- [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) idea 2 - Cost X energy initially, energy/chi spent increases duration, either enough to make it permanently maintainable as long as you're still in combat, or slightly less so it requires more thought to line it up with mechanics and energy regen. (courtesy of OneTrueBalou on in Twitch chat)

The first option would allow that the more targets you have, and thus the more you'd want to use [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind), the less of an impact having less energy will have. If the cost is 5 energy, and you have 10 energy per second worth of regen, that means you have to delay [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) by 0.5s after its available, so you don't drop [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind). If that cost goes down to 2 energy, because it hits 3 targets, then the delay goes down to 0.2s. At 5 targets hit, it would cost 0 and thus you wouldn't have to delay [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) at all.

The second option means that you'd be able to use it and keep it going with good play, promoting stacking and pooling of resources to get the most out of it. It could be tuned so that you can keep it up 100% of the time but do slightly less damage, or not have 100% uptime, requiring more thought, but with more damage as a bonus for using it correctly. This is similar to how I feel that [Inner Strength](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261767/inner-strength) should work.

Lastly my idea to fix the overall tier with just numbers balancing - [Hit Combo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo) back to 12%, [RJW](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) buffed enough to merit the extra thought required to not spam [TP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) without it costing you further damage, [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) stays strong.

### Overall Impressions

**Windwalker is still fun.** Anyone who's been around for a while may know my opinions and preference for how Windwalker should play. I prefer slow, paced, gameplay. Minimal spamming, more time for planning. This was what I enjoyed most about Windwalker at any time during the past 6 years. With that in mind, the current feel of Windwalker on Alpha is just about my dream situation. The choices you need to make are meaningful, but not complicated. Each ability and button press feels important. There's both room for mistakes and room to really squeeze out a bunch more DPS. It is simpler than currently, and more user-friendly, without losing the feel or the complexity that makes many people love Windwalker right now.

Interesting note, with the change to our talent order, the left column is all passive and the right column is all active abilities. I'm assuming it was intentional, so nicely done Blizzard.
