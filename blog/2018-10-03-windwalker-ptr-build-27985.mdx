There was a new build on the PTR today, which added a few things that likely affect Windwalker.

**Spells (1)**

- **[Crackling Jade Lightning](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning)**   Channel Jade lightning, causing (~~27.977%~~22.4% of Attack power) Nature damage over 4 sec to the target and sometimes knocking back melee attackers.

**Related Azerite Power Spells (4)**

- NEW **Dance of Chi-Ji**   Spending Chi has a chance to make your next Spinning Crane Kick free and deal 1415 additional damage.
- NEW **Dance of Chi-Ji**   Spending Chi has a chance to make your next Spinning Crane Kick free and deal 1415 additional damage.
- NEW **Dance of Chi-Ji**   Spending Chi has a chance to make your next Spinning Crane Kick free and deal 1415 additional damage.
- NEW **Fury of Xuen**   Your Combo Strikes grant you the Fury of Xuen, giving your next Fists of Fury a 3% chance to grant 130 Haste and invoke Xuen, The White Tiger for 8 sec. Stacks up to 33 times.

The nerf to [Crackling Jade Lightning](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning) is pretty much meaningless, as its not used for anything meaningful.

Dance of Chi-Ji appears to have a rough proc rate of 2 per minute. Likely, if you have it, you'll want to make sure to use [Spinning Crane Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546) before you could spend Chi again on another ability. This should be a nice boost in AOE; its unclear if the damage will be to each target, although it likely is, and reasonable in single target depending on how its balanced. More importantly, this seems to be a step toward making [Spinning Crane Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546)  a little bit more used and meaningful, which is one of the complaints many have had since the tuning changed going into this expansion. Having more of this trait will likely just increase the damage added to [Spinning Crane Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546) and not the proc chance, as thats how other Azerite Traits stack.

Fury of Xuen is one that we'll definitely need more information on. The 33 stack cap is mostly meaningless, although in situations where you're holding [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) in anticipation of more targets to hit, the stacking chance will be nice, but you will almost certainly not go out of your way to get more stacks to increase the chance. Having more of this trait will certainly stack only the haste amount, and not the % chance to proc or the duration of [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger). In theory, this could mean that you have two copies of [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) up at the same time, which sounds pretty. Since haste isn't as useful of a stat as the others, that part of this trait may not be very helpful, so it is likely going to be in a situation of wanting to get 1, if any, but not stack them.
