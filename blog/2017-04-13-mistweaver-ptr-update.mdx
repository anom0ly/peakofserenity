These changes have just gone live on the PTR.

- [Essence Font](http://www.wowdb.com/spells/191837) now has a 12 second cooldown and a duration of 8 seconds (was 6 seconds).
- [Essence Font](http://www.wowdb.com/spells/191837) cost reduced by 10%, and direct healing increased 23%.
- [Vivify](http://www.wowdb.com/spells/116670) healing increased by 9%.

Over the coming days, we will be busy testing and theorycrafting to see what this means for MWs in patch 7.2.5. Buckle up, it's going to be an interesting ride!
