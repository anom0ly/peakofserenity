New to the game and want to be a Mistweaver? Told to heal for a tricky boss and you're offspec healing? This is the quick guide for you! Longer version can be found [here](/tww/mistweaver/).

### Talents

Note: This is a default build, other talents could be better depending on the fight

- Level 15 - [Chi Burst](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst)
- Level 45 - [Mist Wrap](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap)
- Level 60 - [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep)
- Level 75 - [Healing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122281/healing-elixir)
- Level 90 - [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=198664/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane)
- Level 100 - [Mana Tea](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea)

#### Pre Pull

- [Renewing Mist](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) at 8 seconds remaining on timer
- [Potion of Prolonged Power](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=229206/potion-of-prolonged-power) at 1-2 seconds remaining on timer
- [Thunder Focus Tea](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea) [Renewing Mist](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on pull

#### During the Fight

- [Renewing Mist](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on cooldown
- [Thunder Focus Tea](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea) always used on [Vivify](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) or [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist)
- [Essence Font](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) when 6+ targets are injured (WeakAura can be found [here](https://www.weakauras.online/en/a/65b3ZJD9JV), made by Raysere)
- [Chi Burst](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) on cooldown, unless it can be better used on an ability in the encounter
- [Vivify](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) with any [Uplifting Trance](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=197206/uplifting-trance) buffs.
- [Mana Tea](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) during heavy burst
  - Channel [Essence Font](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) back-to-back while Mana Tea is active
- [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=198664/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) on cooldown when needed to help smooth out healing needed
- [Enveloping Mist](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) on tanks if it won't overheal
- [Sheilun's Gift](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=205406/sheiluns-gift) can be used in three ways:
  - 1-4 stacks as a free single target heal
  - 6+ stacks for bursting up after a powerful ability
  - 12 stacks for covering a mechanical failure

Credit to Si and Arcanedew for the first version of this list, any questions can be asked in the Peak of Serenity discord server.

#### Noteworthy Gear

- [Drape of Shame](http://www.wowhead.com/item=142170/drape-of-shame) - The 10% more healing on crit makes this cloak incredible for the Crit heavy gear Mistweaver is focusing on in raids. Beats out any other cloak in the game right now, except for a better Drape.
- [Infernal Alchemist Stone](http://www.wowhead.com/item=127842/infernal-alchemist-stone&bonus=689:1522) - Great trinket that we're able to level up throughout the expansion. Excellent for managing mana.
- [Gnawed Thumb Ring](https://www.wowhead.com/item=134526/gnawed-thumb-ring&bonus=1826) - At a high item level, this is a very powerful effect to add to our kit, with excellent stats for us.
