The purpose of this article was to try to discover how much of a buff would [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) need to be viable or even strong in 8.3

**Disclaimer:** Because the Serenity APL was doing the old [Swift Roundhouse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=278710/swift-roundhouse) rotation of 2x [BoK](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [RSK](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) I had to swap to [Xuen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) for the purpose of these sims.

Firstly I would like to discuss why [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is fundamentally flawed for AoE DPS this expansion. In Legion [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) had the potential to be strong in AoE because while it lacked the ease to apply Mark of the Crane to targets once it was able to apply them it had the strength of being able to use [Spinning Crane Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546) every other global for 8 seconds while regenerating energy and not wasting Chi and this was incredibly powerful when you also consider that [Spinning Crane Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546) used to cost 3 Chi instead of 2. With Battle for Azeroth, Mark of the Crane had a cap of 5 put on it and it was reduced to 10% per target and [Spinning Crane Kick’](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546)s Chi cost was reduced to 2. With all of these changes and the Azerite traits we currently use for AoE It allows you to play [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) like you would with [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) which means using [SCK](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546) every other global. Because of all these factors [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) doesn’t provide you with almost any AoE DPS increase besides the 20% damage. One of the small benefits of [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) in AoE is you aren’t required to press [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) which is usually a DPS loss in AoE.

**Conclusion:** Barring APL problems with [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), it seems like it would **need to be buffed to 50%-60% damage increase for it to be at the worst net neutral to [WDP](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) on Single Target**. But you will notice that once you start to introduce adds into the fight and we do a Hectic Add Cleave Sim with Serenity at 50%: [https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/report/2e6Zn9GrzzExs1D5AJRNPk](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/report/2e6Zn9GrzzExs1D5AJRNPk) [WDP](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) is just so far ahead of [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) that it seems like no matter what you do it will not be competitive in AoE situations. I would lean towards buffing to [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) to 60% damage increase because this will make it the talent of choice on Single Target ([https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/report/hrp2xBy73kYXtxLfqmqDLA](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/report/hrp2xBy73kYXtxLfqmqDLA)) being 2.9% ahead while [WDP](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) being the talent of choice for Cleave/AoE Situations.

Now this 50%-60% may seem eye opening but there are a lot of forces working against [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) being good this expansion. One that is particularly relevant this tier is the [SEF](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) + ToD 30% Gale Burst interaction for abilities/trinkets that are not copied by the clones. Additionally [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) lacks the flexibility that [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) has with 2 charges. Historically one of the things that made [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) strong was the resource generation it provided you but with the Azerite Traits that Windwalker uses currently you are flooded with resources even while playing [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) which is usually a playstyle that is slower because it doesn’t have enough resources. The result is that this resource generation provided by [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is usually wasted.

Overall I would say while [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) could benefit from a rework of its mechanics it is unrealistic to expect this of Blizzard this expansion and I for one would settle for a tuning buff to it make to usable in Single Target which would be a breath of fresh air in this otherwise [SEF](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) filled expansion.
