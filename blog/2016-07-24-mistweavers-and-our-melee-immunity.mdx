It's been no secret that Mistweavers are not classified as a typical ranged healer, we're instead classified as a melee-healer. What this means is that we're only targeted by spells that only affect melee, or any of the spells that specifically target healers and no one else, like Tichondrius's [Burning Soul](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=216040/burning-soul).

Below is a list of all known abilities that do not target Mistweavers (and now Holy Paladins). I will be updating this as the expansion goes on.

**Emerald Nightmare**

- Cenarius - [Nightmare Brambles](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=210290/nightmare-brambles) will never spawn under our feet, but a nearby ranged player may unintentionally kite them over you.
- Xavius - [Lurking Eruption](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=205741/lurking-eruption) (the small adds) will never explode underneath and then fixate on us.

**\** **Trial of Valor**

- Odyn - Hymdall's [Dancing Blade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228003/dancing-blade) will never center itself under us.
- Helya - We can receive the [Orb of Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=227903/orb-of-corruption) designated for melee players, meaning two healers can be targeted at once.

**The Nighthold**
(Updates in Progress)

- Trilliax - [Sterilize](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=208506/sterilize) will not target us.
- Spellblade Aluriel - [Mark of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=212587/mark-of-frost) will never center on us, but can jump to us if we're too close to others soaking.
- Krosus - [Orb of Destruction](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=205348/orb-of-destruction) will not target us.
- High Botanist Tel'arn - [Parasitic Fetter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218304/parasitic-fetter) will not target us, but the Lasher's fixate will. [Call of Night](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218809/call-of-night) will not target Mistweavers.
- Tichondrius - [Brand of Argus](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=212794/brand-of-argus) will never center on us.
- Gul'dan - [Eye of Gul'dan](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=209270/eye-of-guldan) (and [Empowered Eye of Gul'dan](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=211152/empowered-eye-of-guldan)) will not channel onto Mistweavers.
