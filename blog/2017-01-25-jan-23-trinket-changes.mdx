Blizzard has changed several trinkets recently:

- [Arcanogolem Digit](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140794): Damage increased by 30%.
- [Draught of Souls](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140808): The Use effect of this trinket now respects the global cooldown.
- [Entwined Elemental Foci](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140796): The amount of Critical Strike, Mastery, and Haste granted by this item's proc has been increased by 5%.
- [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806): with  [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639): -62% proc rate
- [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806): with  [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173): -75% proc rate

The [Draught of Souls](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140808) change is a minor inconvenience, and effects other specs more than Windwalker. [Arcanogolem Digit](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140794) and [Entwined Elemental Foci](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140796) buffs are decent.

The [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806) is the biggest change as it bumps the procs per minute (PPM) from 6.3 to 2.4 with [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639)  and 1.6 with [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173).

---

I ran a bunch of sims to show the change and how the trinkets compare to the most popular and powerful non-Nighthold trinkets, [Bloodthirsty Instinct](https://www.wowhead.com/item=139329/bloodthirsty-instinct&bonus=1807) and [Ethereal Urn](https://www.wowhead.com/item=142166/ethereal-urn), all ran at 910 item level.

- [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) - [Pre Nerf](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Pre-Nerf-Serenity.html) | [Post Nerf](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nerfed-Serenity.html)
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) and [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) - [Pre Nerf](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Pre-Nerf-WDP-and-DHC.html) | [Post Nerf](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nerfed-WDP-DHC.html)
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) w/o [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) - [Pre Nerf](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Pre-Nerf-WDP.html) | [Post Nerf](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nerfed-WDP.html)

Time to analyze.

Looking at the loss from one top combo to the new top combo:

- [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) - 6.3% less
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) and [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) - 4.3% less
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) w/o [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) - 1.3% less

Now looking at how the previous top combo looks after the changes:

- [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) - 6.4%
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) and [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) - 5% less
- [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) w/o [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) - 2.4% less

It makes sense that [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) would be hit harder since it has higher uptime, so a decrease in uptime is a bigger deal with it than without. Obviously losing that much damage with [Drinking Horn Cover](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) really sucks. Blizzard stated their goal was to decrease the interaction between [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) and [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806), so they definitely succeeded in their mission.

I ran sims looking at the Nighthold trinkets alone:

- [Serenity](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nerfed-Serenity-solo-trinkets.html)
- [WDP w/o DHC](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nerfed-WDP-DHC-solo-trinkets.html)
- [WDP w/ DHC](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nerfed-WDP-solo-trinkets.html)

The trinkets are still all pretty close now, but obviously [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806) fell down pretty hard. If Blizzard wanted to homogenize the trinket's power, then it worked. Bleh. [Convergence of Fates](https://www.wowhead.com/item=140806) is still BiS for Serenity. Which means.....
