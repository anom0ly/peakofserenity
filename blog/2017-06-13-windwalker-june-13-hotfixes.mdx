Windwalker saw a smidge of a buff today. [[SOURCE](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/20863656)]

**Monk**

- **[Windwalker](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=226728)**

  - [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784) damage increased by 8%.
  - [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) damage increased by 8%.
  - [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780) damage increased by 8%.

In total this increases our single target damage by 3-3.5%. This isn't all the buffs we need to be where we'd like to be, but it is a step in the right direction. These tweaks do not change the relic priority or rotational priorities at all. For information about the other changes that came with 7.2.5, see the [Compiled Changes](/2017/06/04/windwalkers-in-7-2-5-compiled-changes/) post.

This does also increase the power of our t19 and t20 2pc bonuses and hurt the t20 bonus relative to the other bonuses, so there will probably have to be something done about that.

Blizzard has said that this is one one part of the hotfixes and they anticipate more as the week goes on.

---

Update: Blizzard has also swapped around and buffed our tier bonuses. This is going to take the sims being updated to know how good/bad this is.

[https://us.battle.net/forums/en/wow/topic/20755777475](https://us.battle.net/forums/en/wow/topic/20755777475)

**Monk - Windwalker**

- **2pc:** Swapped with 4pc and is now: Rising Sun critical strikes reduce the cooldown of Fists of Fury by 3 sec (up from 2 sec).
- **4pc:** Swapped with 2pc and is now: "When Fists of Fury ends, the critical strike chance of Rising Sun Kick is increased by 65% (up from 50%) for 5 sec."
