**Some of the gear information in this post is outdated, however the tactics on how to handle the scenario remain valid. With gear ilvls are they are post 7.3.2, your gear is less of an issue.**

Before beginning, it's a good idea to get above 900 equipped iLvL, as the high intellect and health will make the scenario smoother to handle, and I recommend having [Whispers of Shaohao](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238130) in your artifact to help healing in stages One, Five, Six (if needed), and Seven.

#### Trinkets

For this Artifact Challenge, you'll want throughput trinkets, with the readily available ones being [Etraeus' Celestial Map](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=140803?bonus=3516) and [Brinewater Slime in a Bottle](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=142507?bonus=3507). In Stage One, Six and Seven, a survival trinket, such as [Perfectly Preserved Cake](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=140793?bonus=3444) or even [Royal Dagger Haft](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=140791?bonus=3444) would be helpful, especially if you do not have [Prydaz, Xavaric's Magnum Opus](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=132444). Do note that the absorb from [Perfectly Preserved Cake](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=140793?bonus=3444) will not apply itself to any friendly NPCs within the scenario.

#### Legendaries

- [Velen's Future Sight](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=144258) - The on use healing bonus is magnificent on Stages One, Five, Six, and Seven, as well as the passive increase to stats a 940 trinket affords.
- [Prydaz, Xavaric's Magnum Opus](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=132444) - The absorb it provides is very helpful, allowing you to spend more time healing the NPCs instead of yourself.
- [Ei'thas, Lunar Glides of Eramas](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=137028) - Turning  [Vivify](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116670)s with  [Uplifting Trance](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=197206) into a big group-wide heal will help on mana as well as random damage.
- [Sephuz's Secret](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=132452) - Due to the high uptime you can have on its effect, this legendary is *magnificent* in Stage One for both reacting to damage and kiting the [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119474) when they fixate, due to the movement speed increase.

#### Consumables

- [Drums of Fury](http://ptr.wowhead.com/item=120257)
- Flask of the Whispered Pact
- The Hungry Magister or  Lavish Suramar Feast
- Potion of Prolonged Power
- Defiled Augment Rune
- Some drink for mana return.

#### Talents

Below is the recommended talent build for the Artifact Challenge.

![](/img/uploads/2017/04/talents-300x297.png)

- [Chi Burst](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=123986) is great for general healing, as well as making one part of Stage Three much easier.
- [Tiger's Lust](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116841) is very useful in Stage One for kiting, giving more control of movement speed.
- [Mist Wrap](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=197900) for increasing  [Enveloping Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=124682) healing and mobile  [Soothing Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=193884) for more uptime on  [Shroud of Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=199365).
- [Leg Sweep](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=119381) is required for Stage One and resetting [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491)'s stacks of  [Arcane Blitz](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235833).
- [Dampen Harm](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=122278) for a general defensive cooldown.
- [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=198664) for increased healing during key parts of the Artifact Challenge.
- [Focused Thunder](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=197895) for more  [Thunder Focus Tea](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116680) augmented spells at key parts of the Artifact Challenge.

## Artifact Challenge Strategy

### Stage One: The Landing

In this stage, you'll be grouped with [Commander Jarod Shadowsong](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118447), [Callie Carrington](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118451), and [Granny Marl](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118448), who will be added to any raid frames available for you to track healing on. Enemies will come to prevent your entering Black Rook Hold, coming in waves.

- Wave 1: One [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492)

  - [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) will cast  [Deadeye Shot](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235837) randomly at your allies. After teleporting using  [Phase Shift](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=236688), they will begin to cast  [Mana Sting](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235992) for 5 seconds, during which you'll want to use  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) to interrupt the cast. If  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) is not available, hide behind one of your allies to soak the hit. They will take a heavy DoT for a few seconds. If you take the hit from  [Mana Sting](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235992), it's pretty much guaranteed a wipe, as you'll run out of mana.

    - One thing to note in this scenario is that  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) works differently. It will only incapacitate for 5 seconds, and less on subsequent uses, during which the enemy is immune to damage. After three uses of  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078), the enemy is immune for a short time to incapacitates.
- Wave 2: One [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) + One [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491)

  - Keep using  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) on [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492)'s  [Mana Sting](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235992), and use  [Leg Sweep](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=119381) when the [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) is around half health, in order to clear its stacks of  [Arcane Blitz](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235833). **Never** use  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) on the [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491), as your allies will switch targets, leaving the [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) up too long, eventually wiping the group.
- Wave 3: One [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) + One [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489)

  - Try to  [Leg Sweep](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=119381) the two of them while the [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489) is channeling  [Knife Dance](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235823). After  [Knife Dance](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235823) is done channeling, or the stun wears off, the [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489) will fixate you with  [Frenzied Assault](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=236720). When you’re fixated by the [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489), use  [Tiger's Lust](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116841) and kite the [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489) over your  [Transcendence](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=101643) spirit for  [Spirit Tether](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=199384) to slow him further.
- Wave Four: One [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) + Two [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489)

  - Same strategy as last wave, except it’s imperative you have  [Leg Sweep](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=119381) ready to interrupt the [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) and double  [Knife Dance](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235823). You can use  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) to desync the two [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489)s for easier group damage, but make sure it's available when going into the next wave. Use personals if you’re caught off guard by the double fixate, and remember to use  [Transcendence: Transfer](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=119996) to give yourself some space if you’re running out.
- Wave Five: One [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) + One [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) + One [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489)

  - Try to  [Leg Sweep](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=119381) the [Corrupted Risen Mage](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118491) and [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489) together, while maintaining  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) whenever the [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) casts  [Mana Sting](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235992). Kite the [Corrupted Risen Soldier](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118489) whenever it fixates you, and be ready to heal the random damage the [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) does in conjunction with  [Knife Dance](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235823) and  [Arcane Blitz](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235833).

### Stage Two: Searching for the Rook

Rest phase. You can let cooldowns come back up, drink for mana, or anything else that you need to do to prepare for the next part of the challenge.

### Stage Three: Desperate Times

This part of the challenge is timed, with both damage and healing parts to it. After your allies are whisked away, you will be given 5 minutes to ascend the stairs and free them, with demons along the way.

In the room you’re in will be six [Flickering Eye](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119475)s, which will explode when killed, dealing a sizable amount of damage to yourself. Take them out one at a time, healing up in between.

The gate leading out will open, revealing a couple [Unstable Fel Orb](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119473)s, three [Fel Bat Pup](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119469)s, and a [Felspite Dominator](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119472). Kill a [Fel Bat Pup](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119469) with  [Crackling Jade Lightning](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=117952), and focus down the other two when they’re in melee, saving the [Felspite Dominator](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119472) for last. Any damage on [Unstable Fel Orb](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119473)s will render them inert for a brief time.

After dealing with that group, open up the gate to find 4 friendly NPCs (a [Panicked Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119471) and three [Damaged Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119470)s).  [Detox](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115450) the [Panicked Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119471) before you yourself are feared through  [Incite Panic](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=237178), and heal the three [Damaged Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119470). You can safely use  [Life Cocoon](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116849) on one to shield them from the fire damage, as well as apply an amplified  [Renewing Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115151) and  [Enveloping Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=124682) to them, while using  [Thunder Focus Tea](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116680)  [Enveloping Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=124682)s on the other [Damaged Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119470) and healing them to full. If the [Damaged Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119470)’ health hit zero, they turn into an enemy that casts  [Knife Dance](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235823), slowing down this part of the stage considerably.

These NPCs will not be added to party frames, meaning you’ll have to manually target them in order to heal them.

Once these four NPCs have been healed, there’s a [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) at the top, near the gate leading to the rest of the stairway. This time, you have to kill them yourself. Handle them the same way you did in Stage One, using  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) during  [Mana Sting](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235992). If  [Paralysis](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115078) is not available, you can use one of the healed [Damaged Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119470)s to soak the  [Mana Sting](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=235992).

Do note that the [Corrupted Risen Arbalest](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118492) can target the [Damaged Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119470)s you healed up and, if they are brought to zero health, can become another enemy to fight, almost guaranteeing a wipe.

After unlocking the next gate, the stairway is covered with [Unstable Fel Orb](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119473). Thankfully we can angle  [Chi Burst](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=123986) all the way up the hallway, making this brief part of Stage Three trivial.

After another locked gate is a [Dread Corruptor](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118487) and three [Flickering Eye](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119475)s. Take out the [Dread Corruptor](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118487) first, watching out for the [Flickering Eye](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=119475)s’ movement to be out of their AoE effect.

### Stage Four: What Lies Ahead

You will be given another break to let cooldowns reset and to drink mana.

### Stage Five: Erdris and the Risen

At this point, your allies will attack one another, while [Lord Erdris Thorn](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118982) summons spirits that you must heal.

Use Potion of Prolonged Power,  [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=198664),  [Revival](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115310),  [Life Cocoon](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116849), and any other cooldown available to make sure you heal the eight mobs required to pass this stage, while babysitting your allies. Any spirits that reach [Lord Erdris Thorn](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118982) will be used in the next stage. If more than two get through, it's very difficult to recover.

Make sure to watch your party closely here. Granny Marl and Callie tend to take the brunt of the damage and can easily die if you are too focused on healing the mobs. Instant [Enveloping Mist](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=124682) from [Thunder Focus Tea](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116680) can help with Waves 1 and 3, and[Revival](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=115310),can almost deal with one wave itself. If you need to heal an add quickly, a 12-stack [Sheilun's Gift](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=205406/sheiluns-gift) can single-handedly heal one while also healing your allies if you have [Whispers](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238130/whispers-of-shaohao). Otherwise, spam [Vivify](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify). This can be a bit mana intensive so make sure you have an Ancient Mana Potion on you, should it be required.

### Stage Six: Rise, Soldiers!

Some dialogue between your allies and [Lord Erdris Thorn](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118982) will happen, during which you are free to drink for mana, after which any enemies (which can be any from Stage One) that made it through your healing will be summoned and have to be killed.

### Final Stage: A Thorn in the Side

This fight is very much like [Nightbane](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=114895) in [Return to Karazhan](http://ptr.wowhead.com/zone=8443). You will be marked by a debuff ( [Ignite Soul](http://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=228796)) that will deal damage to your allies based on how much health you have remaining. Use the fire patches that are dropped under you when [Lord Erdris Thorn](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118982) leaps to whittle your own health down for the debuff. The NPCs cannot be damaged by the fire on the ground, and the only other damage [Lord Erdris Thorn](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118982) does is not significant, mainly just to [Commander Jarod Shadowsong](http://ptr.wowhead.com/npc=118447).

Make sure to overlap the fire in order to save as much space for yourself as possible. Keep your allies topped off, make sure you don’t die, and congrats!
