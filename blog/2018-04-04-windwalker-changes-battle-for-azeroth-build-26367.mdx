That Buff, [(Spell #137025)](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137025), that's been reduced from -5% to -20% is our aura buff. This isn't really a consequential change, because its just numbers. It also is currently showing up as "undefined" which means it may have been removed from the game, but that may be a bug somewhere or not, who knows.

The more interesting part of that change is the removal of the 100% reduction in [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784)'s cooldown. For those unaware, [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) has a base cooldown of 3 seconds that, until now, has been reduced to 0 seconds for Windwalker Monks. This will get its own section shortly.

The final part is the reveal of another Azerite trait, Iron Fists, which seems aimed at making sure that you keep using [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418) in AOE situations. There's more information on what has been shown about the Azerite traits on [WoWhead today](https://www.wowhead.com/news=283292/azerite-traits-for-testable-heart-of-azeroth-system-in-26367?webhook/azerite-traits-for-testable-heart-of-azeroth-system-in-26367), it looks like the choices will be relatively obvious, although this may just be a placeholder system for the time being.

### Blackout Kick

This change, the removal of the 100% cooldown reduction, means that Windwalkers will have to contend with a 3 second cooldown on our [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784). This change has a plethora, a veritable cornucopia, of potential affects on how Windwalkers are played. Lets look at the four ways of seeing this change in four different perspectives:

##### Compared to Live

The way most people are looking at this change is how it compares to live. From that perspective, it is a HUGE change. Current Windwalkers have the resources to use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) over 75-90 times in 5 minutes (300 seconds). By some basic math, that means that you're using it every 3.3-4 seconds, on average. However, [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) is considered our "dump" or "filler" ability right now, so its used only after other abilities are. If you consider that you're never going to use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) when you're channeling [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418), which can take up 50 seconds of that 300, then you're up to using [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) an average of 2.7-3.3 seconds that you're not channeling [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418). Toss in the [legendary Chest](https://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor) and the time you're channeling [Crackling Jade Lightning](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=172724/crackling-jade-lightning), which eats up another 30 seconds of the 300, and you're left with 220 seconds out of 300 that you're not channeling something. This is why it feels like we're using [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) constantly... because we are... every 2.4-2.9 seconds you're not channeling something else.

However, those numbers are not drastically far away from the new 3 second cooldown, so its probably not that different, right?

**WRONG!**

[Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784)'s biggest role in the current Windwalker "meta" is its ability to be interwoven into our rotation in order to minimize the loss of excess resources. You can use a [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) before and after other abilities, in order to cut down on the excess and get some more damage that would otherwise be lost to overcapping. This can be done in such away that you're never left very long without a button to press. However, with the addition of the cooldown, you won't be able to use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) on either side of an ability, as that would require a 2 second or less cooldown, as [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) and the ability that follow would trigger 2 GCDs. So you'd have to use something in between.

I have not been able to test this on Alpha as there are no character copies to test it with current stats and tier bonuses, which is what makes you need, and want, to [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) so frequently. Just think of all the times you've gone [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) > X > [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) in a fight, whether that X is [TP](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm), [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), or [CW](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave), now you can't do that. This is the perspective that many people are going to be looking at this change from, and certainly the one that the mob will latch on to if they decide to grab their pitchforks over this change.

##### Compared to AOE

The one time that we've always gone [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) > X > [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) in a fight, regardless of what point in the expansion, is during AOE. Going [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) > [TP](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) > [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) in a fight allows us to tag three targets in three GCDs, or 9 targets with [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) up. This is no longer possible with this change. It will take some getting used to, specially with [SCK](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) being down to 2 Chi, making it easier to use and more prevalent in AOE.

##### Compared to Alpha

So on the Alpha, there are no tier bonuses nor legendaries, so the resource overload isn't a thing. This moves [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) back into its role of "nothing else to hit, and a little too many resources than I need" as a dump/filler. From this perspective, the change is barely noticeable. You don't have so many resources that you have to use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) > X > [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784), and even if you do, you are only waiting on [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) or [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418) to come off cooldown, so you may be able to afford to wait the extra 1 GCD without delaying anything else.

Compared to live, Alpha Windwalker only has to use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) 60 times, or so, over 300 seconds, for an average of every 5 seconds. With only [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418), you rarely have to use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) every 4 seconds, on average.

Because of this drastic difference in the frequency of [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784), the 3 second cooldown is not often noticed, and when it is, its not very disruptive outside of AOE target tagging.

##### New (Old) Meta?

This final perspective is one that had been mostly written off initially, but this change may be an attempt to show the switch in Windwalker's priorities. This "new" meta is based around one fact:

**[Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) is our highest priority ability based on Damage per CD time.**

Yes, folks, you read that correctly. Damage per CD time is how we've prioritized abilities if you assume you're capable of having all the resources you need and want to minimize the risk of losing damage. If you look at Damage per Chi, the metric we've used to prioritize abilities for much of the time Windwalker has existed, [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) is slightly ahead of [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and slightly behind [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418). Furthermore, if you look at Damage Per Execution Time, the metric that gets used when you're looking at trying to fit as much damage as possible into a small window, such as a [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) buff or [ToD](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death) window, [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) continues to be above [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418).

This means that, if the current number tuning on Alpha are to be believed, [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) is just about our most important ability.

This has a cascading effect on how the spec plays in a variety of ways. If you prioritize [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) over other abilities, as the current priorities would suggest, there's not a lot of extra time, or resources, for [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418). Most of your Chi goes into using [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) and [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) as close to on CD as possible. This resigns [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117418) to the position that it was years ago during Siege of Orgimmar, where it was used just to fill time and do some damage while you refilled energy waiting for other abilities to come off cooldown. So while this is a pretty big change from how things play currently, it is a situation that we've seen before, and was the "normal" pre-Warlords.

The cascading effect, and complicating factor, comes into effect when you consider where that places [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) into the scheme of things. This is a topic for later discussion and further theorycrafting by the people smarter than I, so stay tuned for that over the next several months. If you'd like to take part in this or any other discussion pertaining to theorycrafting next expansion, then check out the #wind-craft channel in Discord.

If this is truly the new way Windwalker is to be played, you'll be hearing a lot more about this over the next few months.
