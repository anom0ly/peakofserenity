**As always, things on this page are subject to updates as we discover new things or things change.**

## Class Changes

- [Mark of the Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=220357/mark-of-the-crane) has been removed, no more needing to track debuffs on targets.
- [Mastery: Combo Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes) has been increased by 35% making it a very desirable stat.
- [Ordered Elements](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=451463/ordered-elements) has been made much easier to use, no longer needing to consider or maintain the [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) chi reduction.
- [Darting Hurricane](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=459839/darting-hurricane) has been replaced by [Slicing Winds](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1217413/slicing-winds) giving a DPS/movement combination.
- [Last Emperor's Capacitor](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=392989/last-emperors-capacitor) has been dramatically buffed, making it a much stronger talent.
- [Dance of the Wind](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=432181/dance-of-the-wind) has been buffed and is now a strong defensive "passive" for physical damage.
- [Heart of the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent) now triggers each time you cast [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord), but its duration has been decreased to 6 seconds.
- [Xuen's Bond](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392986/xuens-bond) now reduces the cooldown of [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) by a static 30 seconds.

## What To Do

#### Talents

The first, and biggest, change for 11.1 is that **Conduit of the Celestial has become the recommendation for Raiding and Single Target**. This is because Undermine has quite a few fights where burst on a 90-second window is very useful and Conduit has pulled ahead of Shado-Pan in the sims.

**For Mythic+ you can play either Conduit or Shado-Pan as both are roughly equal in AOE situations**. Conduit will be stronger in smaller packs and groups where you plan things around the 90-second cooldown cycle. Shado-Pan is more consistent, less reliant on a strong cooldown window, and scales better with big packs.

For more information and our currently recommended talent builds, **[CLICK HERE](/tww/windwalker/pve-guide/#talent-builds)**.

#### Stats

With the buff to Mastery, it moves into a close 1st place in front of Haste, so the overall priority looks like this:

**Agility >> Mastery = Haste >> Versatility = Critical Strike**

#### 2H vs 1H

With Embellishments being weaker and the improved stat scaling, **2-handed weapons have passed Dual Wielding for the general recommendations**. There is a caveat that if you only care about Single Target damage, you will want to grab either both [Capo's Molten Knuckles](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=232804/capos-molten-knuckles?bonus=7981:12042:5878:11996) and [Gallywix's Iron Thumb](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=228899/gallywixs-iron-thumb?bonus=7981:12042:5878:11996) or one of them, a crafted off-hand with [Darkmoon Sigil: Ascension](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=226024/darkmoon-sigil-ascension), and another piece with [Writhing Armor Banding](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=219506/writhing-armor-banding). This is only better in pure single target and drops off quickly with more targets.

#### Embellishments

Embellishments are at an all-time low for strength, as they don't scale as well as the rest of your gear, so having the "wrong" embellishment is not very problematic.

- If you're only interested in single target damage then craft two items made with [Elemental Focusing Lens](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=213770/elemental-focusing-lens).
- If you're interested in more than just single target damage then craft two items with [Duskthread Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=222873/duskthread-lining)  or [Dawnthread Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=222870/dawnthread-lining) .
  - They're practically equal, so which one is largely irrelevant and whats "best" will change based on your other gear, but never so much that it matters.
- If you, for some reason, cannot get a 2H weapon at 678 item level, then you can craft one with [Darkmoon Sigil: Ascension](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=226024/darkmoon-sigil-ascension)  and another piece with [Writhing Armor Banding](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=219506/writhing-armor-banding).
  - This does require 3 Crests instead of 2, so it should be an absolute worst-case-scenario option.
- Your lowest item level slot, preferably the ones with smaller stat budges.

#### Tier

**The tier set is largely passive, so there's nothing you need to worry about with it.** If you played in TWW Season 1, that tier bonus isn't very strong, so its likely that you will replace it for the Season 2 set rather quickly, potentially as soon as you can get [2-Set Bonus](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1215717). **As with most things, it is always best to sim the options you have to know what is best for your character.**

#### Trinkets

**[Eye of Kezan](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=230198/eye-of-kezan?bonus=7981:12042:5878:11996) was nerfed, so it's not as good anymore**. [House of Cards](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=230027/house-of-cards?bonus=7981:12042:5878:11996) and [Mechano-Core Amplifier](https://www.wowhead.com/item=232485/mechano-core-amplifier?bonus=657:12042:5865:11990) are the current top simming trinkets.

#### Consumables

- Food - You may reach the point of stat food being better than Agility food, sim your options to know for sure, but any difference is doing to be nearly negligible.
- Weapon Enchants -
  - Single Target: [Enchant Weapon - Authority of the Depths](https://www.wowhead.com/item=223784/enchant-weapon-authority-of-the-depths) (use 2 if dual wielding)
  - AOE: One of the Stat enchants is likely to be best and change depending on your character, **sim for your character through [Top Gear](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear).**

**Sim your gear set up again to double check any changes that are needed.**
