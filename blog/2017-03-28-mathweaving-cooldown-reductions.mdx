With the launch of 7.2, Mistweavers get a trait that reduces the cooldown of [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival), [Tendrils of Revival](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238058/tendrils-of-revival). Each rank put into this trait reduces the cooldown by another 10 seconds. [Petrichor Lagniappe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137096/petrichor-lagniappe) must be a fantastic legendary now, right? With all of the [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival)s per fight, it must be great boon to have to stack any available [Tendrils of Revival](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238058/tendrils-of-revival?rank=0) relics with [Petrichor Lagniappe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137096/petrichor-lagniappe), right? Surely better than 5% more healing to [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) or [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font), or another second added on to [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist), right? There's a huge caveat with this line of thinking that I want to talk about.

All cooldown reductions that can have their effects stacked are not as good as you may initially think.

Back in July 2016, I made a [Petrichor Lagniappe spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1sHCT58-4w1BX455U8ydZRcM8JeuL6zlc5dlOvAQFBeE/edit?usp=sharing) to track just how many seconds off of [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) you can manage through various methods, whether through using all available [Thunder Focus Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea)s (and whether [Focused Thunder](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197895/focused-thunder) or [Rising Thunder](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=210804/rising-thunder) talents were selected) solely on [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist), or just using [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on cooldown.

Just by using [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on cooldown, [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) had an effective cooldown of 144 seconds, or 2:24. [Petrichor Lagniappe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137096/petrichor-lagniappe) knocked off a total of 36 seconds from [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival). If you did not use [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) in those 36 seconds, the effect of [Petrichor Lagniappe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137096/petrichor-lagniappe) was useless. This shows [Petrichor Lagniappe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137096/petrichor-lagniappe)'s niche: consistent, bursty damage.

![](/img/uploads/2017/03/Petrichor-Grace-Period.png)

The same deal happens with [Tendrils of Revival](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238058/tendrils-of-revival?rank=0) relics. If you do not use the extra [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) within 10 seconds of it coming off cooldown, it's a wasted effect on the relic, better spent on [Infusion of Life](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199380/infusion-of-life?rank=0), [Essence of the Mists](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199485/essence-of-the-mists?rank=0), or [Extended Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199372/extended-healing?rank=0), as they're more reliable healing increases.

The combined effect of [Petrichor Lagniappe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137096/petrichor-lagniappe) and 4/4 [Tendrils of Revival](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=238058/tendrils-of-revival?rank=0) is undoubtedly strong, but will have very niche cases. But when those very specific cases come up, it will be *very* powerful.
