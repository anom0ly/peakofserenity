**As always, things on this page are subject to updates as we discover new things or things change.**

## Class Changes

There haven't been any class changes specific for Season 4, just the [buffs from December](/2023/12/15/windwalker-buffs-take-2/) and the future prospect of a [Talent overhaul in The War Within](/2024/04/17/tww-alpha-windwalker-talent-rework/).

## Other Changes

With thew return of the Vault tier set, you don't need to worry about [Blackout Reinforcement](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=424454/blackout-reinforcement). The Tier set doesn't require any thought as it should take care of its self naturally.

## What To Do

#### Embellishments

The [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch) path has officially given way to [Blue Silken Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193946/blue-silken-lining) in pretty much all situations. You can technically stick with [Slimy Expulsion Boots](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193451/slimy-expulsion-boots) and a [Toxified Armor Patch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193555/toxified-armor-patch) in a situation where the uptime of [Blue Silken Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193946/blue-silken-lining) is less than 30% or so **and** there are no additional targets. But this is SUPER unlikely.

You'll probably want to craft your [Blue Silken Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193946/blue-silken-lining) in a Neck and/or Ring slot since its ok to be slightly lower item level in a slot without Agility.

#### Bullion

During Season 4, we are once again able to buy targeted raid items with [Antique Bronze Bullion](https://www.wowhead.com/item=213089/antique-bronze-bullion). You can acquire one of these per week, and non-cosmetic items each cost 2. We recommend buying the following items:

1. [Djaruun, Pillar of the Elder Flame](https://www.wowhead.com/item=202569/djaruun-pillar-of-the-elder-flame)
2. [Manic Grieftorch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=194308/manic-grieftorch)

From here, the path splits based on your preferred content.

**If you mainly do raid:**

3. [Ashes of the Embersoul](https://www.wowhead.com/item=207167/ashes-of-the-embersoul)
4. [Seal of Diurna's Chosen](https://www.wowhead.com/item=195480/seal-of-diurnas-chosen)
5. [Cataclysmic Signet Brand](https://www.wowhead.com/item=207166/cataclysmic-signet-brand)
6. [Seal of Filial Duty](https://www.wowhead.com/item=195526/seal-of-filial-duty)
7. [Ouroboreal Necklet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=210214/ouroboreal-necklet)

**If you mainly do mythic+ with [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire):**

3. [Neltharion's Call to Dominance](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204202/neltharions-call-to-dominance)
4. [Seal of Diurna's Chosen](https://www.wowhead.com/item=195480/seal-of-diurnas-chosen)
5. [Seal of Filial Duty](https://www.wowhead.com/item=195526/seal-of-filial-duty)
6. [Ouroboreal Necklet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=210214/ouroboreal-necklet)

**If you mainly do mythic+ with [Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity):**

3. [Ominous Chromatic Essence](https://www.wowhead.com/item=203729/ominous-chromatic-essence)
4. [Seal of Diurna's Chosen](https://www.wowhead.com/item=195480/seal-of-diurnas-chosen)
5. [Seal of Filial Duty](https://www.wowhead.com/item=195526/seal-of-filial-duty)
6. [Ouroboreal Necklet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=210214/ouroboreal-necklet)

#### Trinkets

We have a lot of strong trinkets that perform similarly at the moment, so as always, **sim your options** to see what’s best for you in particular.

- Many of the raid trinkets are worth using in different situations. Notable trinkets are [Manic Grieftorch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=194308/manic-grieftorch), [Ashes of the Embersoul](https://www.wowhead.com/item=207167/ashes-of-the-embersoul), [Neltharion's Call to Dominance](https://www.wowhead.com/item=204202/neltharions-call-to-dominance), [Ominous Chromatic Essence](https://www.wowhead.com/item=203729/ominous-chromatic-essence) and [Cataclysmic Signet Brand](https://www.wowhead.com/item=207166/cataclysmic-signet-brand)
- Dungeon trinkets worth mentioning are [Ruby Whelp Shell](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193757/ruby-whelp-shell#comments:id=5452406) (fully trained), [Algeth'ar Puzzle Box](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193701/algethar-puzzle-box) and [Irideus Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193743/irideus-fragment).
