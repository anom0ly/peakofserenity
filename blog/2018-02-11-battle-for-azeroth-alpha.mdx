![](/img/uploads/2017/03/world-of-warcraft-battle-for-azeroth-expansion-generic-banner-300x110.png)News is starting to filter in from the Battle for Azeroth Alpha. There's no real news on any Mistweaver changes and it's too soon to take anything released as gospel just yet. From the notes so far, it looks like Leg Sweep will be baseline at lvl 60, as the talent in that row has been replaced by one that reduces the cooldown and increases its range (which will be nice for dungeon content). Other than that, the talent tree looks pretty much the same so far, so we'll just have to hang tight and see what changes and which – if any – of our artifact traits become baked into the spec.
