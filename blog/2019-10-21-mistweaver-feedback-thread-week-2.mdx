Continuing on from last week's quick write up, we have some new feedback topics that need some discussion. ## Some New Spell Ideas

We have an a proverbial Chi Explosion of ideas posted the other day by user Shadow. With 4 or so new threads on ideas around how some of our basic [DPS abilities](https://mist.mathweaving.com/posts/27/rising-mist-to-become-chi-explosion) can be [enhanced to provide more interaction](https://mist.mathweaving.com/posts/26/rising-mist-bring-back-vital-mists), an [Essence Font replacement idea](https://mist.mathweaving.com/posts/29/shroud-of-mist-to-replace-essence-font), and an interesting idea to [change Rising Mist](https://mist.mathweaving.com/posts/25/rising-mist-proc-gust-of-mist). While I need some time to think and respond on the DPS threads, the [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) change caught my eye. I don't think I am a fan of limiting [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) healing, and moving [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) to proc Mastery off of those with [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on them. This is basically a DPS version of the [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) spell we have today. I think it is a potentially interesting change, but right now, doesn't seem to fit without some additional changes to [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify).

## 2nd Cooldown? Really, Revival is not enough?

A thread that was slightly under the radar last week around adding a [second healing cooldown to Mistweaver](https://mist.mathweaving.com/posts/24/2nd-cooldown), beyond just [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival). With pretty much every other healing class have a lower impact, but more frequently available baseline cooldown, Mistweaver is certainly missing out in this area. There is some interesting thoughts in the thread itself you should go check out. I for one think a small, targeted DR type spell may be interesting. Making it a shorter cooldown, AoE bubble we either channel for the duration, or has a longer cast time to provide some level of thought and planning for when to use. Jump in and let us know your thoughts.
