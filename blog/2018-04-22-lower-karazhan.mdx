*This guide assumes that you have a knowledge of the mechanics of both bosses and trash in the dungeon and will take a more monk focused approach. For more information on the mechanics of all the different mobs and bosses in the dungeon check out the guide over at [Wowhead.](http://www.wowhead.com/lower-return-to-karazhan-dungeon-strategy-guide)*

## Talents

The recommended talents for Lower Karazhan are:\
-[Chi Wave](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave)\
-[Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) / [Chi Torpedo](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115008/chi-torpedo)\
-[Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir)\
-[Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep)\
-[Dampen Harm](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122278/dampen-harm)\
-[Hit Combo](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo)\
-[Whirling Dragon Punch.](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch)

The damage talents selected are taken due to being the current best setup in all scenarios, there is no real deviation in talents for aoe/st.

[Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can't be used for any dispels in Lower Karazhan, however a number of small corridors make Chi Torpedo somewhat annoying and hard to use, although it does come down to personal preference

A strong mix of damage throughout the dungeon means that [Dampen Harm](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=122278/dampen-harm) is the best choice.

## Boss Tips

#### Opera Event: Wikkit

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips:**\
-You can use [Transcendence](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) to get back to the ground quickly after dodging [Magic Magnificent](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=227776/magic-magnificent).

#### Opera Event: Westfall Story

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips:**\
-Make sure you have some damage ready to burst down the adds on the Murloc side, the [Bubble Blast](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=227420/bubble-blast) that they cast can be extremely dangerous on higher keys as it can easily combo with [Wash Away](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=227783/wash-away) by knocking you into it.\
-When the two main bosses are active it can become quite hectic and there won't be much room to maneuver, try to use [Tiger's Lust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) to aid your movement as rolling into deadly mechanics can be quite easy here.

#### Opera Event: Beautiful Beast

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips:**\
-You have to make sure that your clones are focused on the target your group intends to focus first, dealing damage to the other 2 adds as WW is completely useless.\
-Delaying [FoF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) 8s for [Kara Kazham!](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=232153/kara-kazham) can be very useful as the summoned forks can end up doing a lot of damage if [Dinner Bell!](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=227987/dinner-bell) goes through.

#### Moroes

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\

**WW specific tips:\** -There isn't much related to WW specifically here, just push damage on the priority targets if you're going for a CC and focus one strat. If you plan to let all 4 come down then you can play with [Drinking Horn Cover](http://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover&spec=269) and keep your clones unfocused for [SCK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) stacks.

#### Maiden of Virtue

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\

-There isn't anything really specific here other than using [Roll](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=109132/roll) and [Flying Serpent Kick](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101545/flying-serpent-kick) well to handle [Sacred Ground](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=227789/sacred-ground).

#### Attumen the Huntsman

**Gear**:\
-Legendaries: [The Wind Blows](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151811/the-wind-blows) + [The Emperor's Capacitor](http://www.wowhead.com/item=144239/the-emperors-capacitor&spec=269)\
-Trinkets: [Golganneth's Vitality](http://www.wowhead.com/item=154174/golganneths-vitality&spec=269) + [Shadow Singed Fang](http://www.wowhead.com/item=151968/shadow-singed-fang)\
-Other: A normal ST set is advised (T21 4pc)

**WW specific tips:\** -It's possible to solo the Shared Suffering mechanic as WW. You have to place a [Transcendence](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) spirit in the middle of the room then as the boss casts the ability you [Provoke](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115546/provoke) him and run out, when his cast is nearly finished you [Transcendence: Transfer](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119996/transcendence-transfer) away and dodge it at the last second.

## Trash Tips

#### Leg Sweep

**Notable packs to be ready to** **stun:**\
-Packs of philanthropists at the start should be stunned to stop [Pennies from Heaven](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=228001/pennies-from-heaven) casts.\
-Ghostly Understudies will do a very deadly cast in an area around them, stunning them can you get some free damage in.\
-Making sure you have [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) available from the packs below the stage is very important, if too many [Firelands Portal](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=232115/firelands-portal) casts go off you will definitely wipe and the run back takes a very long time.\
-There are many big pulls between the Opera Event and Maiden of Vigilance, all with many different casts to stop. Setting up a rotation for stuns with you group will be very helpful here so that you don't waste [Leg Sweep](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep).\
-In the Attumen room the large packs of Spectral Apprentices and Spectral Chargers, stunning the Chargers to help lock them down during [charge](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=228603/charge) casts or picking up any [Trampling Stomp](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=241828/trampling-stomp) casts you may miss can be impactful.

#### Paralysis

There isn't much specific to use [Paralysis](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) for so try and pick up any interrupts when your [Spear Hands Strike](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116705/spear-hand-strike) is on cooldown.
