##### Site

Say what you want about the state of the game, but things have been slow for the past few months. There hasn't been any class changes since [March 12th](/2019/03/10/windwalker-8-1-5-survival-guide/), there are no Monks in Classic, so nothing to talk about there, 8.2 is still more than a month away and is void of Windwalker spec changes, and Essences are still in flux, so any discussion or work to figure them out could be rendered obsolete quickly.

Because of all this, and a general lack of disappointment in [my own performance in-game](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1e6m46lmlTxLX7OSy2zHNb7cG-sWewrVZeNYHVkG0tlM/edit?usp=sharing), there hasn't been much content on this site for Windwalker, save [JFunk's thoughts on the state of Windwalker](/2019/04/22/my-thoughts-on-the-state-of-windwalker-monk-going-into-8-2/). I had started a series where I was going through ways to improve the spec, and got as far as outlining how Blizzard could balance the [Level 15](/2019/02/02/windwalker-improvements-1a-level-15-talent-balance/) and [Level 45](/2019/02/12/windwalker-improvements-1b-level-45-talent-balance/) talents. I do plan on continuing that series, as well as addressing the [thread on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/wow/comments/alrt9x/windwalker_monk_spec_design_suggestions/) that I started where I'll discuss people's suggestions and how realistic they are.

However, in the meantime, I was able to finish our guide for [Advanced Tricks](/tww/windwalker/) that you can do as a Windwalker to truly get the most out of the spec with some tricks that you may not have known about. Hopefully this helps you eek out a bit more damage. As more things pop up, we'll add them to the list.

I've gone through and scaled down what we have offered to simple examples of each kind of apparel that we have available to sell.
