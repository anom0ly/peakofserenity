New PTR, detailing the new legendary ring, as well as changes to our Tier 20 bonuses.

#### Build 24116

- [Soul of the Grandmaster](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=151643/soul-of-the-grandmaster) - New legendary ring that gives the effect of [Mist Wrap](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap), allowing you to equip it along with another Level 45 talent.
- [Doorway to Nowhere](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=151784/doorway-to-nowhere) - New legendary cloak that, as of this build, allows each Essence Font to have a chance to Invoke Chi-Ji the Red Crane for an unknown amount of time.
- [Fistweaving Legendary?](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=151788/ph-blackout-kick-grants-effuse-stacks) - While listed as a Brewmaster legendary, there are other cases where one spec has benefited off another's legendary (Balance's [Lady and the Child](https://www.wowhead.com/item=144295/lady-and-the-child) working for Guardian, for example) where this could prove to be useful for Mistweavers. Nothing to get worked up over just yet, but something to think about.
- [Tier 20 2 Piece Bonus](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=242257/item-monk-t20-mistweaver-2p-bonus) - Essence Font has a 30% chance to proc [Surge of Mists](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=246328/surge-of-mists), reducing the cost of Enveloping Mist by 75%.
- [Tier 20 4 Piece Bonus](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=242258/item-monk-t20-mistweaver-4p-bonus) - Consuming [Surge of Mists](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=246328/surge-of-mists) grants a 10 second buff [Dance of Mists](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=247891/dance-of-mists) that increases healing by 12%.

#### What These Changes Mean

Okay, new legendaries are always exciting. As of this build, [Soul of the Grandmaster](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=151643/soul-of-the-grandmaster&bonus=3530) is not worth taking whatsoever. It's held back by low secondary stats and an underwhelming effect in raids. [Doorway to Nowhere](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=151784/doorway-to-nowhere) has too little information to make any assumptions on right now. It could be good, or utter crap.

The [Tier 20 Two Piece Bonus](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=242257/item-monk-t20-mistweaver-2p-bonus) change is not good. Going from a guaranteed 2 procs per minute, increasing with Haste, to a low chance for every [Essence Font](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) we cast is such a hit to its overall proc rate that it is ultimately unreliable. Combine that with the fact that [Essence Font](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) is cast as damage is incoming, where the healing bonus is needed most, you need a point where you cast [Essence Font](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font), gain the [Surge of Mists](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=246328/surge-of-mists) buff, and have more damage incoming soon to benefit from the [Dance of Mists](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=247891/dance-of-mists) buff.

The [Tier 20 Four Piece Bonus](https://ptr.wowhead.com/spell=242258/item-monk-t20-mistweaver-4p-bonus) is good, but is tied to the low proc rate from the Two Piece that it loses its power and can be unreliable when you need it.

Unreliability kills anything for healers.
