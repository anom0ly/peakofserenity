Following up on [last Friday's build](/2018/04/13/mistweaver-changes-battle-for-azeroth-alpha-build-26433/), more Mistweaver changes are here, including a slew of talent changes, as well as some base spell number tweaking. Let's go!

**Until databases are updated, the links to spells will display the previous build’s tooltips.**

### New This Build

*New Talent Pane*

- [Mist Wrap](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap) - Can no longer channel [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) while moving. Just increases the duration and healing bonus of [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) (1 second and 10%, respectively).
- [Tiger Tail Sweep](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=264348/tiger-tail-sweep) - Touched on in the [initial Mistweaver Alpha post](/2018/02/16/first-look-at-mistweaver-in-battle-for-azeroth-8-0-1-build-26032/), but, since [Leg Sweep](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) went baseline, this talent was introduced to augment it. Increases its radius by 2 yards and reduces its cooldown by 10 seconds.
- [Summon Jade Serpent Statue](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115313/summon-jade-serpent-statue) - The [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) channeled by the statue heals for has been increased from about 16.3% per tick to about 51.6% per tick.
- [Essence Well](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274963/essence-well) - For every 6 seconds [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) spends off cooldown, your next [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) may be channeled for 1 additional second. The duration of [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font)'s heal over time is increased by 4 seconds.
  - Based on the tooltip of [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) (since that's where the talent displays itself), it looks like 1 [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) bolt is added per second, instead of a plateau of 6 bolts every 6 seconds. The rate at which bolts are added does not appear to scale with Haste.
- [Rising Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) - [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) heals all allies with your [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist), [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), or [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) for 20% Spell power and extends those effects by 2 seconds.
  - If a target has multiple HoTs on them ([Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) and [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font), for example), the heal from [Rising Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) only heals once. It does not heal per HoT on the target.
- [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) radius increased to 30 yards, from 25 yards.
- [Life Cocoon](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon) cooldown reduced from 3 minutes to 2 minutes.
  - Shield strength increased from 700% Spell power to 900% Spell power.
- [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) healing reduced from about 56% Spell power per tick to about 41% Spell power per tick.
  - Definitely a bug. Both [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) and [Jade Serpent Statue](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115313/summon-jade-serpent-statue) out heal it, per tick.
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify)'s [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) cleave healing has been nerfed from 60% Spell power to 45% Spell power.
- [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) triggers a 1 second Global Cooldown, down from the standard 1.5 second GCD.

### Speculation

...Okay. There's a lot to unpack here. The most noticeable change is the repositioning of a majority of our talents. Level 30 row isn't *just* mobility, and Level 75 row isn't *just* defensives. They've been mixed around, allowing you to go full in on mobility or defensives, emulating the [Windwalker changes](/2018/03/31/windwalker-alpha-talent-impressions/), or anywhere in between, depending on what's needed. For fights with more telegraphed and avoidable damage, expect a full mobility suite, with unavoidable damage being soaked up by double defensive talents. For anything in between, take your pick.

[Mist Wrap](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap) leaving its home in the Level 45 row and bedding with the damage + heal talents is an odd thematic choice, but its a straightforward passive that just increases the usefulness of [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist). The Level 45 row has now turned into the mana row, with [Mana Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) leaving the 100 row to sit in as the row's active ability.

The absolutely massive buff [Summon jade Serpent Statue](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115313/summon-jade-serpent-statue) has received makes it more powerful per tick than [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), which is *incredibly* unintentional, so expect a reversion to one of the two's changes soon.

The two new Level 100 talents are situationally amazing. [Essence Well](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274963/essence-well), when at maximum stacks and combined with [Thunder Focus Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea), can be a ludicrous amount of burst group healing in dungeons. At current numbers, this combo would be 1011% Spell power (thirty 33.7% Spell power bolts) to the group over 3 seconds, reduced by Haste, and not counting the healing from the HoT or any [Mastery: Gust of Mists](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117907/mastery-gust-of-mists) chasing. For reference, [Revival](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) would be 900% Spell power instantly. The increased duration on the HoT portion of [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) naturally increases the strength of [Mastery: Gust of Mists](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=117907/mastery-gust-of-mists), simply because the opportunity for HoT chasing is increased. This also increases the burst healing of the current [Mana Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) combo, where, if there were no overlaps (unlikely in a raid limited to 20 people), the HoT will start falling off 6 seconds into [Mana Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea). If it does refresh, it doesn't pandemic (extend past its initial duration by 30% when refreshing an over time effect), it goes back to a 12 second duration, allowing for 50% off [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify)s with amazing single target healing.

[Rising Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) evokes the original form of [Ei'thas, Lunar Glides of Eramas](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137028/eithas-lunar-glides-of-eramas&bonus=3630), but better. Originally, Ei'thas left a debuff on the target you just [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) ed to increase the duration of your HoTs on the next target that *they* hit by 2 seconds. However, the heal on top of the extension is an amazing sight to see. Comboing [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) with a [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) afterward will provide another small burst of instant group healing. With the added chance for a [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) reset from a pre-stacked [Teachings of the Monastery](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery), the potential for this talent is amazing, both from a current numbers perspective and a design standpoint.

All in all, these talents make for some interesting builds for throughput, as well as personal decisions for other tiers, which is what talents should be like. I'm excited for these changes, as well as any more changes/tweaks coming to Mistweaver as *Battle for Azeroth* development continues along.
