A bit reminiscent of [Part 1](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/05/23/panda-on-legion-rsk-vs-bok-in-new-rotation/), but lets look at all spells and disregard artifact traits for now:

[BOK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick): 325% of AP, used 5-ish times per minute without T19. Total: 1625% of AP\
[RSK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick): 750% of AP, used 6 times per minute without T19. Total: 4500% of AP\
[SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord): 1500%+1500% of AP, used 1.5 times per minute. Total: 3375% of AP\
[FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury): 2500% of AP, used 2.4 times per minute. Total: 6000% of AP\
[WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch): 1080% of AP; used 2.4 times per minute. Total: 2592% of AP\
Grand Total: 18000% of AP

This showcases how little impact [BOK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) has in the overall damage. [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) will do twice as much in a couple casts per minute. I'm glad [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) feels relevant: at 3 chi and 1125% of AP, it was too awkward to use on cooldown because it would mostly just mess up [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) usage. At 2 chi, it's really smooth as a "super [RSK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)", with big enough of a cooldown to allow us to delay it for [SEF](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639)/burst AoE/others, maybe it's a bit too strong all of a sudden still.\
[FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) is hitting harder than anything; it'll have on average 4-5 stacks of transfer the power, crosswinds, so its damage will easily be amplified as much as [RSK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)/[BOK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) will from artifact traits.

Other issue: to this 18000% of AP, we'll be adding [chi burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst)/[chi wave](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) 2/4 times per minute, melee hits, potentially [xuen](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger)... However considering [chi burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) damage will AOE anyways, making up for melee hits, we have 6000+3375+2595= 12000% of AP, aka two thirds of our skill damage, which deals AoE damage. This is extremely concerning considering this was just ST rotation with [WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) instead of [serenity](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity)/[chi orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit). WW has many AOE tools, we'll get to that; if the base single target rotation already has half its damage in the form of instant bladestorms (because, let's face it, [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) doing 2250% of AP instantly in a cone is craaaazy compared to the rest of our kit), then balancing is in trouble.

TL;DR: Few important casts ([FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury), [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord), [WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch)) add up to a huge amount of cleaving damage, rest is pretty irrelevant. At least [TEB](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116740) generation meant spending chi felt relevant out of damage windows.
