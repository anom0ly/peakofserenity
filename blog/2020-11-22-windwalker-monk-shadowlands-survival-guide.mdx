Since there is so much going on with the change over to Shadowlands, I thought I'd write up a quick reference for people to see what is changing. This has come with a bunch of new changes, including a brand new [Windwalker PvE Guide](/tww/windwalker/pve-guide/), so check all that out too as it will have the same information as in here. Both will be updated as things change.

## Unchanged

- Talent Choices
- Priority
- Stat Priority
- WeakAuras
- Macros
- Racials
- Professions

## Changes

### Consumables

- Flask – [Spectral Flask of Power](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171276/spectral-flask-of-power)
- Potion – For pure single target, use [Potion of Phantom Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/item=171349/potion-of-phantom-fire), and make sure you pair it with [Shadowcore Oil](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171285/shadowcore-oil). For all other situations, use [Potion of Spectral Agility](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171270/potion-of-spectral-agility). As always, you can also use [Raidbot’s Top Gear Tool](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear)for the most specific and accurate results for your character.
- Rune – [Veiled Augment Rune](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=181468/veiled-augment-rune)
- Food – [Feast of Gluttonous Hedonism](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=172043/feast-of-gluttonous-hedonism)
- Weapon Enhancement – For pure single target, use 1 [Shadowcore Oil](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171285/shadowcore-oil), and make sure you pair it with [Potion of Phantom Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/item=171349/potion-of-phantom-fire). For all other situations, use 1 [Shaded Weightstone](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171439/shaded-weightstone)/[Shaded Sharpening Stone](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171437/shaded-sharpening-stone). If you are dual wielding, the second slot will always be 1 [Shaded Weightstone](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171439/shaded-weightstone)/[Shaded Sharpening Stone](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=171437/shaded-sharpening-stone). As always, you can also use [Raidbot’s Top Gear Tool](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear)for the most specific and accurate results for your character.
- Armor Kit – [Heavy Desolate Armor Kit](https://www.wowhead.com/item=172347/heavy-desolate-armor-kit), though this is mostly utility/optional. Technically, it can provide a very small DPS increase, as [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) and [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) use your max HP as a reference for damage values.

### Enchants

**Major DPS Enhancing Enchants:**

- Weapons – Use [Raidbot’s Top Gear Tool](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear)for the most specific and accurate results for your character, but here are some general recommendations: for single target, use 1 [Enchant Weapon - Sinful Revelation](https://www.wowhead.com/item=172368/sinful-revelation). For AoE, use 1 [Enchant Weapon - Lightless Force](https://www.wowhead.com/item=172370/enchant-weapon-lightless-force). If you are dual wielding, use 1 [Enchant Weapon - Celestial Guidance](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/item=172366/enchant-weapon-celestial-guidance) on the other weapon in all situations. It doesn’t matter which hand each one is on.
- Chest – [Enchant Chest - Eternal Skirmish](https://www.wowhead.com/item=177659/enchant-chest-eternal-skirmish)
- Boots – [Enchant Boots - Eternal Agility](https://www.wowhead.com/item=172419/enchant-boots-eternal-agility)
- Rings – Use [Raidbot’s Top Gear Tool](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear)for the most specific and accurate results for your character. In general, it will usually follow the stat priority above.

**Minor Utility Enchants:**

- Cloak – [Enchant Cloak - Soul Vitality](https://www.wowhead.com/item=177660/enchant-cloak-soul-vitality). Technically, this is a DPS enhancing enchant due to [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) and [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) using your max HP as a reference for damage values.

## New Things

### Covenants

If your focus is....

- Raiding: Kyrian
- Mythic Plus: Necrolord
- Both: Night Fae

Kyrian is the best Covenant for single target and Necrolord in sustained AOE situations, making them the best for their respective contents. However, Night Fae is 2nd in single target and 2nd in AOE, which makes Night Fae the best covenant for people who want to do both at an equal level. You won't be 100% optimal for either, but you'll be close enough for both.

[Link to sims courtesy of Scurch](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/391774290242174996/780167193220350043/st__aoe_covenantlegendary_sims.png)

Currently Night Fae is simming about 2.24% behind Kyrian in pure single target and 4% behind Necrolord in DungeonSlice. Necrolord is only simming 4.75% behind Kyrian for Single Target and Kyrian is only simming 5.8% behind Necrolord for Dungeon Slice. This means that you can reasonably choose any of the three and not see it dramatically hinder your DPS, although the recommendation will stay Kyrian for Raiding, Necrolord for Mythic Plus, and Night Fae if you're looking to split both.

**There is almost certainly going to be more tuning going on, despite how horrible of a thing that will be; so don't feel that you HAVE to choose a Covenant based on sims. They're a huge part to the expansion and a decision that's a real PITA to undo, so don't just blindly follow sims and what gives the highest theoretical DPS.**

### Legendaries

Generally you'll craft them in this order:

1. [Invoker's Delight](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight)
2. [Jade Ignition](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337483/jade-ignition) (Multi-Target)
3. [Last Emperor's Capacitor](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337292/last-emperors-capacitor) (Single Target)
4. [Keefer's Skyreach](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337334/keefers-skyreach)
5. [Xuen's Battlegear](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337481/xuens-battlegear)

[Invoker's Delight](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) is miles ahead in single target and only slightly behind [Jade Ignition](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337483/jade-ignition) in AOE. This does mean that you may want to wait until you complete the first raid to craft your first legendary, but if you focus on Mythic Plus then you can safely start with [Jade Ignition](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337483/jade-ignition).

\*\*NOTE\*\*\* If you know that someone else will be using it, [Judgment of the Arbiter](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=339344/judgment-of-the-arbiter) should fall behind [Invoker's Delight](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) in single target and [Jade Ignition](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337483/jade-ignition) and [Invoker's Delight](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) in Multi-target.
