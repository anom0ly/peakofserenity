Well, now we've come to the moment you've all been waiting for, and I've been dreading; Talents. This is an area that Windwalkers have seen a decent amount of changes in. I will do my best to explain each tier, what is best, and why. Because I am a PvP noob, I'll leave the War Mode PvP talents to someone else.

Fair warning that as with the previous two articles; **what follows is entirely based on today’s information and may not be correct even tomorrow, or whenever the next Beta build goes through**. This has never been more true than at then at the end of this article.

### Tier 1 (level 15)

Although this tier is largely the same, the fact that [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) provides 1 Chi per target damaged makes it the undisputed king of this talent row. This talent row is super straight forward; without any changes, [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) is the best for single target and AOE.

To explain further, right now [Eye of the Tiger](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196607/eye-of-the-tiger) does roughly 28% AP over 8 seconds, or 3.5% AP per second. [Chi Wave](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) does a maximum of 4 bounces, each of roughly 14% AP every 15 seconds, for a maximum of 3.7% AP per second. [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) deals roughly 65.5% AP per target, every 30 seconds, for a maximum of 2.18% AP per target, per second. However, it also provides 1 Chi per target hit. As I mentioned in [this post](/2018/05/10/windwalker-bfa-theorycrafting-3-questions/#fotwt-v-ee), the best way to look at "extra" Chi is to turn it into [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)s, as those do not have a cooldown so they are our "dump" spell. Each [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) is 77% AP, before factoring in the cooldown reduction on [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) and [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick). This adds, at least, another 2.57% AP per second to [Chi Burst](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst)'s maximum, for a total of 4.75% AP per second. As you can see, that's more than 25% more DPS than [Chi Wave](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098/chi-wave) can provide, even in single target. In multiple targets? [Forgetaboutit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zf0ZyoUn7Vk).

### Tier 2 (level 30)

This is still a tier of personal preference, there hasn't really been much change to this tier, just choose whatever way you prefer your movement boost.

### Tier 3 (level 45)

I went into much more depth of it in [this post](/2018/05/10/windwalker-bfa-theorycrafting-3-questions/#fotwt-v-ee), but to summarize; [Ascension](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) stands no chance, [FotWT](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947/fist-of-the-white-tiger) is good for nice smooth damage and, in my opinion "feels" the best, but [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) reigns supreme in all situations due to a much more easily reachable maximum.

### Tier 4 (level 60)

[Good Karma](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=280195/good-karma) is the only one that directly affects DPS, so that will be the default choice unless you specifically need the functionality of the other two.

### Tier 5 (level 75)

Mostly the same as in Legion, with one small tweak: [Diffuse Magic](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) for big magic damage, [Dampen Harm](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122278/dampen-harm) for big physical damage, and [Inner Strength](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261767/inner-strength) for sustained or small damage.

### Tier 6 (level 90)

This tier is much more difficult, potentially more difficult than the next tier. [Hit Combo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo) is almost certainly not going to be a possibility now that its been nerfed and the other options buffed. [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) provides a pretty large amount of consistent AOE damage, and will definitely be the go-to for running old content. [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) provides a HUGE amount of damage in single target and low target cleave. To figure this out, I have to stretch my brain past its potential, but here goes:

I'll use the "constants" from the [previous theorycrafting article](/2018/05/10/windwalker-bfa-theorycrafting-3-questions/#constants) as a basis for these numbers, as well as 0 haste as a baseline. In a 2 minute span, you'll:

- Generate 1200 energy from base regen, which equates to 48 Chi
- Cast [Tiger Palm](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) 24 times - 816% AP
- Cast [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) 12 times - 2,952% AP
- Cast [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) 5 times - 2,355% AP
- Have 9 Chi left over for [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) - 1,314% AP
- Use [Energizing Elixir](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) twice, which based on the previous article, equates to a total of 1,398% AP
- **Subtotal - 8,835% AP**
- Factor in that all the above damage is Physical, and thus beholden to the 29.98% armor penalty...
- **Total - 6,186% AP over two minutes**
- Yes, I'm ignoring the CDR from [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), my brain hurts enough. I'm also ignoring buffs. Deal with it.

So now we have to look at how each talent affects that total:

- [Hit Combo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo) provides "basically" a static 6% increase to damage. So if we increase the total by 6% we get 6,557% AP, or an additional 371% AP over two minutes
- [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) is the most complicated one as it takes away from the total before it adds to it. [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) does 9.2% AP every 0.8s or 11.5% AP per second, which means that it adds a total of 1,380% AP to the total over 2 minutes. However, it also takes away 4 energy per second, or 480 energy over the 2 minutes. If "extra" energy goes into [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), then taking away energy should take away [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick). 480 energy equates to 9.6 [Tiger Palm](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) and 19.2 [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), or 2,191.3% AP, for a net loss of 811% AP, so obviously not desirable in single target. However, if [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) hits 2 targets, then you'll deal more damage than is lost. Factor in that you'd be dropping [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) for [Spinning Crane Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) at higher targets and stacks and it just gets more complicated, but suffice it to say that [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) isn't going to be used in single target.
- [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) is more straightforward, he is up for 20 seconds during the 2 minutes. His formulas are confusing AF, but from my understanding; his [Tiger Lightning](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=125930/crackling-tiger-lightning) does 28% AP per second, and his melees do another 28.6% AP per second, modified by Armor Reduction. So Xuen does a total of 48% AP per second, or 960% AP over the 20 seconds that he's up. His [Tiger Lightning](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=125930/crackling-tiger-lightning) scales up to 3 targets, but his melees do not, this keeps him relevant at 1-3 targets, but he starts to fall off after that.

I hope that this makes sense, and even more, I hope that my math resembles something correct. As I said above, [Hit Combo](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo) doesn't really stand a chance, unless having [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) every 2 minutes isn't frequently enough. [Rushing Jade Wind](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261715/rushing-jade-wind) will be the go-to in AOE situations, and [Xuen](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) in single target and low target cleave.

### Tier 7 (level 100)

Its hard to pick which tier of talents was more complicated to figure out, this one or Tier 6, but here we go. Another note... **DO NOT be surprised if my math is faulty, slightly off, or totally batshit crazy; this is meant to be an introduction, not the end-all-be-all; Dammit Jim I'm a speech therapist, not a mathematician.**

To really understand this tier, you have to understand what you're comparing. Its not the [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) vs [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) that its long been described as, its Shorter CD on [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) ([Spiritual Focus](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=280197/spiritual-focus)) vs [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) AND [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) vs [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity). This makes it a little bit more complicated....

I'm going to use a baseline of 300 seconds as that's frequently the fight duration that sims look at, if we take the above information based on 2 minutes, it equates to 15,465% AP over 5 minutes. As a baseline, [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) has a 90 second cooldown, so in 300 seconds, you'd get 3.3 uses plus the 2 you start with for a total of 5.3. This equates to a 74.2 seconds uptime (14 seconds each to account for the GCD), 24.7% uptime, 8.645% damage increase, or 1,337% AP over 5 minutes.

- [Spiritual Focus](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=280197/spiritual-focus) is pretty easy to figure out. If [Spiritual Focus](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=280197/spiritual-focus) reduces the CD on [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) to 70 seconds, then you'd get 4.28 uses, plus the 2 that you'd start the fight with, for a total of 6.28. It has an up time of 14 seconds after the GCD, that's an uptime of 87.92 seconds, or 29.3%. Since it provides 35% more DPS when its up (45% x 3), that equates to 10.255% more damage, or 1,586% AP over 5 minutes.
- [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is also pretty straightforward. You take the 1,337% AP from [SEF](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) and add in the fact that in 300s you'll get 12.5 uses of [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch), which equates to another 3,109% AP, bringing the grand total to 4,446% AP over 5 minutes.
- [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) is where we take things up another level, and if I didn't screw the math up above (which is unlikely), then here's where I'm sure I will....

First, we need to figure out an "optimal" way to use [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity). Since [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) lasts 12 seconds, but one of them is the GCD, that leaves us with 11 seconds to play with. Since you'd still very likely want to end [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) with a [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury), for the free cast, you'd get 1/4 of its worth at the end of [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity). This leaves 10 GCD. Here's where it really gets complicated...

If you have less than 37.8% Mastery, you'd gain more damage ignoring the Mastery bonus inside [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) and just spam [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) between [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick). If your [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) has a cooldown of 10 seconds, it has a cooldown of 5 seconds inside [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity). Every time you use [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) between that, 1 second comes off the CD of [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick). This means that you'd basically go [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) (5s CD) > [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) (4s CD reduced to 3) > [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) (2s CD reduced to 1) > (0s CD) [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) > repeat. This only fills 3 GCDs, so it could be repeated 3 times, with the 10th GCD being a [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), and the 11th a [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) cast. Because this leads to an extra cast of [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), it pretty much guarantees that you'd want to do this regardless of your Mastery, as the extra cast will likely make up for any lost otherwise.

So now that we have the "optimal" use of [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity), how much does that actually gain us. Well without [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity), during that 12 seconds, we would have cast [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) 1.2 times, and (on average) [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) 0.5 times, equating to 531% AP from those two abilities. Inside [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) you cast [Rising Sun Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) 4 times, and [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) 0.25 times for a total of 1101.75% AP modified by [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity)'s 20%, for a total of 1322.1% AP from those two abilities. If [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) can be used 4.3 times during that 5 minutes, that equals a total of 5,685.03% AP over 5 minutes without taking any of the [Blackout Kick](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)s, energy regen, or the fact that since the spells inside [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) are free, you have even MORE resources to use, or that you will cap energy during [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) and the ensuing [Fists of Fury](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) cast, so you don't gain quite as many resources as you would otherwise. As you can see... complicated. **And, again, don't be surprised if there's no discernible logic to this with this.** [It was tough for me so BACKOFF!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QUf2pmpMfO4)

##### TL:DR

So to recap:

- [Spiritual Focus](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=280197/spiritual-focus) provides 1,586% AP over 5 minutes
- [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) provides 4,446% AP over 5 minutes
- [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) provides 5,685.03% AP over 5 minutes

So, by this testing, [Serenity](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152173/serenity) would be the best talent in this tier for single target. However, there is always the possibility that, like Antorus, the flexibility provided by having a slightly longer buff and second charge may push [WDP](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) back on top regardless of what the hard numbers say.
