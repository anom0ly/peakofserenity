##### [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) has received large fixes, so the majority of this post, specially the list of bugs, is no longer applicable.

Hello fellow monks.\
I initially intended to get a general blog post on all the things you might have missed about Windwalker in the first month of legion; gear, talents, dungeons, performances, legendaries...

However, early on in that post I got stuck on explaining the issues I had with [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire). My drafts got pretty long and since [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is likely the biggest issue about Windwalker right now it deserved its own article. This post is likely to be full of rants. I'm pretty annoyed by the current situation, it's been a problem with Windwalker for several years now and it shouldn't be. This can also work as yet another report of these bugs, although I'm sure I'll forget some. I'll state once again my suggestion in that regard, although I doubt it'll get used.

#### Everyone and their sempai is running Serenity on Warcraftlogs; I was told Whirling Dragon Punch was on par with it?

You might remember a couple [articles](http://www.walkingthewind.com/2016/08/17/serenity-theorycrafting/) we wrote a while back; at that time we used a very simple action priority list for [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) when the talent required it. Despite that, with a couple easy tweaks we were able to have simulations show that on single target, [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) was probably no more than 5-10% worse than [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175), if not better in the long run.

In these articles, I also highlighted the fact that [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) brought a lot more than [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) on 2-5 targets. On this amount of targets, the tagging mechanic of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is not relevant as a skilled player will easily cover all available targets with tab-targeting. When I wrote the article about [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) was doing crazy damage on AoE so that more than made up for the lost [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175) casts, and free [Spinning Crane Kick](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) casts represented way too many free resources.

However, we told our readers (you) that the talents would likely be on par. This is not what we're seeing right now; people are pulling extremely high numbers with [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) and despite the AoE potential, the double stacks, the interaction with [Spiritual Focus](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195298/spiritual-focus), [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is barely seeing play at the highest level.\
Of course part of it is due to the cleave potential of [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173). Emerald Nightmare always has at least a second target on Mythic difficulty. Higher Mythic+ dungeons fit [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) very well, since on 2-5 targets it shines. But the reason lies elsewhere.

[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) has a very high amount of bugs affecting it, many of which can be game-breaking. This doesn't make the spell completely useless since it's very potent, but since the bug free [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is the alternative option, it's too good to pass up.

#### Bugs? I thought Storm, Earth, and Fire was looking fine?

Well, unfortunately you're wrong. We're partly to be blamed for this, as we missed some of the [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) issues on beta or notified them late. However, it's been months since we've reported most of these (trying to whisper blizzard CMs, reporting through the in-game bug report on NA and EU, through alpha/beta bug reports...). We can't be held fully responsible for the main cooldown and iconic spell of the Windwalker not working correctly. Some of the *bugs* I can understand being "intended". However some of these are *most definitely not intended*.

I understand if everything was fixed, Windwalker might be too strong and only mages are allowed to do a lot more DPS than others. However I'd rather see [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) damage percentage reduced slightly so that [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) can remain somewhat competitive. It's a better alternative than being *balanced around bugs*.

Here is a list of some of these issues with the spell:

- ~~**[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) clones do not benefit from haste:**~~ This is probably the most annoying one to me. Clones have a set haste level and do not channel any faster as you gain some. This includes the benefits from [Bloodlust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust). Therefore, if you use then immediately use [Rising Sun Kick](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) at the end with [Bloodlust](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) up, you're likely to have just cancelled 2 out of 5 ticks of [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) from both your clones. That's a 27% DPS loss on [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury), meaning without [Spiritual Focus](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195298/spiritual-focus) it's a 1% DPS loss to use [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) with [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) rather than without if you're not aware of this. That's pretty sad for a *DPS cooldown*.

\*\*This **has** been fixed by Blizzard. However, the fix made it worse. The clones will now cast the instant abilities that you do, while still channeling [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury). The problem is that they now stop at 4 ticks of [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) even if you do not cancel the channel early.\*\*

- **[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) clones stack their own [Hit Combo](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo):** I heard at some point this might be considered as intentional. I find this hard to believe that so many little things would be intended to reduce the mechanics around which the class is balanced to the point where a player might doubt whether using their DPS cooldown is a good idea or not.
- **[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) clones attacked banished targets, even in a cluster of crowd controlled targets:** This is more of a quality of life change, but just like I how I dislike when 33% of my damage is wasted on the immune Eye of Il'Gynoth, I dislike it even more when my Earth Spirit goes to [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) a banished target and breaks [Paralysis](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078) or [Hex](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=51514) in the process.

Some other notable targets in dungeons are tagged as in combat at all times and [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) clones will aggro them. The trash packs before Corstilax in the Arcway and Sentries in Court of Stars are two such targets which can ruin a good run.

- **Activating [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) reduces the damage of some spells and effects that do not get copied**: I've stopped counting these, and I don't know which ones get fixed or not. To be frank, I won't use Fishbrul Special and Potion of the Old War every week to check whether or not they are functioning. The latter is the most annoying one, since it means it's more beneficial to use Potion of the Old War outside of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire), preventing Windwalker from doing effective burst on priority targets, something we would otherwise excel at. This applies to a high number of consumables, trinkets and others. [Apparently](https://twitter.com/BlizzardCS/status/781956504043814912) this might not be considered a bug.
- **[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) will often fail to pick a target quickly enough:** If you intend to use [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) on a target in your opener, you'd better press it twice because otherwise your clones are likely to not cast the first spell after they spawn. It feels really bad to go [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) into [Strike of the Windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) to only get 50% damage on it. It's even truer for [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury). Furthermore, if they are jumping around in a pack, they are more likely to fail at picking targets for [Fists of Fury](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury).
- **[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) reduces the damage from [Tornado Kicks](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=196082/tornado-kicks) but doesn't replicate it**: this is part of the bugs which make little sense, specially considering the other artefact golden trait, [Crosswinds](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195650), doesn't get replicated by [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) but it's damage is tripled during [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) to make up for it.
- **[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) was not benefiting from [Cinidaria, the Symbiote](http://www.wowhead.com/item=133976/cinidaria-the-symbiote)**: I have not tested this legendary since beta testing so I am unsure if this is still the case.
- **[Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) doesn't benefit from [Transfer the Power](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195300/transfer-the-power)**: [Transfer the Power](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195300/transfer-the-power) is strong, but [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) not benefiting from it will just eventually lead to awkward situations where people wonder whether they should [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) or not.

#### These are not bugs - apparently working as intended, Windwalk along folks.

So early during alpha, developers were toying around with [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) as a toggle and [Tigereye Brew](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116740/tigereye-brew) as a DPS cooldown. Many different ideas were tried, each with its own issues. Everything was all right because it was alpha and we didn't expect things to work perfectly. We were happy developers were letting us test so that we could get a feel of it.

One day, [Tigereye Brew](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116740/tigereye-brew) was removed and we were told [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) was now our DPS cooldown, on single target *and* AoE. We were given two stacks of it to replicate our on demand burst from Warlords of Draenor and [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) stopped being a DPS loss on single target.\
Or did it? When this change happened, [Celestalon](https://twitter.com/Celestalon) was *very* good at communicating with the players, going as far as to hop in the community discord for Windwalkers, answering questions. That place was way less crowded back then and a good discussion took place.\
Among things brought up on forums and in Discord was the issue that [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) had been buggy in the past. Many players were uncomfortable with it being our DPS cooldown considering how the clones behaved in the past. The concept looked very unreliable but the idea was really cool and exciting.

We were told that since it was now our DPS cooldown, developers would be aware of it and quicker to fix things not affecting it. And here we are today, more than half a year later. Out of maybe a dozen bugs affecting [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire), only the one where our mastery didn't affect the clones damage (meaning [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) was a DPS *loss* with a strong mastery proc) ended up being fixed.

I believe Windwalker is NOT Blizzard's highest priority; we're a small part of the community and our spec is niche, under represented and always kind of tricky to play. Can we have some quality of life changes for once?

I'm not asking for everything to get fixed but [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) reducing your damage is very triggering.

- This is something I think would help a lot with Windwalker's issues. I've said it before, it can be very annoying for a PvP player to lose 50% of his damage while his pets are up. If they get crowd controlled, he can't really do much and should cancel the buff, which is sad. PvP players are mostly using [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), which makes sense, having only one talent choice for a major cooldown is silly.
- I've suggested this many times before; now that [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is a single target spell, there is no real reason not to have *the main clone deal 100% of the player's damage and the other two deal something like 15% of the player's damage*. This might look like a nerf of 5% damage buff when looking at numbers alone; clone AI and player control would cause it to be a strong buff.
- There is no reason not to have this change happen. +30% damage is very relevant, +45% with [Spiritual Focus](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195298/spiritual-focus) if nothing changed. It could be tuned easily, but more importantly than anything, there would be no way [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) could end up being a DPS loss.

I'll try to go back to discussing these talents, giving players tips about how to play [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) while it's their best option. But above all, I hope we can discuss this issue with developers better than by getting [tweets](https://twitter.com/BlizzardCS/status/781956504043814912) like this. [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) was supposed to be the iconic spell of the new Windwalker. Windwalker is very strong and in a decent spot and can compete with Rogue despite having lower utility. We crush dungeons, and raids, but [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is a failure. Let's make this change folks :(
