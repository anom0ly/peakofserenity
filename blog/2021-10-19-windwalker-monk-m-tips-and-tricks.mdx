UPDATED for patch 9.2's season 3

Now that we’re well into the expansion, the pool of dungeons has become intimately familiar to players that have been pushing their score. However, there’s a ton of small “tricks” you can pull off to make your run just that little bit smoother that newer players might not immediately think of. None of these tricks will make the difference between timing a key and depleting one, but every little bit adds up.

Disclaimer: this guide is intended to highlight the extra things a windwalker can do to make the run easier, and is not an extensive guide on m+ mechanics or strategies. That said, I couldn’t resist the temptation to throw in a few non-windwalker tips as well.

## General

We have a great toolkit to assist a tank with kiting. [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) is a given, allowing for a 5 second pseudo-tank-immunity if placed correctly. [Disable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116095/disable) is an oft-forgotten spell which lets you slow any eligible target by 50% which is refreshed by melee abilities, including [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick). Using this you can keep your targets slowed forever, if it's worth the gcds spent to apply it to each target. You can also [Disable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116095/disable) a target that's already slowed to apply an 8 second root. Activating [Flying Serpent Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101545/flying-serpent-kick)'s stomp ability also slows everything it hits by 70%, which can be a lifesaver for your tank on a cool 20 sec cooldown.

[Tiger’s Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) is a great spell for mobility, but it’s easy to overlook its additional effect: breaking roots and snares (aka slows). This can be a lifesaver, as being rooted can be very dangerous while you’re trying to dodge area-of-effect spells. Some root spells even do a lot of damage themselves. You can also use it on your group members instead, if they get rooted by something.

You can find a list in the [PeakOfSerenity](https://discord.gg/gNpr7Hhh) discord server (#wind-resources-faqs channel) with all the spells that you can reflect with [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic). Remember, you can *dispel* any magic-type debuff (the kind that all healer specs can dispel) with diffuse, but only the ones listed here will be applied to their caster. This can do a large amount of damage on higher keys.

[Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death)'s damage is not affected by any damage amplification *or* damage reduction. This means you may be able to execute a target that is taking reduced damage, like [Ingra Maloch](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164567/ingra-maloch) after casting [Embrace Darkness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=323149/embrace-darkness). Shields will still absorb [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death)'s damage, though.

On [Inspiring](https://www.wowhead.com/affix=122/inspiring) weeks, a few particular packs are especially dangerous to deal with because the Inspiring mob is protecting other mobs with nasty spells. A prime example is the couple of [Zolramus Necromancer](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163618/zolramus-necromancer) packs in Necrotic Wake, protecting his [Brittlebone Mage](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163126/brittlebone-mage)s which will cast [Frostbolt Volley](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=328667/frostbolt-volley). However, the Inspiring mob himself is *not* immune to ccs. This means you can place a [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) in between him and a Mage, knocking it far enough away to no longer affect the Mage, which will then be knocked the other way by the Ring. You can now quickly Paralyze the mage and pull the necromancer without it.

If you use [Cruciform Veinripper](https://www.wowhead.com/item=186388/cruciform-veinripper) from [Painsmith Raznal](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176523/painsmith-raznal), [Disable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116095/disable) can be a great way to keep your target eligible for the application of the DoT. However, [Disable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116095/disable) does cost a little bit of energy and a gcd, so I wouldn't recommend spamming it on all targets just for veinripper.

The best [Encrypted](https://www.wowhead.com/affix=130/encrypted) relic for us is always [Urh](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=368239/decrypted-urh-cypher), primarily to get more casts of [Bonedust Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325216/bonedust-brew), [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire), and [Invoke Xuen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger). The reduced cd on our main abilities like [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) is nice, but not decisive. The 15% haste from [Vy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=368240/decrypted-vy-cypher) and the damage proc just isn't that attractive, so even when a double relic buff is possible (such as in Halls of Atonement) I would still recommend to kill [Uhr](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=368239/decrypted-urh-cypher) twice. *That said,* listen to your tank or whoever is leading you through the dungeon to see if they want to kill [Wo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=368241/decrypted-wo-cypher) for a skip. And for the love of god, hold your big aoe cooldowns until *after* the chosen relic has died, to prevent random relic kills and potentially messing up the route. (Seriously, this happens so often, and yes it's still your fault if you're doing "equal" aoe dps.)

#### Paralysis + Ring of Peace skip

This combination is fairly well known, but there are some details that you need to know about before you accidentally fail the skip and people get mad at you.

Primarily, [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) doesn't work like [Sap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6770/sap) or [Imprison](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=217832/imprison), and *only* freezes the target in place. It does not cause you to enter combat with your target, but it also doesn't reduce the target's aggro range, so you will body pull if you get close to it. You'll need to cast [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) from almost max range to avoid this. This is why it is always used in combination with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) , so you can knock the paralyzed target out of your path.

There is an interesting interaction that can be exploited as a fail-safe; while paralyzed, the target will only enter combat with a single person when they body pull. Other party members can run by the target even within body pull range after that. If you let a hunter or rogue be the first group member to body pull the paralyzed target, they can feign death or vanish after everyone has passed to make the skip successful after all.

Additionally, there is a bug when using the Finesse conduit [Lingering Numbness;](https://www.wowhead.com/item=181510/lingering-numbness) when [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) expires, the slow debuff will aggro the target and pull the group. Don't slot this conduit into your soulbind if you intend to use this skip method.

In order to quickly ascertain if you failed a skip, I highly recommend installing a WeakAura which clearly shows you that you’re in combat. This way, you won’t be caught off guard by [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) wearing off and its victim coming at you with a bone to pick. Even better if everyone in your group has such a WeakAura, so everyone can see when they've pulled and can just run to die for a quick ress rather than pulling the pack later.

## De Other Side

Rolling off the ledges allows you to "jump" a large distance, which may allow for skips. For example, you can avoid the two mobs right at the start of the dungeon this way. The [Risen Cultist](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=168992/risen-cultist)s in the larger pack downstairs will wander around and can be paralyzed when in a favorable position to just walk past, or knocked with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) if not.

Preplacing [Transcendence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) or being quick on the trigger can save your life if you get hit by a [Death Speaker](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=168942/death-speaker)s' [Erupting Darkness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=334051/erupting-darkness) or [Oros Coldheart](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=179892/oros-coldheart)'s [Frost Lance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=356414/frost-lance).

[Hakkar](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166473/hakkar-the-soulflayer)’s [Sons of Hakkar](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=170488/son-of-hakkar) can be knocked with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) right before they die, to limit area-of-denial from the pools they drop.

You can also preplace [Transcendence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) on [Dealer Xy'exa](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164450/dealer-xyexa) to prevent fall damage after getting knocked up by [Displacement Trap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319619/displacement-trap). [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) can also negate this fall damage, but that's arguably not worth the loss of damage if you don't also hit the boss with it. Additionally, you could place your [Transcendence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) on the ledge before dropping down. That way, you have a backup if all the Displacement Traps are taken.

The [Cosmic Artifice](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325725/cosmic-artifice) spell cast by [Mueh’zala](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=169769/muehzala) is a magic-type debuff, so you can dispel yourself with [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) and manually drop the purple exploding orb off to the sides. It won’t do any damage to the boss, though.

## Mists of Tirna Scithe

If you're lacking a hard cc class or a priest, you'll probably be expected to use the aforementioned [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) + [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) skip on some key levels. Specifically, this can be used to skip the [Soulcleaver](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164920/drust-soulcleaver) + [Harvester](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164921/drust-harvester) pack right before the first [Tormented](https://www.wowhead.com/affix=128/tormented) miniboss, and the [Spinemaw Gorger](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=172312/spinemaw-gorger) pack right after the third Tormented miniboss, after [Mistcaller](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=170217/mistcaller). I wouldn’t recommend using the latter, as the positioning makes it pretty sketchy.

It is possible to pull [Tormented](https://www.wowhead.com/affix=128/tormented) minibosses and additional trash packs through the mist wall with [Xuen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger), by using Xuen's pet bar. This is obviously a large dps loss and should be considered a last resort.

Although [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) will reflect [Anima Injection](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325224/anima-injection) from [Mistveil Stingers](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166304/mistveil-stinger), it doesn't quite work as you might expect. The large aoe circle will be on the mob, but the explosion will still hit your group, potentially oneshotting people. It's much safer to hold on to [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) and let the healer dispel you.

[Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) and [Tiger's Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can both be used to remove the root effect from [Bramblethorn Entanglement](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=324859/bramblethorn-entanglement), which can be cast by [Mistveil Guardians](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166276/mistveil-guardian). Diffusing yourself and Lusting a group member (who cannot break roots themselves) is an efficient use of your tools.

[Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) is one of the best tools to counter the [mob](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=165251/illusionary-vulpin) spawned by [Mistcaller](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=170217/mistcaller)'s [Freeze Tag](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=341709/freeze-tag), but keep in mind that it will break on *any* damage taken. As a backup, remember that a double tap [Disable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116095/disable) will root the mob for 8 seconds.

One of the easiest ways to die in the Gorm area is by getting hit by [Spinemaw Gorger](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=172312/spinemaw-gorger)s' [Acid Globule](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=326021/acid-globule) and getting knocked off the edge. A preplaced or quickly cast [Transcendence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) can save your life.

It can be easy to forget that, other than [Spinemaw Staghorn](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=167111/spinemaw-staghorn), all mobs in this area can be stunned and knocked. This can be invaluable in a big pull when [Acid Globule](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=326021/acid-globule) is being cast by several targets at the same time.

[Detox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218164/detox) list:

- [Poisonous Discharge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=340283/poisonous-discharge)
- [Triple Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=340288/triple-bite)
- [Poisonous Secretions](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=340304/poisonous-secretions)
- [Debilitating Poison](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=326092/debilitating-poison)

## Halls of Atonement

All damage dealt by [Halkias](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=165408/halkias) is avoidable, so you should have [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) available as an offensive cooldown. On lower to medium keys, you can safely take a single tick of the [Refracted Sinlight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322913/refracted-sinlight) to consume the Karma shield. On higher keys, simply running through the pools of [Glass Shards](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=323001/glass-shards) will consume your Karma quickly and (relatively) safely.

If your mage or druid is nice enough to decurse your [Curse of Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319603/curse-of-stone) on [Echelon](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=156827/echelon), you can use a preplaced [Transcendence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) or a well-timed [Roll](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=109132/roll) to avoid his [Stone Shattering Leap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319592/stone-shattering-leap). You need to press transcendence or roll *after* he has started his leap, as he'll lock onto your position upon cast. Note: if you play as an orc, your [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20573/hardiness) racial passive allows you to wait for the curse stun to end and still roll/transcendence out just in time.

[Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) can be used to knock in any straggling [Undying Stonefiends](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164363/undying-stonefiend), so that they're nice and grouped up for [Stone Shattering Leap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319592/stone-shattering-leap).

You can taunt the [Ghastly Parishioner](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=165913/ghastly-parishioner)s during the fight with [High Adjudicator Aleez](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=165410/high-adjudicator-aleez). This won’t make them attack you as it doesn’t override their fixation, but it does apply our trademark speed boost from [Provoke](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115546/provoke), making them run into their vessel just a little bit faster.

We have quite a few defensive cooldown at our disposal, even more if you're a Necrolord. This means you can soak the beams on [Lord Chamberlain](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164218/lord-chamberlain)'s [Ritual of Woe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335338/ritual-of-woe) on both of its casts. For example, you can use [Fortifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew) + [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) for the first cast, and [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) for the second. This will be enough for all but very high (think 23+) tyrannical keys. Note: if you stand very close to the boss, you can soak two of the four beams by yourself. For some reason, this doesn’t actually do any more damage than soaking one beam, so it’s always worth doing.

## Plaguefall

There's lots of poison and disease debuffs in this dungeon, and some of them are pretty nasty. [Corroded Claws](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=320512/corroded-claws) from [Rotting Slimeclaw](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163892/rotting-slimeclaw)s can kill an unsuspecting tank very quickly due to the stacking stat reduction. [Dispel](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218164/detox) as much as you can!

[Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) is a great way to keep the [Slimy Smorgasbord](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=171887/slimy-smorgasbord) cc-ed on [Globgrog](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164255/globgrog), but remember to re-cc it before it expires, and don't override your paralysis on a different add.

[Doctor Ickus](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164967/doctor-ickus)' [Slime Lunge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=329217/slime-lunge) can be easily predicted and thus avoided. He will always jump to the farthest player, and the lines he shoots out on impact will always form a plus sign aligned with his jump direction.

When [Doctor Ickus](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164967/doctor-ickus) spawns his [Congealed Slime](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=165010/congealed-slime) add, you can knock it away with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) and [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) it while it’s out of range of the boss. Just make sure to communicate this with your group so they don’t hit the slime and break the Paralysis.

[Tiger's Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can break the root from [Brood Ambusher](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164737/brood-ambusher)s' [Enveloping Webbing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=328409/enveloping-webbing).

Just like [Lord Chamberlain](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164218/lord-chamberlain), our defensive toolkit makes us easily capable of dealing with [Domina Venomblade](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164266/domina-venomblade)'s [Shadow Ambush](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325245/shadow-ambush).

When Domina spawns her invisible [Brood Assassins](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=170474/brood-assassin), you can make good use of the fact that Roll does not interrupt [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick), allowing you to "sweep" across a large area, hitting the stealthed targets.

It bears repeating for [Margrave Stradama](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164267/margrave-stradama): Dispel often! Her [Infectious Rain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322232/infectious-rain) can deal lots of damage, but dispelling a party member at 4 stacks while popping a defensive yourself can make the healer's life much easier. Ideally you even coordinate with your healer, who might have a preference on who gets the dispel.

## Sanguine Depths

When a lantern is activated, it may be difficult for the tank to keep all targets neatly stacked in its area of effect. You can knock in any mobs that refuse to move in with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) .

[Tiger's Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) can break [Grand Overseer](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=162040/grand-overseer)s' [Dread Bindings](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=326827/dread-bindings), saving you the run to break them.

It can also get rid of [Depths Warden](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=171799/depths-warden)s' [Barbed Shackles](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335306/barbed-shackles), which not only frees the player to move, it also removes the DoT which deals a lot of damage on higher keys.

A very small tip: [Executor Tarvold](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=162103/executor-tarvold)'s [Sintouched Anima](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=328494/sintouched-anima) doesn't deal damage or apply its curse for a few seconds after you pull the boss.

Mobility spells can be used to dodge [General Kaal](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=165318/general-kaal)'s [Wicked Rush](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=323845/wicked-rush), avoiding the damage and the dangerous bleed it applies. You can even just strafe while she's rushing at you which will often work as well. Remember that a premature [Transcendence: Transfer](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119996/transcendence-transfer) may lead to the bleed being applied to your group members, so be careful!

## Spires of Ascension

A few mobs will occasionally buff themselves with [Imbued Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=327331/imbued-weapon), which, when purged, will drop a forsworn weapon on the floor that you can pick up for a good amount of bonus damage.

If you use a WeakAura to track timers, or you have *very* quick reflexes, you can paralyze the [Kyrian Dark-Praetor](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163524/kyrian-dark-praetor)s mid-[jump](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=323786/swift-slice) to stop them in their tracks, keeping them close to whatever pack you’re fighting for better cleave. Naturally, [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) can nudge them back in as well.

There are quite a lot of spells that you can reflect with [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) in this dungeon, more than in any other. Refer to the list I mentioned above to see which ones. [Devos](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=167410/devos)' [Lost Confidence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322818/lost-confidence) stands out as being especially valuable, probably the most valuable of any boss spell in m+. You might want to tell you group that you're doing this, as the healer might otherwise snipe the dispel and your tank might get confused by the ominous looking ring that just appeared on the boss.

The pack at the top of the stairs in [Oryphrion](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=162060/oryphrion)’s area is another [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) + [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) skip option, but it has some subtleties to it. As you need to place [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) on the floor, there’s a line of sight issue when you’re trying to place it just beyond the top of stairs next to the right-most mob. You’ll need to move pretty close to be able to place your ring, which is pretty risky. A relatively reliable approach would be to hug the wall, walk close enough to be in range of Paralysis (you still won't be able to cast due to line of sight), jump + Paralyze, jump + Ring of Peace, then jump up on the ledge *in front* of the brazier.

[Astronos](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=168845/astronos)' [Charged Spear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=298425/charged-spear) (and the other Forsworn minibosses after Astronos dies) will always shoot its projectiles in a plus-shaped pattern, aligned with the tilt of the spear itself. You can use this to see if you're currently standing in a safe spot.

Remember you can heal your group members while someone is throwing the [Archon's Spear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322921/archons-spear) at [Devos](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=167410/devos). This can be a strained moment for the healer.

## Necrotic Wake

You can use the [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) + [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) combination to knock the [Corpse Harvester](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166302/corpse-harvester) on the right of the dungeon entrance away, so any group members can sneak in to grab the hammer next to him without body pulling.

[Blightbone](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=162691/blightbone)'s [Heaving Retch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=320596/heaving-retch) leaves behind a nasty disease DoT, remember to dispel it.

It may be useful to Paralyze and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) one the [Brittlebone Mage](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163126/brittlebone-mage)s away from its [Zolramus Necromancer](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163618/zolramus-necromancer). You don't need to kill it as it will die when the necromancer does, and this prevents it from casting its [Frostbolt Volley](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=328667/frostbolt-volley).

When [Amarth](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=163157/amarth) casts his [Final Harvest](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=321247/final-harvest), any cc-ed add will not explode. You can immediately paralyze one of the [Reanimated Mage](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164414/reanimated-mage)s, as they will always spawn some distance away from the boss (and the cleaving melee players). You can also stun all adds at the last second with [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) to guarantee that none of them will explode.

Make sure to save [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death) for [Nalthor](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166945/nalthor-the-rimebinder). If he casts his [Dark Exile](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=321894/dark-exile) on you, you can usually immediately oneshot the add downstairs and return to the fight quickly.

## Theater of Pain

The [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) + [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) skip can be used on the first pack of this dungeon, but their positioning makes it more difficult than most of such skips, making the “gap” between body pull range and the wall of spectators quite small. I wouldn't recommend trying this unless you have a good sense of the body pull range and the distance you can knock a target with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) .

If you track the timing for [Xira the Underhanded](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164464/xira-the-underhanded)'s [Opportunity Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=333540/opportunity-strikes), you can place [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) on top of the melee group (around 2 seconds before the spell hits) and have a decent chance at immediately breaking the stun.

The mobs in the Upper Barrow of Carnage cast a lot of disease and poison spells, so dispel often! The [Blighted Sludge-Spewer](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=174210/blighted-sludge-spewer)s' [Withering Discharge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=341969/withering-discharge) and [Disgusting Burst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=321041/disgusting-burst) apply especially dangerous diseases that can be fatal very quickly if not dispelled. These mobs will also attempt to leap at ranged targets, but you can restrict their movement by placing a [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) on the ranged dps/healer right after the pull starts. (The closer to the melee group you place it, the better, but if it's too close they might leap over it.)

The [Putrid Butcher](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=169927/putrid-butcher) mobs will cast an ability called [Devour Flesh](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=330586/devour-flesh), which will leech a reasonable amount of health from his target. You can interrupt this with a well-timed [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) or [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis).

On some weeks, the [Ancient Captain](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164506/ancient-captain) pack may be especially challenging. You can make the pull a lot safer by paralyzing the [Ancient Captain](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=164506/ancient-captain) and pulling the rest of the pack out of the range of his [Commanding Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=330545/commanding-presence).

The [Maniacal Soulbinder](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=160495/maniacal-soulbinder)s' [Soul Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=333708/soul-corruption) is probably the strongest debuff that you can reflect with [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) out of all the trash spells in m+ right now.

When fighting [Kul’tharok](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=162309/kultharok), consider saving your burst cds for after the first [Draw Soul](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319521/draw-soul) has been cast. With some luck, you’ll have a [30% damage buff](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319637/reclaimed-soul) at that point.

Although Kul'tharok's [Phantasmal Parasite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319626/phantasmal-parasite) won't deal any damage when Diffused, it's still a big help to your healer if you do.

- [Withering Blight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=341949/withering-blight)
- [Decaying Blight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=330700/decaying-blight)

## Tazavesh: Streets of Wonder

The first trash area contains a lot of mobs with casts, which can make it annoying for the tank to keep them stacked. Help him out by interrupting where you can. The [Interrogation Specialist](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=177816/interrogation-specialist)'s [Stasis Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=356031/stasis-beam) stuns his target during the cast, so that's a high priority to interrupt.

On the [Zo'phex](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=175616/zophex) fight, the [Containment Cell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=345990/containment-cell) that he applies during [Interrogation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347949/interrogation) have relatively low health. On most key levels, you'll be able to instantly kill it with [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death).

Now, there are two main routes you can take in this dungeon: the left path, or the right path.\
On the left path, you have to deal with [Tracker Zo'korss](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=179837/tracker-zokorss), who casts [Lockdown](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=356943/lockdown) on the tank which you can break with [Tiger's Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust). The two [Ancient Core Hound](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180091/ancient-core-hound)s will cast [Ancient Dread](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=356407/ancient-dread), which applies a massive cast speed and haste reduction to your whole group and *must* be interrupted, and [Lava Breath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=356404/lava-breath), which deals a large chunk of damage to targets in front of the caster, but a half-decent tank should be able to handle that. The [Enraged Direhorn](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180495/enraged-direhorn) and [Frenzied Nightclaw](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180567/frenzied-nightclaw)s have some targeted abilities that you can play around. If you stand on one of the decorative edges on the sides of the stairs, the Direhorn's [Frenzied Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357512/frenzied-charge) will fail to cast, and the Nightclaws will immediately snap back to the tank after jumping to you with [Frantic Rip](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357827/frantic-rip).\
On the right path, you can use [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) to dispel [Hyperlight Bomb](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357029/hyperlight-bomb). It doesn't apply it to the caster, but it does help your healer.\
I personally recommend the left path, since it's easier on the tank and fast.

The [Market Peacekeeper](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=179840/market-peacekeeper)s will charge to targets outside of their melee distance with [Quelling Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=355637/quelling-strike), which deals a fair amount of damage. Stay close to all peacekeepers in the pack to avoid getting charged at. They will also cast [Phalanx Field](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=355640/phalanx-field), which reduces *magic* damage and reflects spells. Not a big deal for us specifically, but very bad for the group as a whole. Use [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) to stop the cast. The [Veteran Sparkcaster](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=179841/veteran-sparkcaster)s will cast [Scintillate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=355641/scintillate), which deals a lot of damage and applies a debuff increases the damage of subsequent casts, and [Hyperlight Salvo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=355642/hyperlight-salvo), which will very quickly kill its target on medium-to-high keys. Both are interruptable, but I would recommend holding your [Spear Hand Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116705/spear-hand-strike) for the salvo, since it deals way more damage and you can outlast the scintillate with defensive spells. Additionally, you can remove your own scintillate debuff with [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic). If you're lucky, this will also reduce the damage of the next hit.

During The Grand Menagerie encounter, you can use [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) to clear [Achillite](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176555/achillite)'s [Purification Protocol](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=349954/purification-protocol), so that your healer can dispel the other one. You can use [Tiger's Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) to break [Venza](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176705/venza-goldfuse)'s [Chains of Damnation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=350101/chains-of-damnation) immediately, removing the need to kill the chains. Depending on timers, it's possible to break every chain, but that seems to be uncommon.

When you're in the mail room, keep an eye on the [Defective Sorter](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176396/defective-sorter)s. You can stop their [Open Cage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347721/open-cage) ability with [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) to prevent the annoying stuns from the chickens running around.

Not much to say about [P.O.S.T. Master](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=175646/p-o-s-t-master), just clear your [Hazardous Liquids](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=346286/hazardous-liquids) debuff with [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122783/diffuse-magic) and use your mobility for the [Unstable Goods](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=346947/unstable-goods).

On the gauntlet in Myza's Oasis, you'll get two waves of adds before the boss himself activates. The [Disruptive Patron](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176565/disruptive-patron) deserves special mention for his [Teleport](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=353783/teleport) ability, which you can stop with the old [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) to keep him stacked.\
Additionally, and I'm mentioning this because I just constantly see this happening on every level of key: *you are supposed to maintain your damage buff throughout the fight with [Zo'gron](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176563/zogron).* At the end of his [Suppression Spark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=355438/suppression-spark) casts, there will be one additional set of musical notes that will extend your damage buff for another 50(?) seconds, long enough to last until the next cast. You're supposed to use the instrument ability to avoid the splash damage at the end (each dps charging out of the middle, the tank jumping up, and the healer sitting pretty on the drums).

While fighting [So'azmi](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=175806/soazmi), you can preplace your [Transcendence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101643/transcendence) far away from where you'll be engaging the boss. With some luck, you can use it to cross the [Shuri](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347610/shuri) ring or get back to the boss to interrupt his [Double Technique](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357188/double-technique). Remember that you can dodge his [Quickblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347623/quickblade) by just strafing as he casts it, it has a very small hitbox.

## Tazavesh: So'leah's Gambit

We have a good toolkit to deal with the [Murkbrine Scalebinder](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=178141/murkbrine-scalebinder)s' [Invigorating Fish Stick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=355132/invigorating-fish-stick) with [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace). I'd recommend keeping one Scalebinder targeted whenever possible, to immediately stop its cast. You can also use [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) when the murlocs run away in fear, to prevent them from running into other packs.

While fighting [Hylbrande](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=175663/hylbrande), his summoned [Vault Purifier](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=176551/vault-purifier)s will cast [Empowered Defense](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=346980/empowered-defense). This reduces damage taken by the boss by 50%, and this will continue as long as you can see the energy beam connecting the boss and the add. Make sure to stop it with [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace).

During the Boralus Harbor trash, specifically the [Burly Deckhand](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180015/burly-deckhand)s, try to always stay further away from them than your tank. Their [Haymaker](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=368662/haymaker) ability will hit the closest target if the tank cannot be reached, and it's a common pack to be kited. This ability can easily be a oneshot if combined with [Super Saison](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=356133/super-saison) and/or [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228318/enrage#comments).

[Timecap'n Hooktail](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=175546/timecapn-hooktail) is notorious as a melee-unfriendly boss, but it's not so bad if you handle it right. When the adds spawn, check which one is fixated on you and bait it to the front of the boss with the other adds so the tank can hit all of them with [Infinite Breath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347149/infinite-breath). You can help out with [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) to keep them close together if needed.\
When you or anyone else gets hit by [Anchor Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=352345/anchor-shot), use [Tiger's Lust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116841/tigers-lust) to mitigate the drag effect. When the boss turns or moves, she'll cast [Hook Swipe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347151/hook-swipe) if anyone is behind her for more than a second or so, but this can be dodged by side-stepping.

In the last room, the [Devoted Accomplice](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180432/devoted-accomplice)s and [Focused Ritualist](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180431/focused-ritualist)s have some very dangerous abilities. Make sure you use all of your available tools ([Spear Hand Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116705/spear-hand-strike), [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis) and [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace)) to prevent any casts of [Reinvigorate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357284/reinvigorate) and [Unstable Rift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357260/unstable-rift). The [Hyperlight Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=354297/hyperlight-bolt) isn't that important, you'll want to save your crowd control spells for the other two. While fighting the [Adorned Starseer](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180429/adorned-starseer), remember that [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) can quickly kill a [Wandering Pulsar](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180433/wandering-pulsar).

You can use [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) and [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119381/leg-sweep) to keep [So'leah](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=177269/soleah)'s [So' Cartel Assassin](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=177716/so-cartel-assassin)s close to you. If they get a cast of [Shuriken Blitz](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=351119/shuriken-blitz) off, you're most likely wiping.\
While [So'leah](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=177269/soleah) is preparing to cast [Hyperlight Jolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=350875/hyperlight-jolt), be careful: she occasionally still melee hits anyone within range, which can easily kill you if the tank is already standing close to a relic. We have fantastic mobility, so be ready to fly to the other side of the room to hit faraway relics with the [Jolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=350875/hyperlight-jolt) on later casts.
