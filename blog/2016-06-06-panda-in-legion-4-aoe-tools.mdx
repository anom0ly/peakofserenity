Let's just list how many AOE tools we have access to. For comparison, in WOD we have [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) doing pitiful damage, [RJW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=116847/rushing-jade-wind) being unplayable in most tiers because competing with stronger talents, [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst), the infamous [Chi Torpedo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115008/chi-torpedo) and then some passive cleave on [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)/[Chi Explosion](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152174).

In Legion, we have:

- [Rushing Jade Wind](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=116847/rushing-jade-wind) tuned to be relevant; does more than 150% of
  [BOK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)'s damage single target, even with artifact traits its strong single target
- [Whirling dragon punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) gives burst AoE after each [Fist of fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury).
- [Fist of fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) deals 100% of its damage to all targets, making it crazy burst AoE damage.
- [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) remains
- [Strike of the windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) is almost as strong as a [Fist of fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury), except its instant. Sure feels like our [cataclysm](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152108/cataclysm) and gives us crazy threat when we deal 1 million damage instantly to 10 targets.
- [Spinning Crane Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) has been reworked, with the target tagging system, making it very relevant when a few targets are tagged with [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire); while [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is up, it's quite easy to stack up.
- [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196745/chi-orbit) now changed to deal AoE damage; Hinalover thinks there'll be some diminishing returns mechanics beyond a number of targets, still AOE/Cleave.

All of those AOE tools are, in design, usable. So, when adds come up, what do I do?

- Here we face kind of a conflict. [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) can be precast prior to adds spawn so that one is fine.
- [Fist of fury](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury), [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord), [WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) are part of the single target rotation so they'll kinda fit nicely. [WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) requires you to maybe toss in a [RSK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) at some point, considering haste will make its cooldown pretty short so it won't be obvious for most players to have it on cooldown when adds spawn.
- What about [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)? [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) scales on [tiger palm](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm), [RSK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [BOK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) tagging targets. Notice how most of those are not mentioned earlier.
- [Ascension](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115396/ascension) will grant you access to 6 chi when adds spawn, covering [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)/[SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord)/[WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). [Energizing elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) will grant you access to 9 chi, covering [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)/[SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord)/[WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) and one [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick). Issue is, where do you stack up [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick), to what extent?

Of course you keep in mind that adds/AoE in World of Warcraft raiding is often very volatile. That's why Warriors/Warlocks were able to shine most of WOD there: it's way more relevant to be able to do crazy AoE for first few seconds of adds than to be able to set up for damaging them 8 seconds later. Also, from one group to another, adds may die at very different speeds.

So what does this mean for young Windwalkers?

- Adds are going to live 2-5 seconds: [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord)/[FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)/[WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) asap, precast [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst)/[RSK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)
- Adds are going to live 5-15 seconds and are less than 8: [SOTW](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord)/[FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)/[WDP](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) then go crazy on [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)
- Adds are 10+ and live 7+ seconds: go ham on [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)?

This of course is not precise nor definite yet but the right choice will be very hard to make, depending on how strong [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) is. If you start setting up for [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) and adds die before you get rewarded, it'll be extremely frustrating.

TL;DR: Supposedly we just underwent "pruning", which basically means tiger palm is gone and [Energizing elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288/energizing-elixir) replaces [energizing](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288) and [chi brews](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115399). On the other hand, we went from 1-2 AoE tools to 6 different relevant tools working for the same spots.

When first spoiled, [Whirling dragon punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) sounded like our crazy burst AoE mechanic after [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury). Now its just one of our many AoE tools. I think it may be VERY confusing to tell people what to do in which cases. Also, I fear either some of those buttons will end up irrelevant or we'll be too strong on AoE, too weak for single target, or imbalanced in some other way?
