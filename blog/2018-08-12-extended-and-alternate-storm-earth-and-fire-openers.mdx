**Welcome to opener hell.** This article is here to serve as a public scratchpad for all of my [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) opener testing, with which I had help from Slappa, Wizard Dog, Eliro, and Neeb. It features longer planned out versions of each opener found in the main guide, though they might be a bit long to memorize or actually execute without mechanics getting in the way. The talents for these openers are [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986), [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947), [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904), and [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175). Some of them apply to [Chi Wave](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098) as well, including both openers found on the main guide, and [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) is skippable if not talented in all of them. Also, a quick note on [Touch of Karma](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=122470), you can use it (and [Provoke](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115546)) at the same time as [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) if applicable, since they are off the global cooldown. If you just want a quick summary, here’s the **TL;DR:**

- If you’re just here to see the extended versions of those found in the main guide, just look at the sequences marked “**MAIN GUIDE**”.
- There are also marked sequences that [Chi Wave](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098) works fine for too. [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) is skippable if not talented in all sequences.
- For [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825), the recommended opener is theoretically very slimly behind another, but is likely the best in practice.
- For non-[Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825), there’s a few extremely close options, but a pretty easy choice for which is the go-to.
- All of these will probably change with traits, especially [Swift Roundhouse](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=277669).

####

#### A Word on Using [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) or [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) First

For anyone wondering about why to use [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) before [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639), or vice versa, here is a general explanation that is considered for every sequence below. Using [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) first makes you use waste a GCD inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window to cast [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639), so you lose a potential 10% damage on a cast. Using [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) first makes you waste a GCD of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) casting [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080), so you lose a potential 35% damage on a cast. In a vacuum, this would mean you always cast [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) first. However, if the extra cast inside [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080)'s 10% bonus damage outweighs the final GCD of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639)'s 35% bonus damage, it can be worth it to swap the order of the two. For example, if you could get an extra [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window, that would provide double the bonus damage of [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) at the very end of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639). Some non-[Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) opener options also had downtime towards the end of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639), so for those you don't lose anything at all by using [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) first. All the openers below took this into account, as you will see if you read on.

###

### [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) Openers

#####

##### Technically Optimal [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) Opener

> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)

*\*[Chi Wave](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098)/[Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) Agnostic\* - You use [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) here because there’s nothing better to do, not for the 1 chi. Works with either.*

This is the opener that I mapped out that would do the most damage, by a very slim margin. However, the timing for this is extremely tight, and seems unrealistic to reliably pull off after a bunch of testing. Namely, getting the second [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window completely depends on if you can get [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)'s channel to be under 3 seconds long. Theoretically, this only requires 3.33% haste with [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) active. Even using a /stopmacro [channeling:[Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)] macro (see the bottom of the main guide) to cast [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) right after will result in casting [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) later than [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) actually \*should\* finish (but still faster than manually watching for the 5th tick). As an example, ignoring [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825), and with 8.77% haste, [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)'s channel time should be 3.677 seconds long. In testing, even when binding the [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) macro to the scroll wheel to get the input in as quickly as possible, I was only able to get [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) to go off at a minimum of 3.854 seconds after [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) was cast. By messing with the spell queue window, I could get it down to 3.786 seconds, but this is still a far cry from 3.677 (and yes, that 0.1 second gap can and will mean the difference between [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) being in and out of the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window). So, with a bunch of haste as a band aid, or possibly with low ping or a low spell queue window, or some combination of those, this opener would win out. It’s also worth noting here that with a very fast, stable connection and no, low, or normal spell queue window (I tested each), I couldn’t consistently get this to actually work outside of one or two times, and sometimes the final [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) fell out of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) as well. I noticed a sort of lag on spells whenever I did [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) instead of the other way around. These inconsistencies lead me to list a “safer” version that’s not far behind, and is actually ahead if you don’t get 2 [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window.

##### MAIN GUIDE: More Consistent/Safer [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) Opener

> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428)  -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)

*\*[Chi Wave](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098)/[Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) Agnostic\* - You use [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) here because there’s nothing better to do, not for the 1 chi. Works with either.*

If you’re looking for the difference, it’s just swapping [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) and [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639). The downside to this opener is that you don’t have a chance of getting 2 [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) casts inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window. However, the punishment for not having perfect timing is much more lenient. Missing the last GCD inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window (i.e., having [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)'s channel last longer than 3 seconds) is now just missing out on a measly 10% of a [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) (and weirdly, without the strange timing delay that happened every time I tried [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080), I usually got this [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) inside [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) anyway. Something tells me the usual culprit, [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) spaghetti, is to blame here). Similarly, if you don’t get the last spell of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) quite in there, it’s also just a [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780). In the previous opener, this final GCD of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) was the last [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428), but the timing wasn’t quite as ridiculous as the second [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428). Still, if you were at all delayed in hitting each spell as it came up, you could realistically miss it (you could swap it with the [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) before it if you’re insistent on using [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) first for whatever reason). So compared to above, you mostly just lose the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window’s 10% bonus on one [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428), and don’t risk losing a [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) inside [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) if you don’t have quick timing. Personally, I like that it also has the very minor “feel” bonus of preserving the order of [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639), and it matches the non-lust opener that way. But you might not care about that.

##### Sidenote: Another (Worse) Approach Entirely

> (Prepull) [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428)-> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)

This is really just for posterity, but this was the [Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) opener I ended up with before Slappa tried out using [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) before anything else in search of a more energy efficient opener (you can note that the openers with [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) first barely cap energy). It more closely resembles the Legion style openers, with [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) in place of [Energizing Elixir](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115288). It doesn’t line up quite as smoothly, in that there are a few ~0.15 second gaps where you wait for [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) to come off CD before casting it, so you actually end up with 1 less spell inside [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) (which turns out to be a [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780)). You energy cap briefly in the middle, and also have to wait a half a second before the last [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780). Also, a bigger, unsolved problem with this is that [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) comes off CD as you’re hitting the second [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656), and using [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) first here would delay [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) a ton. You end up delaying [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) for ~4 seconds. I stopped looking at this at this point since the first two openers seemed like they would perform equally or better anyways, so I wouldn’t really recommend using this.

### Non-[Bloodlust](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=2825) Openers

#####

##### MAIN GUIDE: The “Best” One

> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

*\*[Chi Wave](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115098)/[Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) Agnostic\* - You use [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) here for the damage and to maintain mastery, not for the 1 chi. Works with either.*

All of the options listed here are very close in damage, with about a 1% difference between the best and worst. This one has the advantage of getting [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) on cooldown as early as possible. You use [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) first after [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) here to get energy regeneration going as quickly as possible, too. When testing with [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) first (inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window), you end up energy starved later in the sequence and miss [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) and [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) being inside [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639). The only other thing to really say about it is that the timing at the end for getting [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) and [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) in at the end is kind of tight, but shouldn’t be an issue if you’re just hitting everything as it comes up, and preferably use the /stopmacro [channeling:[Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656)] macro for [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175). This timing also applies to the rest of the openers below, but since this opener has two hits at the last second because of [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986), it would be hurt by missing the last cast a little more than the rest - just a note if you find yourself constantly missing out on [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986)/[Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) inside [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639).

##### Close Behind (or Technically, Ahead)

> (Prepull) [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

This actually gives extremely slightly more damage than the first sequence listed, even if only 1 of [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) 3 damage ticks fits inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window (which is usually what I got during testing). However, the damage difference is so miniscule (if you somehow get all of [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) inside [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080), ~0.5%) that getting [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) on cooldown sooner outweighs it. That’s why this one isn’t the one on the main guide.

##### Another Considered (But Slightly Worse) Option

> (Prepull) [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

This is slightly worse than the above openers in that it does slightly less damage, has slightly worse worse energy management, and also waits to use [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) for a few seconds. It’s still very close, but definitely behind, so I figured I’d throw it on here for the curious. One weird thing you might note about this sequence is that you end up fitting a [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window even though you have 1 chi and are ready to use [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) instead. The reason for this is that if you swap those and [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) first, you sit on capped energy for a while. This directly results in having to wait for energy for the last [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780), and missing out on getting one more [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) inside [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639). That far outweighs the 10% bonus on a [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) vs [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780).

##### A Note on the Above

> (Prepull) [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) -> [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Fists of Fury](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=113656) -> [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Rising Sun Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=107428) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Blackout Kick](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100784)

All this does is swap [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) and [Storm, Earth, and Fire](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=137639) to be in the more obvious order that the others follow. This is strictly worse than the above, but the difference is extremely minor. You miss out on a [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) and either 1 or 2 of [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=152175)'s hits, inside the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) window, depending on your timing/haste. That comes out to less than a single [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780)'s worth of damage lost. But, as an inferior version of an inferior version of the opener, try not to do this.

#### Post Script 8/23/18: [Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288)

No specific opener has been made for [Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288) for now, especially since big changes to our playstyle are on the horizon due to traits that will likely push us to use [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) instead. If you just want an idea of an opener to use with [Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288), you could just (use [Xuen](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123904) first, if applicable, then) build to 5 chi via a sequence such as [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) -> [Chi Burst](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=123986) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780), and then just proceed onwards from the [Touch of Death](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115080) of either opener as normal. So that building would just replace the [Fist of the White Tiger](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=261947) -> [Tiger Palm](http://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=100780) of the normal openers. Then you can just use [Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288) as you normally would later in the fight. This does delay the use of [Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288) by quite a bit, but if you're using [Energizing Elixir](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=115288) then you're probably in a dungeon where that would be less important anyways (it might even be on CD from a pre-boss trash pack). This admittedly probably isn't perfect, but it's likely very close to any slightly more optimal sequence. If any such sequence is brought up, I will edit this and put it here.
