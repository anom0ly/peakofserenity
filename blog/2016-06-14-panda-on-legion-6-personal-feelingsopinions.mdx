Since I've reached the end of what I originally wrote during alpha, I thought I'd finally toss out my personal opinions on the state of things and many of the changes. There is still plenty I want to write about on legion and WW in particular, do not worry.

I like many changes for WW in legion; I like that the [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) debuff is gone, I like that the [TP](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=125359) buff is gone. Those two were noob traps more than anything, misleading newcomers into thinking those [TP](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=125359) procs were any good, or misleading players into thinking rising sun should be used every 15 seconds when in most cases it should be kept on cooldown.

I'll miss [tiger eye brew](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116740); I really enjoyed it when it was kinda low stacks generation, with average 60% uptime maximum; I still enjoy it with [serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) to some extent. However, 60% was too much of a damage increase, making us hit like wet noodles outside of it, and like hurricanes during it.

I love new mastery. It's mostly intuitive since obviously it affects almost all your spells baseline, but that's fine; most classes get dull masteries anyways and don't complain. Also, this gives WW a damage amp that affects spells and not melee hits/chi orbit/other dull stuff the way RSK debuff used to, making those less relevant, which I prefer.

I'm kind of lost on [Whirling dragon punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). It looked like it was meant to be our burst AoE at first, but then [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)/[Strike of the windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) started becoming way way way stronger AoE tools, then [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) happened, and now [Whirling dragon punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is just a weaker tool with some condition on it. It looks cool, smooth and all though.

I want to test [serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) with current iteration, but with alpha/beta server lag it was hard, also when I tried testing it there was still the [RSK](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) bug (where it doubled the cooldown instead of halving it). I haven't spoken of it but I believe it has potential to be a very relevant spell for 2+ minutes fights. It'll be very skill intensive and unintuitive probably, since the right choice will most likely NOT be to smash one button per GCD, but to channel [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury)'s during it and sometimes to wait 0.3 seconds to hit [rising sun kick](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428) rather than instantly using [BoK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), however it looks pretty strong.

I love having crazy burst on opener. It's a radical change from live, but it feels good: [FoF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) finally feels like a cooldown, [TOD](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death) feels pretty great, [Strike of the windlord](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=205320/strike-of-the-windlord) gives us that "wow" button which you press and see huge results out of it instantly. Feels way more proactive than live version.

I dislike [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)'s changes because they make everything unclear about how to use spells, what to do on AoE, and it becomes a nightmare for AoE once [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is on cooldwon.\
If you are replacing [TEB](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116740) with [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire), then there is no reason to have main character deal reduced damage during [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) anymore. For WOD, each spirit dealt 70%/55% damage to avoid us abusing [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) on single target. However, this is not an issue anymore since we are going to use [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) on single target anyways in current iteration.

Please, pleaaaaase, pleaaaaaaaaase make [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) leave us at 100% damage and each clone deal 15% damage, adding up to 130% like [tiger eye brew](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116740) did, which is a very relevant damage amplification, tagging targets for [SCK](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) as easily, yet allowing less things to go wrong. It feels very bad when you have no idea where [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is going, if you feel like you're just nerfing your damage down for no reason.

Also, in pvp its easy to [ice nova](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=157997/ice-nova)/[mass entanglement](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=102359/mass-entanglement) the [SEF](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) spirits and deny a WW of 67% of his damage during burst. Dealing reduced damage because your closes are rooted would feel very bad for pvp players. At least at 100% / 15% / 15% it would feel like normal pets, feral wolves, stampede or something: you can CC them but the player himself keeps his damage.

I hope you've enjoyed these posts. There will certainly be more as we get more information and changes closer to Legion. If anyone wants a specific topic adressed, leave a comment on chi burst and I'll do my best!
