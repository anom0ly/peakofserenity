#### Bonus

Since I got a little bored in the middle of all this work, I decided to take a look at how the artifact traits increased damage. The first chart looks at the damage increase from 0 traits to totally full 101 traits, the second looks at how much % gain each trait is over the previous. Both graphs were done with the Mythic NH SimC profile, 300s fight length with 50% variance. The artifact was filled in following the recommended path in the [Artifact Guide](/tww/windwalker/).

The big jumps are, as to be expected, at the golden traits. Remember that these are done with the t19 tier bonuses, in a single target scenario, so things that augment [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428) are extra strong.
