On today's installment of *How to Train your Celestial,* we will be talking all about [Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) and [Invoke Chi Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane). We will hit on what each Celestial does, any fun interactions they have with our kit, how to pick the right one, what to do during each one, and how to decide which one is stronger.

**This guide has been updated for Patch 9.2** and is regularly updated when discussion, theorycrafting, or testing yields new information.

## What can my Celestial do?

[Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) and [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) both share a passive called [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath). This ability places a HoT on 6 different people within 10 yards of your [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) target while your Celestial is alive (one of these 6 HoTs will always go on the person you casted [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) on). This HoT is decently hard hitting, and also has a buff that increases all healing done to the target by 10%. This usually contributes a large part of your Celestial’s healing in raid, but due to the HoT having a short range, it doesn't find much use in Mythic+ unless your group is stacked.

[Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) has its own special ability called [SooB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343737/soothing-breath), which is just 3 half-power [SooMs](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) that channel from Yu'lon [herself](https://wow.gamepedia.com/Yu%27lon); you don't need to cast [SooM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) for her to cast it. You have the ability to direct who she is casting [SooB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) on the same way you would with [JSS](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115313/summon-jade-serpent-statue). This has very niche use cases and can generally be ignored.

[Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) also has its own special ability which converts certain damaging spells into healing through [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists). Additionally, it grants you a buff which reduces the cast time and mana cost of [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) by 33% each — this can stack up to 3 times, making it instant cast and cost no mana. The damaging abilities that Chi-Ji can convert to healing are [BoK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick), [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), and [SCK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick). It also indirectly converts [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) into healing through [TotM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery) when you use [BoK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick). Each time you use one of these abilities, it will proc 2 [Gusts of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists) heals that will randomly target injured players within 40 yards; this will no longer prioritize the closest ally. These [Gusts of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists) scale off of Intellect, Mastery and Versatility, and can also Crit, while also working with any [Gusts](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191894/gust-of-mists) duplication powers such as [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) and [Bonedust Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325216/bonedust-brew). [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) also will passively attack the boss for a decent amount of damage, as well as rendering you immune to movement impairing effects.

## How does my Celestial interact with my kit?

There are only a few interactions with other spells/talents to mention here.

- [Mist Wrap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap) extends [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath) by 1 second, but doesn't increase the healing buff.
- [RM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) does NOT interact with [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath)
- [FO](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=326860/fallen-order)'s [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist)s do not proc [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath)
- Casting [SooM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) focuses one of Yu'lon's [SooB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343737/soothing-breath) on that target.
- [TotM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery) [BoK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)s do produce [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists) healing events
- [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) and [Bonedust Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325216/bonedust-brew) duplicate Chi-Ji’s [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists)
- [WoO](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=310454/weapons-of-order) increases Mastery, thus increasing [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists) healing
- [Jade Bond](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=336773/jade-bond) doesn’t increase [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath)
- Chi-Ji’s [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists) does work with [Jade Bond](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=336773/jade-bond)’s reduction
- [replmist](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=336812/resplendent-mist) does work with Chi-Ji’s [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists)

## How do I know when to pick each Celestial?

Picking the right Celestial depends on a few factors, such as  “Can I be in melee?” and “Do I care about mana?”.

The first is fairly obvious, as in, if you can't (or don't like to) be in melee, then [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) will be pointless, as you have to proactively hit melee dps buttons to use him correctly. So if you can't be in melee, then don't take [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane).

The latter isn't as obvious, but is extremely important, as your aim during a Celestial cooldown is to cast a good amount of [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist)s which costs a sizable portion of your mana. This mana cost can be virtually dropped to 0 if you use [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) as his buff through using damaging spells will reduce the cost of [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) massively, while also replacing other expensive casts with low mana/free dps casts. There are things you can do to lower the mana usage during [Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) such as using [Mana Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea), calling for [Innervate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=29166/innervate), or using [Potion of Sacrificial Anima](https://www.wowhead.com/item=176811/potion-of-sacrificial-anima).

## How to play with each Celestial?

One core concept about Celestials is to cast it right before damage hits, as the button press to summon them does absolutely nothing. You also need to decide how far ahead/what you should do beforehand, which changes based on which Celestial you have selected.

### [Enveloping Breath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath)

The fundamentals of what to do during a Celestial remains the same either way: cast [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) to proc [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath). However, the way you accomplish this is different for both of them. Another core concept is to hard-cast [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) during this window, as **hard-casting is always faster than** [**SooM**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) **->** [**Env**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist). Now, for the most important [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) casting rules, these require on-the-fly brainpower, as you can't just reference frames for an answer.

- There should be at least 2 people within 10 yards of your [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) target
- These people don't have [EnvB](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/enveloping-breath) on them
- There is (or is about to be) damage to heal

If one of these conditions is not met, do not cast [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), and play as you normally would.

### [Invoke yu'lon, the Jade Sperent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent)

With Yu'lon, you will want to cast it about 2 GCDs before damage hits, and then precast an [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) so it finishes as damage hits. From here on out, you will just run through the normal healing rotation while replacing [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) casts with [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) if they meet the cast requirements.

### [Invoke Chiji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane)

With Chi-Ji you have some pre-planning happening here, since he does convert [TotM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery) [BoKs](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) and his [Gust of Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=347647/gust-of-mists) are duplicated by [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font)'s HoT. This means you'll want to enter Chi-Ji with a fully stacked [TotM](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery) and a fresh set of [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) HoTs out — this *does* mean cast [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) BEFORE damage. Your rotation will look something like this:
[Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) x3 -> [Essence Font](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) -> [Chi-Ji](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) -> [BoK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) -> [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist)
After this, you should try to get 3 stacks of Chi-Ji's buff before casting [Env](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), but if you need more healing, you should consider casting with lower stacks. The way you should get stacks follows a priority list of dps buttons that changes based on the situation:

##### Max mana savings/burst

1. [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)
2. [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) (if [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) isn't up and you have 0 stacks, you should [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) twice here)
3. [BoK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)

##### Constant healing

1. [RSK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)
2. [BoK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick)
3. [SCK](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)

##### Note: These do the same hps, the only difference is how the hps comes out.
