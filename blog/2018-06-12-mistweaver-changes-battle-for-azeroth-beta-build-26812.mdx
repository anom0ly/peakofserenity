Another week, another build.

### Changes This Build

- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) single target healing increased from 105% Spell power to 120% Spell power.
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) healing on [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) targets increased from 70% Spell power to 80% Spell power.
- [Uplifting Trance](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197206/uplifting-trance) removed.
- [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) now has 2 charges on an 8 second recharge.

### Speculation

Brought up in a [blue post late last week](https://us.battle.net/forums/en/wow/topic/20762158001?page=13#post-258), these changes are, succinctly, a quality of life improvement in raids and a great buff for Mythic plus and other small group content.

I touched on it in the last build's post, but I'll elaborate a bit more in this build. [Uplifting Trance](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197206/uplifting-trance), to be balanced, had to take some of the baseline power from [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) in order for an [Uplifting Trance](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197206/uplifting-trance)'d [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) to not heal for an absurd amount of health. This was doubly true in *Legion* thanks to the traits in the Sheilun that provided a more stable rate of procs with longer duration on [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist), as well as more instances of [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) through [Dancing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=199573/dancing-mists). However, with the scarcer amount of [Uplifting Trance](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197206/uplifting-trance) procs available to Mistweavers in *Battle for Azeroth*, the argument for this harsh trade off has become weaker. With the original intent of the buff, [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) interaction in group healing outside of being a standard HoT effect, being reintroduced into the game, its class fantasy component also dwindled. Its removal allows a regular [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) to be immediately impactful when its needed, which it definitely is now.

[Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) going ([back](https://wow.gamepedia.com/Pool_of_Mists)) to a charge system is amazing when you have any group wide burst inside dungeons, where [Essence Font](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=191837/essence-font) is hamstrung because of how its designed to lose bolts the fewer targets there are in range. Being able to pool these charges for incoming damage, or better yet use [Focused Thunder](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197895/focused-thunder) to push out two 30 second long [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist)s to the group back to back, is amazing for this type of content. In raids, this a simple quality of life fix that allows you to not be punished for finishing up a cast or channel as [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) comes off cooldown. Its a nice buffer that also gives some slight burst potential in any fight that has you heal a smaller group occasionally.
