Babs

For talents I ran each combination, ignoring any with [Ascension](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115396), [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit), [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst), and [Eye of the Tiger](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196607), since those are not really feasible. [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) is usable in single target, but its so definitely better than [Chi Wave](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098) in Multi-Target that it didn't matter and wouldn't change the sims. Most people stick with [Chi Wave](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098) for the flexibility as WW doesn't lack in AOE damage.

As I ran literally 200 different simulations, its too much to link all the results so I compiled a chart for you to see the top option for all combinations of 1-10 targets and various fight lengths. This assumes [Chi Wave](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098) and [Energizing Elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288), the only variables are the final 2 tiers of talents and whether or not you have [DHC](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) or not.

[Click Here for Chart](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i-IynldXChUh7pm8TItF33m9TZcyBx0ht_w-0HfQRjo/edit?usp=sharing)

This shows that, if you do not have [DHC](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover), [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is the way to go in nearly all situations. [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904) is most useful on very very short fights, and [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847) beats [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) at 5 targets and above. Even if you do have [DHC](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover), [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) beats out [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) at high targets and prolonged fight times.

It should be noted that even in all the sims less than 5 targets, Serenity and Whirling Dragon Punch, both with and without Drinking Horn Cover, were pretty close to one another, sometimes just outside the margin of error. So if you prefer one play style over another, don't feel like you're drastically hindering your performance.

When using [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), currently it looks like using it as close to on cooldown as possible due to the change to the cooldown workings. I'll spend more time on this in a few days once everything settles down.

For Tier sets I wanted to look at a few things. The benefit of the bonus at different gear levels, as well as roughly how many ilevels the bonus was worth. I have never done the bonus vs ilvl comparison, so its always possible that I messed it up.

[Tier Bonus - Serenity](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Tier-Bonus-Serenity.html)  |   [Tier Bonus - WDP](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Tier-Bonus-WDP.html)

As you can see, [the 2pc bonus](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=211427/item-monk-t19-windwalker-2p-bonus) is more useful for [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173), which makes sense as it makes it much easier to fit a 3rd [Rising Sun Kick](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=107428) into the duration.

[Tier Bonus vs 2pc](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Tier-Bonus-Vs-Ilvl.html)

The item level changes in that sim are for all gear, so each ilvl is roughly equal to 1 more average ilvl across your equipped gear. +1 average ilvl is equal to one piece going up about 15 ilvl. This means that the 2pc bonus is worth a little more than +3 average ilvl, or +45 ilevels gained across the gear you're upgrading. This means that you'd have to gain roughly 45 ilvl between the items that you'd use in order to justify breaking up the 2pc bonus.

[Tier Bonus vs 4pc](http://www.walkingthewind.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Tier-Bonus-Vs-ilvl-4pc.html)

This works the same way as the 2pc above, in order to justify dropping the 4pc bonus, [bonus](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=211430/item-monk-t19-windwalker-4p-bonus), you need to gain +5 average ilvl, or 75 ilevels total across all gear.

**Again, this is the first time I have ever done, or thought about this situation, so I am/was making it up as I go.**

To assess the power of [Cenedril, Reflector of Hatred](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137019), add 30k DPS to the simulation's DPS, this is equal to the bonus provided to the cloaks' damage if used on cooldown. SimC is also not using [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115080) a second time when using [Hidden Master's Forbidden Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137057), so to assess that legendary, add 17.3k dps to all sims 2 targets and above.

As  you can see [DHC](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover) is incredibly strong now. However, nothing stops [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) and [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847) at 5 targets and higher, its just too powerful. If you have [DHC](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137097/drinking-horn-cover), you should use it until you start using [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847).

The vast majority of legendaries are relatively close to one another, so there is a wide pool of options.

There are obviously many more things that changed slightly in 7.1.5. I ran all the other information on the guides and nothing has changed enough to merit further changes to the guides. The only thing that remains is Relics vs Item Level, which by early estimation, saw the weight of all relics drop by 1 lvl in single target and 2-3 in AOE, and stat weights and gaps. Relic order is very unlikley to change except with the 2pc bonus which will push [Rising Winds](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195263/rising-winds) ahead of [Fists of the Wind](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=195291/fists-of-the-wind) for single target.
