#### Part 3: Talents

##### Tier 1: Damage/Healing

This tier is one of the talent tiers that require very little changes. Some additional flavor could be added, such as making [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) an instant cast, allowing [Eye of the Tiger](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196607) to be on multiple targets, or some way for [Chi Wave](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098) to continue to bounce through keeping up the Mastery bonus. However, I think the biggest thing that could be done for this tier is to take [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) and move it into the spot where [Eye of the Tiger](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196607) exists currently. This keeps with the "Chi" motif of this tier, as well as provide a passive damage talent. The orb explosions could have small range heal added to it, similar to the other talents.

This would give [Chi Wave](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115098) as the controllable single target, [Chi Burst](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=123986/chi-burst) as the controllable AOE, and [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) as the passive choice.

##### Tier 2: Movement

This tier is perfect, make no changes.

##### Tier 3: Resources

Contrary to what it may have seemed like, this tier is the one that was the least flexible all expansion. The dominance of [Energizing Elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288) is something that was pointed out very early in the Alpha/Beta process. The fact that it can generate nearly a dozen Chi worth of resources per minute, when the next best option maxes out 4, is INSANE. Futher, [Ascension](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115396) was nerfed during the Alpha/Beta phase from 15% down to 10% when, at the time, it was no where near being considered as a useful talent.

The biggest thing that can be done to help balance this tier is to reduce the resource gain from [Energizing Elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288). If it were to simple refill our Energy, it would provide 4 Chi worth of resources (100 energy if [Inner Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195243/inner-peace) is gone) on demand. This would balance with [Power Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121817/power-strikes) providing 4 Chi, but without as much control. It could also be changed to simply provide maximum Chi, which would mean 5 Chi, on demand, every minute. Either of these changes would allow [Energizing Elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288) to be more balanced with the rest of the tier, and significantly easier for players to use, rather than just resigning yourself to wasting some of the resources it provides. Since its "Energizing" Elixir, I think having it fill out energy would be the best choice.

[Ascension](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115396) would likely need to be buffed back from 10% to 15% to be considered.

##### Tier 4: Crowd Control

This tier is just fine, no changes.

##### Tier 5: Defensives

This tier is also perfect, although the defensives could use to be buffed to narrow the gap between what some other classes (see: Rogues) have.

##### Tier 6: Damage

For much of the expansion, this tier has been dominated by [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740), with [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904) having a very small stint being useful in very specific situations. [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847) has always seemed like it has potential, but that potential was never realized, even seeing its usefulness nerfed when it wasn't useful at all. The main problem with this tier is that the way that [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) is designed, it will either be the run away favorite, or never used.

To solve this, I resubmit my suggestion to combine [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740) with our Mastery from my [Stats Wishlist](/2017/11/04/battle-for-azeroth-windwalker-wishlist-stats/). This would then free up a slot in this tier for something useful. [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904) fits a sustained single target or cleave niche, [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847) can be buffed to provide [Mark of the Crane](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane) stacks in a much more efficient way, which I pointed out as a problem previously, with increased damage for sustained AOE, and the new ability can be designed for burst single target. As an outside suggestion, not necessarily one I'd prefer, [Touch of Death](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115080/touch-of-death), combined with [Gale Burst](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=195399/gale-burst), as I suggested in my [Ability Wishlist](/2017/11/04/battle-for-azeroth-windwalker-wishlist-abilities/), could be made into a talent to replace [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740), giving Windwalkers a strong burst single target talent when needed without Blizzard needing to design something new for that slot.

[Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904) could be made into a permanent guardian when talented. I know I'd certainly go get myself a [XuFu](http://www.wowhead.com/npc=71942/xu-fu-cub-of-xuen) to match, but Windwalkers don't really need one more thing to manage.

##### Tier 7: Damage

This tier hasn't been without its issues, seemingly going back and forth between whether [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) or [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) is the best talent, with [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) pretty much being useless all the time. The big thing that this tier needs is direction. [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is seemingly designed to fill the role of sustained AOE, with [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) appearing to focus more on burst damage, and [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) being focused on looking cool when in town or giving a talent for [Soul of the Grandmaster](https://ptr.wowhead.com/item=151643?bonus=1502:3530) to use.

If [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is going to stick with the role of sustained AOE, then its likely that its damage should be improved a smidge, although mechanically it should remain the same. [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) as a burst AOE/single target cooldown works just fine, although it would be nice to have the CD reduction back to snapshotting as that felt like the way it was supposed to be before it was "fixed".

This leaves me with the 3rd slot, once [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) is moved to the first tier to join the other "Chi" talents. As reluctant as I am to say this, and I MUCH prefer the suggestion that comes after this, I feel that we should see the return of Chi Explosion. HOWEVER, it needs to be strong (see: weak) enough, that it is not better than [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) for sustained AOE, nor better than [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) for burst. It should be focused on being the flexible talent for fights with both single target and AOE, while not excelling in either. [Whirling Dragon Punch](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) can be adjusted to benefit from [Mark of the Crane](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane), so that it gets stronger with more AOE targets, furthering its strength as an AOE talent. [Serenity](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=152173) could be adjusted so that it focuses more on single target, although it being a burst cooldown should be fine without tweaks.

My favorite idea for this tier of talents came from a very humorous discussion in Discord with several members of the community. This idea was that the 3rd slot becomes "Chi-mehameha Wave" modeled after [Goku's Kamehameha Wave from Dragon Ball](https://youtu.be/Z0EvVvMmWcU?t=222). This would be a Chi "finisher" that used all available Chi, and gained power based on Chi spent, and sends forth a ball of Chi toward the target, damaging all enemies in its path. To further this homage to Dragon Ball, the base spell could be instant cast, and cost 1 Chi, further use of more Chi requires additional channel time, maybe 0.5s per Chi spent, up to the 5 Chi maximum, similar to how Goku spends time "channeling" his energy into his signature attack. Similarly, it could be modeled after his [ultimate ability of Spirit Bomb](https://youtu.be/ngpPW_Js00U?t=116), calling forth ball of Chi from the sky down onto the enemy, with the mechanic of increasing Chi spent by channeling longer, and the ball gaining size, damage, and AOE radius with more Chi channeled into it. This talent could/would replace Blackout Kick as it could be used to dump small amounts of Chi as an instant cast. As much as this may seem like a joke talent, it could absolutely be made into a feasible talent that fits the Monk's theme while being visually and mechanically interesting.

##### Talent TL:DR

- Tier 1 - [Chi Orbit](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196743/chi-orbit) here in place of [Eye of the Tiger](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196607)
- Tier 2 - NO TOUCHIE
- Tier 3 - Nerf [Energizing Elixir](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=115288), fills energy bar OR Chi, not both
- Tier 4 - NO TOUCHIE
- Tier 5 - Small buffs to be closer to other defensives (or nerf Rogues)
- Tier 6 - Remove [Hit Combo](http://legion.wowhead.com/spell=196740), [Rushing Jade Wind](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847) for sustained AOE, [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904) for extended cleave/single target, new ability for burst single target (Touch of Death?)
- Tier 7 - Define their roles, add Chi based "finisher" modeled after old Chi Explosion, or Goku's Kamehameha Wave or Spirit Bomb
