Midnight's second patch is bringing nothing in the way of changes, but it is adding a new raid boss with good loot and an additional "talent" tree in the Omnium Folio for some more time-gated borrowed power... since we needed more.

## What's Changed

Nothing

## What's New

#### Sporefall + Rotmire

The new boss, Rotmire drops Heroic pieces at 285 and Mythic pieces at 298. This means that nearly everything it drops is probably Best-in-Slot:

- [Festerbloom Crown](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=268283/festerbloom-crown?bonus=13334:13787) - Get this and Catalyze it
- [Sash of the Putrid Giant](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=268286/sash-of-the-putrid-giant?bonus=13334:13787) - Get this and sim if you should Catalyze it
- [Rotmire's Sporeheart](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=268291/rotmires-sporeheart?bonus=13334:13787) - Good secondary stats and 298 item level. The cantrip is designed for healers, but instant Vivify should trigger this and a shield that deals additional damage is better than no shield at all.
- [Sporecaller's Blooming Loop](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=268290/sporecallers-blooming-loop?bonus=13334:13787) - Good secondary stats and 298 item level. This has a cantrip for DPS, so this is a clear Best-in-Slot ring to be paired with [Loa Worshiper's Band](https://www.wowhead.com/item=251513/loa-worshipers-band?bonus=12214:8960:12497:12066:13622:13667) or [Eye of Midnight](https://www.wowhead.com/item=249920/eye-of-midnight?bonus=13668) if you are using [Arcanoweave Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240167/arcanoweave-lining)
- [Sporelord's Mycelial Insignia](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/item=268292/sporelords-mycelial-insignia?bonus=13334:13787) - The only piece that isn't a slam dunk. Since trinkets like [Gaze of the Alnseer](https://www.wowhead.com/item=249343/gaze-of-the-alnseer?bonus=12806:13335) and [Algeth'ar Puzzle Box](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193701/algethar-puzzle-box?bonus=657:12052:5884:10390:13446:12053) can be upgraded to 298 with [Ascendant Voidcore](https://www.wowhead.com/item=268552/ascendant-voidcore), the strength of this trinket is on-par with [Gaze of the Alnseer](https://www.wowhead.com/item=249343/gaze-of-the-alnseer?bonus=12806:13335) for the passive trinket slot.

Rotmire is a relatively simple boss with add spawns and mechanics that play to a Windwalker's strength perfectly. Enjoy feeling like an all-star and blasting the adds if that's what you're into.

#### Omnium Folio

A time-gated, weekly unlocked, "talent" tree, this provides some more borrowed power to slowly nerf the current raids. Here's the current recommendation:

- Week 1 – 1st Row
  - (Default) [Rune of Void-Touched Orbs](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279596/rune-of-void-touched-orbs) - for ST and low-target cleave (Raid and Mythic+ bosses)
  - [Rune of Unleashed Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279599/rune-of-unleashed-fire) - for +4 target AOE (Mythic+ trash and raid padding)
- Week 2 – 2nd Row
  - [Rune of Self-Mending](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279603/rune-of-self-mending) if you want pseudo-Leech
  - (Default) [Rune of Void-Tainted Shell](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279604/rune-of-void-tainted-shell) - prevented damage is better than healed damage
  - [Rune of Lynxlike Reflexes](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279605/rune-of-lynxlike-reflexes) - if you are racing, I guess
- Week 3 – 3rd Row
  - [Rune of Lingering](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1287555/rune-of-lingering)
- Week 4 – 4th Row
  - (Default) [Rune of Critical Power](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279609/rune-of-critical-power) – stacking Crit is generally more useful
  - [Rune of Burning Haste](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279610/rune-of-burning-haste) or [Rune of Masterful Cunning](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279612/rune-of-masterful-cunning) - if they sim better
- Week 5 – 5th Row
  - [Rune of Overload](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=1279614/rune-of-overload)

The 1st, 4th, and 5th Rows are best simmed for your character as there may be variations.
