It's that time again: another week bringing another beta build. Unlike [last week](/2018/06/12/brewmaster-changes-in-battle-for-azeroth-build-26812/), Brewmaster's adjustments are back to being small tuning tweaks rather than sweeping changes

## What's Changed:

- [Guard](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115295/guard) has had its scaling coefficient buffed from 5 to 7.5.
- Threat modifiers for all tanks have been changed to +350% (up from +300%).
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670) has had its buff from last week reverted (back to 105% ap from 120%).
- Azerite traits were shuffled around on gear and some previously datamined traits (like [Sweep the Leg](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=280182)) are now available to be chosen.

  ![](/img/uploads/2018/06/WowB_2018-06-19_20-01-56.jpg)

  *An ilvl 325 piece of Azerite gear that now has Sweep the Leg (and two other generic traits not available until today)*

  ## What Does It Mean? Feelycraft

This build's changes look like they may be the start of Blizzard focusing more on tuning. More or less every tank specialization received some numbers tweaks to mitigation and self healing in this update. To that end, it's good to see [Guard](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115295/guard) receiving a fairly substantial **50%** buff, though early math still suggests that it will need an even greater one just to match [High Tolerance](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=196737/high-tolerance) in effective damage reduction (even if the two spells are meant to fill different niches). On the other hand, [Guard](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115295/guard) will be scaling up incredibly fast, which we can currently see through clever use of azerite traits and trinket procs. Properly applied, [Guard](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115295/guard)'s shield size can already jump from roughly [34%](https://puu.sh/AIsc0.jpg) of a player's max hp to over [55%](https://puu.sh/AIs5G.jpg)! While the odds of lining up enough trinket procs, azerite traits and buffs to actually get shields that relatively large are low, the fact that it's possible highlights just how potent [Guard](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115295/guard) can and likely will become as Battle for Azeroth continues barring additional tuning.

Outside of ability tuning, the threat modifier for all tanks was brought back to the level it was a few beta builds ago. While this change doesn't impact Brewmaster as much as the other tanks--[Dave](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115315/summon-black-ox-statue) works wonders for holding threat for us, after all--it will be more relevant if you find yourself tanking content with a dps player that substantially outgears you. As for Azerite traits, it's good to see a bit more variety being brought in, though some of the traits remain as questionable as ever (which we'll discuss as launch approaches).

Long story short: it's good to see [Guard](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115295/guard) getting some much-needed attention; now to keep our fingers crossed for a change to [Spitfire](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=242580/spitfire).
