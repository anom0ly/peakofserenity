**Yes... I reused the previous header, its just still applicable.** To stem the tide of questions, here's what you need to know about Windwalker in 9.0.5. I'll update this page as things get changed, if they do.

## Changes

- [Xuen's Battlegear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=337481/xuens-battlegear) - [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) critical strike chance increased by 50% (was 30%) and Fists of Fury cooldown reduced by 5 seconds (was 2.5 seconds).
- [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) is now vulnerable to Hibernate, Polymorph and Fear crowd-control effects.
- [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) is now affected by Mastery: Combo Strikes.
- Fixed an issue that caused [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire)’s cast to sometimes break nearby enemies’ Stealth.
- Fixed an issue that caused [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) to be able to target enemies in Stealth.
- Fixed an issue that caused [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) to grant an additional stack of [Mark of the Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane) when [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) is active.
- Fixed an issue that allowed [Mark of the Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane) to be maintained permanently at maximum stacks while fighting a single target.
- Fixed an issue that could prevent [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) spirits from using [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury), [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), and [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch).
- Fixed an issue that allowed [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire) spirits to continue channeling [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) after the Monk's [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) channel ended.
- Fixed an issue that could cause [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)'s damage to not be properly increased by [Mark of the Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane) if targets die with the Monk's mark active.

## What They Mean

#### Damage Loss

> There will be damage loss. I talked about it in [my last article](/2021/03/02/ptr-bug-fixes-bug-fix-good-bug-fix-bad/), and its likely not to be as bad as I mathed it to be, but there will still be damage loss.

#### Mark of the Crane

> You won't be able to maintain your stacks of [Mark of the Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane) between packs, you'll have to rebuild them at the start of pulls.

#### Covenants

> We're not changing Covenants. Kyrian will still be the recommended go-to Covenant. There are tons of memes and ideas out there about other ideas, but they'll take weeks to sort through and test, so Kyrian is still the best.

#### Legendaries

> We're not changing Legendary. [Invoker's Delight](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337298/invokers-delight) will still be the best for nearly all situations. [Xuen's Battlegear](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=337481/xuens-battlegear) moves up to 2nd place for all content.

#### ST vs AOE

> With the fact that you will have more [Mark of the Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228287/mark-of-the-crane) stacks than you have targets becoming a lot more rare, this shifts when you switch to the AOE priority. With this change it looks like this:
>
> - Drop [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) from your priority at at least 2 targets and at least 2 stacks.
> - Drop [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) from your priority at at least 3 targets and at least 1 stack.
> - If you're not using [Calculated Strikes](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=336526/calculated-strikes) then add 1 target to the above recommendations.

#### [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) Clipping

> There's almost no change to how we handle [Weapons of Order](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=310454/weapons-of-order) and [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury). From now on, whenever you are inside [Weapons of Order](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=310454/weapons-of-order) (regardless of [Storm, Earth, and Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=137639/storm-earth-and-fire)) you'll change what you do based on how much haste that you have.
>
> - - If you have 50% haste or higher then you'll full channel [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) when inside [Weapons of Order](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=310454/weapons-of-order) using a sequence like this:
>     - [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) > [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) (Full Channel) > [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) > [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)
>   - If you have less than 50% haste then you only cast [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) to enable [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) clipping it with the next GCD; using a sequence like this:
>     - [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) > [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) (Clipped) > [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) > [Expel Harm](https://shadowlands.wowhead.com/spell=322101/expel-harm) > [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) > [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)> [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) > [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) > [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) > [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick)
>   - If there are at least 3 targets then you only cast [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) to enable [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) clipping it with the next GCD and focus on getting out as many [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) as you can, just like you do now.
