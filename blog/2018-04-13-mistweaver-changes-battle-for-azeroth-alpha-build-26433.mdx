A new alpha build for Battle for Azeroth is upon us, bringing castable [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist) back, the removal of [Effuse](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116694/effuse), and a big change to [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify). Let's dig right in!

**Until databases are updated, the links to spells will display the previous build's tooltips.**

### Added/Changed This Build

- [Effuse](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116694/effuse) is removed!
- [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) has changed to be a single target heal that heals for 100% Spell power that also heals all targets with [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) active for 60% Spell power.
  - Uplifting Trance buffs all healing done by [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify), the main target heal as well as the [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) cleave.
  - Mana cost reduced from 4% mana to 3.2% mana.
- [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist) is back to its *Mists of Pandaria*/*Warlords of Draenor* roots, changed to an active channel that heals for about 47% Spell power 8 times. Haste increases the rate at which [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist) heals, but does not add more ticks per channel.
  - Makes all casts of [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) or [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) instant on the target.
    - There is a bug where if you're moving while channeling [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist) with the [Mist Wrap](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap) talent, [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) and [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) will not be cast, with the error message saying you cannot cast that spell while moving.
  - Activating [Thunder Focus Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea) does not interrupt this channel.
- [Summon Jade Serpent Statue](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115313/summon-jade-serpent-statue) is changed to accommodate the [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist) reversion, healing for a maximum of 30 ticks of about 16.3% Spell power per tick on the last target you used [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist) on, continuing to channel after your cast ends. Haste increases the rate at which this heals, but does not add extra ticks.
- [Thunder Focus Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea), when used to empower [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), instantly heals the target for 130% Spell power.
  - This heal falls under [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), and as such isn't buffed by itself.
- [Mystic Touch](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=8647/mystic-touch) was added a couple builds ago, but to summarize, each enemy hit with an attack increases Physical damage taken by 5% for 60 seconds, refreshing on each new hit. This is a Monk-wide passive.
- [Mana Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) is now on the global cooldown, but now lasts for 12 seconds.
- [Invoke Chi-Ji](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=198664/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) healing increased from 22% Spell power per hit to 30% Spell power per hit.
- [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) now amplifies its own healing by 30% (40% if [Mist Wrap](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197900/mist-wrap) is talented). This is likely intentional as it had to be added to the aura that it affects.

As a reminder, all Spell power coefficients were lowered along with the iLvL squish. For reference, [Life Cocoon](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon) absorbs for 700% Spell power and [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) heals for about 56% Spell power per tick.

###

### What These Changes Mean

These changes bring Mistweavers back to (some of) our roots. [Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) is now a combination of Surging Mist/[Effuse](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116694/effuse) and Uplift, now that it (again) heals all targets with [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) on them, as well as a targeted ally. No longer being tied to chi, this also gives a lot more power and flexibility to the burst potential of Mistweavers in dungeons, allowing [Focused Thunder](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=197895/focused-thunder) to apply [Renewing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) to 3 of your party members, and [Soothing Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=209525/soothing-mist)-[Vivify](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) to quickly burst up the group.

The [Thunder Focus Tea](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea) empowerment of [Enveloping Mist](https://bfa.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) now gives it *some* value as a more reactionary button instead of being a bad call in most circumstances in *Legion*.

One thing that seemingly didn't make it into this Alpha build were talent changes, which I'm excited to see with the return of this playstyle.
