The *Shadowlands* prepatch hit live servers this week, and **emallson** and **Anomoly** are here to talk about how the launch went! Before getting into that, however, they address criticism from a *World of Warcraft* designer about the role of theorycrafters and class discords in promoting numbers-focused "buff/nerf discourse" among the playerbase.

You can find both of our glorious co-hosts on Twitter at [@emallson](https://twitter.com/emallson) and [@Anom_MW](https://twitter.com/Anom_MW).
