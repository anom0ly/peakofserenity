The release of Patch 10.1 has been announced! Release is May 2nd, with Season 2 PvE & PvP content releasing one week later on May 9th. This week on the podcast, emallson and Anomoly look at the current state of dungeons on the PTR, the new affixes (emallson is back for ROUND TWO of Entangling Chat!), the (buggy) raid testing, and more.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/J3ocWFKhR9w) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EDT over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
