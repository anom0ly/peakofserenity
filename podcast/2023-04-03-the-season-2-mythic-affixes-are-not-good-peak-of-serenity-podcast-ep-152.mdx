This weekend, the new Mythic+ affixes were up on PTR for testing. They, uh, well...they're certainly affixes. This week on the podcast, emallson and Anomoly walk through the many issues with the new affixes slated for patch 10.1, the buggy mess that was raid testing, some of the neat new transmog from the April Trading Post, and discuss the impact of the new-style gear scaling on Vault of the Incarnates.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rsBu2ZZEMEU) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EDT over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
