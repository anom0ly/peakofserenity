At long last, Dragonflight Pre-Patch has arrived! This week on the podcast, Anomoly and emallson recap our experiences with the first week of the patch, talk about the UI updates (and how we've dealt with addons that are no longer updated), cover updates to Mythic+ and Crafting on the beta, and talk pre-patch raiding builds for Brewmaster and Mistweaver.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/hTXo_9BArl8) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EDT over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
