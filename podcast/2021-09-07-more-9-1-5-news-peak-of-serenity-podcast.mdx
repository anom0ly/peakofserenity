A steady stream of news about Patch 9.1.5 has come out over the past week---including a partial rollback of the AoE cap! This week on the podcast, our hosts Anomoly and emallson sit down to recap the announcements, as well as discuss some clarifications from last week's announcements that have come due to the launch of the 9.1.5 PTR.

This week’s show is also available on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4pww5la_h0). You can catch the show live at 1:30pm Eastern Time on Sundays over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson).
