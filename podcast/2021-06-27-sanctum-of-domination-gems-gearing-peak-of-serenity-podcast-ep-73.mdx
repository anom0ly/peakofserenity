The podcast returns after emallson's brief foray into the great outdoors! This week, our hosts emallson and Anomoly are joined by Lincoln (from the Hammer of Wrath discord) to talk about gearing in Sanctum of Domination, with a special focus on the new *Domination Gems* coming with Patch 9.1. What is the new system? How does it work? Will we be wearing Cloth gear? Probably not, but that hasn't stopped us from checking!

This week’s show was streamed on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/1069686954) and is also available on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bRdc4Eaf4iQ). You can catch the show live at 1:30pm Eastern Time on Sundays over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson).
