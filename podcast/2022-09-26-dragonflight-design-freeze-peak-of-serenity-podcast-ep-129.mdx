Blizzard has announced that design of the new talent trees has been effectively frozen---they are now focused on tuning & bugfixes. This week on the podcast, emallson and Anomoly talk about the design freeze, the upcoming testing of Vault of the Incarnates, and the impending launch of Wrath of the Lich King Classic.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/slz-FBe7W7c) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EDT over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
