Dragonflight is out. We've leveled, we've crafted, we've dragonrode, and now---finally---we raid! This week on the podcast, our hosts emallson and Anomoly talk about their experience with the first week of Mythic+ Season 1, Vault of the Incarnates progression, the Race to World First, and take a (very early!) look at the Brewmaster & Mistweaver changes slated for Patch 10.0.5.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/hJO8tYpcyEg) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EST over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
