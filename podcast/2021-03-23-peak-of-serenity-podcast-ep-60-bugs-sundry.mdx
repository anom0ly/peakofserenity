An otherwise quiet week ended with a rather large BANG as major bugs were discovered for both Windwalker and Vengeance Demon Hunter. Anomoly and emallson tackle these and other topics like Valor and the consequences of the Monk changes from 9.0.5.

As a note: emallson speculated about the possibility of a queueing bug with *Invoke Niuzao* on the show. Testing has shown that no such bug exists.

This week’s episode was streamed on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/960720629) and is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/BrbzIqxPOL4). Want to watch the show live? We stream every Saturday at 8:30pm Eastern Time.
