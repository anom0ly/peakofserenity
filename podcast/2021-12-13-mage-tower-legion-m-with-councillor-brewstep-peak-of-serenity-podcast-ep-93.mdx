Legion has returned! The Mage Tower and a few Legion keys have been out since the start of last reset, and emallson and Anomoly are joined by freshly-minted Community Council member Brewstep to talk about our experiences with them and give some tips. We also talk about the first week of the Community Council and discuss the recent updates to the Mistweaver and Brewmaster tier sets.
