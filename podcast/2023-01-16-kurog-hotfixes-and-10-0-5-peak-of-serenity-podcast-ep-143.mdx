The World of Warcraft has been unusually quiet this week, perhaps in anticipation of the upcoming 10.0.5 release. This week on the podcast, Anomoly and emallson chat about Kurog progression (and the ridiculously tight timing of the Storm tech), the slate of Mythic+ and Raid nerfs, as well as further upcoming changes to Mistweaver, Group Loot, and the Crafting Order system.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/PMrdQHHIV1Q) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EST over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
