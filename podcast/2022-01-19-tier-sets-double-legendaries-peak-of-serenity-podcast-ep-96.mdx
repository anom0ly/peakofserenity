As testing of 9.2 trundles toward its impending release, we've got some NEWS! Our hosts emallson and Anomoly sit down this week to discuss updates to how Tier Sets will be acquired and an unexpected requirement for getting your 2nd legendary.

Addendum: emallson was *rather optimistic* about the time taken for tier set acquisitions. About 30% of players will get a 2 set the first week if they do all 8 Normal and Heroic bosses with no trading, and about 70% will get a 2 set the 2nd week if they do so again.
