The long-awaited Monk legendaries (and Mistweaver buffs!!!) have finally appeared on the PTR! This week on the podcast, our hosts emallson and Anomoly dive into how they work, what they're good for, and whether they seem likely to unseat any of the current BiS legendaries. Anomoly also breaks down the Mistweaver changes (turns out the Revival change is [*very* good](/2021/05/20/mistweaver-changes-shadowlands-9-1-build-38709/)!)

This week's show was streamed on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/1034979845) and is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/M_7E_lxckmA). You can catch the show live at 1:30pm Eastern Time over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson).
