Surprise!!! Patch 9.1.5 has been announced, coming with a huge number of community-requested features like easy covenant swapping, removal of conduit energy, alt campaign skips and Timewalking Mythic+! This week on the show, our hosts Anomoly and emallson are joined by Hinalover and Abelito75 to discuss the announcement, what we're most excited about, and we think this will do for the game as a whole.

This week’s show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/jPTg3DJ_x6M). You can catch the show live at 1:30pm Eastern Time on Sundays over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson).
