**emallson** and **Anomoly** are back to recap how *Battle for Azeroth* played out. This week, we take an in-depth look at patches 8.0 and 8.1, discussing what changes we got going into the expansion and the initial round of improvements made in BFA's first major patch.

*Correction:* The buff to AP from emissaries came in [patch 8.2](https://www.wowhead.com/news=292116.2/azerite-rewards-buffed-in-patch-8-2-ptr-emissary-and-weekly-island-expeditions), not patch 8.1.

The Brewmaster changes were also discussed on [this week's episode of the Titanforge podcast](https://titanforge.libsyn.com/website/64-tanks-in-shadowlands-ksm-and-the-watcher-post).
