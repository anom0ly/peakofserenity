This weekend, Mythic+ testing opened and the first round of Mythic Raid testing began! On this episode of the podcast, our hosts emallson and Anomoly recap their experience on Mythic Terros and in Mythic+, talk about the new Thundering affix, and work through some thoughts on how Mythic+ could be made a real "3rd pillar" of WoW's endgame.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B3l54AxArf4) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EDT over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
