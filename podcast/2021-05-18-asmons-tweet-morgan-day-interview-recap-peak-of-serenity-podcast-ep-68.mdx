On this week's episode of the podcast, our co-hosts emallson and Anomoly discuss Asmongold's [recent Twitlonger](https://mobile.twitter.com/Asmongold/status/1391525202014703616) and how it relates to our own perspectives on the game. But that's not all! Slootbag did an [interview with Lead Developer Morgan Day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pszQZqG3vUA) this week and we recap the most important hits (and misses) from their chat.

This week's show was not streamed on Twitch due to network issues. It is, however, available [on Youtube](https://youtu.be/Rc5xDZGgCzk).
