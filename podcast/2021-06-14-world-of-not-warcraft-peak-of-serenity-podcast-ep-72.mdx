As the content lull drags on in (Retail) World of Warcraft, our hosts Anomoly and emallson have been spending some time with (gasp!) *other games!* This week on the show, we chat about what games we've been playing that aren't Warcraft-flavored, along with some discussion about the Retail news from the past week and (of course) The Burning Crusade.

This week’s show was streamed on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/1055958696) and is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/ysUofypCFYI). You can catch the show live at 1:30pm Eastern Time on Sundays over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson).

**Note:** There will not be an episode next week because emallson will be out of town. The podcast will resume the following week at the regular time.
