Keys are getting nerfed :) Mistweavers are getting buffed, though, so that's good! This week on the podcast, Anomoly and emallson talk through the implications of the God Comp nerf, the keystone scaling nerf, and lay out the details of the changes to Mistweaver.

We also discussed overlays, and of course immediately before posting the podcast we got a blue post confirming they will be changing the combat log to combat them (ha!) before next season: [https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/nerfing-combat-logs-will-it-happen-should-it-happen/1654695/11](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/nerfing-combat-logs-will-it-happen-should-it-happen/1654695/11)
