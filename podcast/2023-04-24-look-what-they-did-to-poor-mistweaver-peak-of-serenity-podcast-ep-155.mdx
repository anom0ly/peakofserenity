The Mistweaver tier set for Aberrus has received a substantial internal cooldown, leaving it much weakened and a new build potentially dead in the water. This week on the podcast, Anomoly and emallson go through the changes, the healer nerfs via player HP buffs, and the recent Community Council interview with the WoW team about Mythic+.
