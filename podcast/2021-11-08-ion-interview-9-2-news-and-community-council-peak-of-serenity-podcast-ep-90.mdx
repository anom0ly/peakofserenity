This week on the show, our hosts Anomoly and emallson cover the *many* bits of news that came out over the past week, including a new interview with Ion Hazzikostas, news that the first 9.2 build has reached the PTR CDN, as well as a new Brewmaster build (and new Mistweaver bugs). Of course, we must also discuss the announcement about the WoW Community Council and how that fits into the existing feedback that Blizzard already has access to. Spoiler: we're not impressed.
