The podcast is back from its IRL hiatus, and just in time for a MASSIVE info-dump about Patch 10.1! This week on the show, emallson and Anomoly recap the upcoming changes in Patch 10.0.7 (releasing next week, March 14th/15th/16th) and dive into the changes to gearing, the new raid info, and lots of info about Mythic+ coming out for Patch 10.1.

This show is also available on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/sD8PkaXfxaQ) and is streamed live every week at 1:30pm EDT over at [https://twitch.tv/emallson](https://twitch.tv/emallson)
