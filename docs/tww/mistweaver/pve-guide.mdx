    **Conduit of the Celestials is the recommended hero tree for raid, Mythic+
    and Delves this season.** It is ahead of Master of Harmony for healing,
    damage and survivability.


    Class tree talents are flexible. Anything that doesn't directly increase
    your healing or damage can be swapped for the utility a fight needs:


    - [Restoral](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388615/restoral) is the raid
    default, so you never mass-dispel by accident. Use
    [Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) in Mythic+, or in a
    raid fight that rewards dispelling everyone at once.

    - Take [Diffuse Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1243287/diffuse-magic)
    for a second personal magic dispel on top of
    [Detox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115450/detox).

    - [Niuzao's
    Protection](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442747/niuzaos-protection) and
    [Jade Sanctuary](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443059/jade-sanctuary) are
    interchangeable. Jade Sanctuary is the better pick on progression.

    - Situational picks: [Improved
    Detox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388874/improved-detox) for poisons and
    diseases, [Pressure
    Points](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450432/pressure-points) for enrage
    effects, [Swift Art](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450622/swift-art) for
    snares, [Disable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116095/disable) for a
    dangerous mob, and [Tiger Tail
    Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=264348/tiger-tail-sweep) with [Ancient
    Arts](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=344359/ancient-arts) for a bigger, more
    frequent [Leg Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292306/leg-sweep).

    - Drop [Windwalking](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=157411/windwalking) if
    another Monk in the group already has it.

    ##### Conduit of the Celestials

    **Raid Import: [C4QAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAghx2YZYzixMzyyM2wYGmZZZbmxCzoZMDYwgxYmZmhZbMGsYCAAAAgAsYZmlZbmBAAGwAMDYMMWkxMA](https://www.wowhead.com/talent-calc/blizzard/C4QAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAghx2YZYzixMzyyM2wYGmZZZbmxCzoZMDYwgxYmZmhZbMGsYCAAAAgAsYZmlZbmBAAGwAMDYMMWkxMA)**

    ![](/img/uploads/2026/11/mistweaver-raid-talents.png)

    This build is about covering the raid with [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist). Every Renewing Mist you have out speeds up your cooldowns through [Heart of the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent) and makes the others heal harder through [Misty Coalescence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1268817/misty-coalescence). [Rushing Wind Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1269159/rushing-wind-kick) extends them anywhere on the field through [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist), and [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) turns that coverage into healing with [Invigorating Mists](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274586/invigorating-mists).

    - [Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) is the default celestial, and the build gives you Haste while it's out.
    - [Energizing Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=422031/energizing-brew) is the default mana talent. Try [Lifecycles](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197915/lifecycles) on long fights if you run short, but it adds more presses to an already busy priority.
    - Keep [Refreshment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=467270/refreshment) and [Chrysalis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202424/chrysalis): extra [Life Cocoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon) casts are a big part of your mana.

    In dungeons most of your downtime goes into damage, and [Jadefire Teachings](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=467293/jadefire-teachings) turns more of it into healing through [Ancient Teachings](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388023/ancient-teachings).

    - [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) is your most important button, even while you focus one player. Ancient Teachings splits its healing across everyone who is injured, so holding it to hardcast a heal is almost always a loss.
    - The build skips the apex talent [Spiritfont](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1260677/spiritfont) for nodes with wider coverage.
    - [Invigorating Mists](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274586/invigorating-mists) is here for its [Sheilun's Gift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=399491/sheiluns-gift) healing. Its Vivify cleave is wasted with only five players.
    - [Gift of the Celestials](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388212/gift-of-the-celestials) (a celestial every minute) is the safe default. [Jade Bond](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388031/jade-bond) is better when you need long coverage for one mechanic.

    ##### Advanced

    **Apex Import: [C4QAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAghxyMLDL2MmZ22MwgZbmZbZZmxCDNjZADGwsMzMzwshZYWmJAAAAACwitZ2mtZGAAYADwMgxALyYGA](https://www.wowhead.com/talent-calc/blizzard/C4QAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAghxyMLDL2MmZ22MwgZbmZbZZmxCDNjZADGwsMzMzwshZYWmJAAAAACwitZ2mtZGAAYADwMgxALyYGA)**

    Unlike the standard Chi-Ji build, this one does take our Apex Talent, [Spiritfont](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1260677/spiritfont), and pairs it directly with [Vital Expenditure](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1299572/vital-expenditure) to buff it. The catch, and the reason this is labelled advanced, is Spiritfont's collapsing mechanic: as players are topped off, the [Soothing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) beams collapse inward, and once your group is at full health you're ultimately only healing a single person. When damage ramps up (particularly with key level), that inverts: more players spend more time below 100% health, so the beams stay spread out and continue healing all 5 players as necessary.

    Its output falls off hard when there simply isn't much damage going out on the group. The standard will be significantly easier to simply DPS your way through low to moderate healing needs.

    The loss of our Season 1 tier set means [Renewing Mist's](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) healing isn't as impactful as it used to be (and requires a lot more time manually pressing Renewing Mist to make a modicum of difference). [Mistline](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1280297/mistline) and subsequently [Rising Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274909/rising-mist) loses a good deal of value as a result, so it comes out of the build entirely here.

  dungeon: >+
    Conduit of the Celestials. Heal first, but keep weaving melee in rather than
    saving it for filler.


    1. [Sheilun's Gift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=399491/sheiluns-gift) to
    save someone who would otherwise die.

    2. [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick).

    3. [Spinning Crane
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) with a
    [Dance of Chi-Ji](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=438439/dance-of-chi-ji)
    proc.

    4. [Thunder Focus
    Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea), empowering
    [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick).
    Press it on cooldown: [Heart of the Jade
    Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent)
    rewards every use.

    5. [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) if
    someone is missing one or one is about to expire.

    6. [Enveloping Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist)
    with a [Strength of the Black
    Ox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443112/strength-of-the-black-ox) proc.

    7. [Spinning Crane
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) at 5 or more
    targets.

    8. [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) at least
    once before each [Blackout
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick).

    9. [Mana Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) as you need
    mana.


    **Melee by target count:** on 1 to 4 targets, [Rising Sun
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick), then at least
    one [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm), then
    [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick). On 5 or
    more, [Rising Sun
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) on cooldown,
    then [Spinning Crane
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick).


    If you're the only Monk, keep [Mystic
    Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8647/mystic-touch) on whatever the
    group is hitting.


    **Reading the damage in a key:**


    - One injured player gets all of [Ancient
    Teachings](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388023/ancient-teachings), so keep
    attacking. With two injured you can usually keep attacking too. At three or
    more taking moderate or heavy damage, consider a cooldown.

    - For heavy damage on one or two players, [Life
    Cocoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon) saves you casts.

    - Before a big hit, ramp with 2 or 3 [Enveloping
    Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) on the
    squishiest players (DPS first, then yourself, then the tank). Make [Rising
    Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) or a cast
    heal your first press after the damage lands.

    - Before spending a cooldown, check what will be back for the next mechanic.
    [Sheilun's Gift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=399491/sheiluns-gift) is
    usually the safest emergency button.

#### Cooldowns

- **[Thunder Focus Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea)** empowers your next [Enveloping Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) (instant, and heals its full HoT amount at once), [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) (10 seconds longer) or [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) (9 seconds off its cooldown). With Conduit of the Celestials, press it as soon as it's ready. With [Secret Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388491/secret-infusion), each empower also grants a stat: Critical Strike from Enveloping Mist, Haste from Renewing Mist, Versatility from Rising Sun Kick.
- **[Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) / [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane)**: [Gift of the Celestials](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388212/gift-of-the-celestials) halves the cooldown to 1 minute and halves the duration with it. [Jade Bond](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388031/jade-bond) keeps the 2-minute cooldown and makes the celestial much stronger. [Celestial Harmony](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343655/celestial-harmony) shields 5 allies with [Chi Cocoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=406139/chi-cocoon) when you summon. With Yu'lon, cast [Enveloping Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist) to spread [Renewing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115151/renewing-mist) through [Rapid Diffusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388847/rapid-diffusion), then [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify). Chi-Ji starts you at 4 [Teachings of the Monastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery) stacks: kick to heal through [Gust of Mists](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343819/gust-of-mists), and your Enveloping Mists become instant and cheaper.
- **[Revival](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115310/revival) / [Restoral](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388615/restoral)**: the same raid-wide heal, but Revival also dispels everyone. Restoral is the safe raid default.
- **[Life Cocoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon)**: your external. The lower the target's health, the more it's worth. [Chrysalis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202424/chrysalis) lowers the cooldown to 75 seconds.
- **[Sheilun's Gift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=399491/sheiluns-gift)** builds clouds over time and spends them all in one cast. [Veil of Pride](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=400053/veil-of-pride) builds them twice as fast, [Legacy of Wisdom](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=404408/legacy-of-wisdom) makes it hit 5 targets (great in dungeons), and [Emperor's Favor](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=471761/emperors-favor) makes it instant and stronger on the main target.
- **[Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit)** (Conduit of the Celestials) heals and damages around you and, with [Unity Within](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443589/unity-within), calls on all four celestials at once.

#### Responding to Damage

- **Light damage:** let your HoTs handle it and keep attacking. With [Jadefire Teachings](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=467293/jadefire-teachings), [Ancient Teachings](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388023/ancient-teachings) sends all of its healing to the one injured player.
- **Medium damage:** spend a [Strength of the Black Ox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443112/strength-of-the-black-ox) or [Spiritfont](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1260677/spiritfont) proc, or cast [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) or [Sheilun's Gift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=399491/sheiluns-gift) (instant with [Vivacious Vivification](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388812/vivacious-vivification)). Use [Life Cocoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon) if more damage is coming.
- **Heavy damage:** [Life Cocoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116849/life-cocoon), then channel [Soothing Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115175/soothing-mist) and chain [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify), [Sheilun's Gift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=399491/sheiluns-gift) and [Enveloping Mist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124682/enveloping-mist), which are all instant during the channel.

#### Before the Pull

- Start at full mana: drink any [Mana Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) stacks, or sit down with mana water.
- Check that [Thunder Focus Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116680/thunder-focus-tea) and your other cooldowns are back after a wipe.
- Put your flask, weapon oil and food buff on before the pull.
- Check your talents for the fight, and whether another Monk already has [Windwalking](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=157411/windwalking).

## Gearing

### Stats

**Raid:** Intellect >> Haste > Critical Strike > Versatility >> Mastery

**Mythic+:** Intellect >> Haste > Mastery > Critical Strike > Versatility

Item level comes first: a big item level upgrade usually beats better stats. Haste makes your healing stronger and brings [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [Rushing Wind Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1269159/rushing-wind-kick) back faster. Critical Strike gives extra [Mana Tea](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=197908/mana-tea) stacks. Mastery is weak in raid, but in Mythic+ [Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325197/invoke-chi-ji-the-red-crane) casts so many [Gust of Mists](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343819/gust-of-mists) that it moves ahead of Critical Strike. In high keys where one-shots are a worry, Versatility can be worth more than Critical Strike. The priorities are the same for both hero trees.

Use [QE Live](https://questionablyepic.com/live/) to compare gear for your own character.

> One thing to note here, is the greatest impact you can make in your output is improving your play over improving your gear. While higher item level gear will have an impact, proper play will always outpace "perfect" gear optimization. - Abelito75

#### One Handed vs Two Handed Weapons

**It doesn't matter.** Auto attacks are normalized, weapon damage isn't used by any of our abilities, and everything scales with Attack Power, which comes from your Spell Power.

#### Gems

Use [Indecipherable Eversong Diamond](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240983/indecipherable-eversong-diamond) in your Eversong Diamond socket, since the other versions cost too much Intellect, and [Flawless Deadly Peridot](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240890/flawless-deadly-peridot) in every other socket.

#### Tier Set

- **2-piece:** [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) deals 30% more damage and [Rushing Wind Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1269159/rushing-wind-kick) heals for 100% more.
- **4-piece:** [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [Rushing Wind Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1269159/rushing-wind-kick) can give you a free extra cast with no cooldown or mana cost. It works like a second charge, so you don't have to press it right away.

In Season 2, catalyzed tier pieces keep their stats, so you can choose which items to convert.

#### Crafting

- First craft: [Aln'hara Cane](https://www.wowhead.com/item=245770/alnhara-cane) with [Darkmoon Sigil: Void](https://www.wowhead.com/item=245874/darkmoon-sigil-void).
- Best embellishment on armor: [Arcanoweave Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240167/arcanoweave-lining).

#### "Best-in-Slot"

Fixed best-in-slot lists only describe a finished character. While you gear up, judge each item on its own: item level first, then stats, using [QE Live](https://questionablyepic.com/live/).

## Miscellaneous

#### Consumables

- Flask – [Flask of the Blood Knights](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241324/flask-of-the-blood-knights), or Flask of the Shattered Sun if you have too much Haste.
- Potion – [Potion of Recklessness](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241288/potion-of-recklessness). Mana potions in combat aren't recommended.
- Health potion – [Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion](https://www.wowhead.com/item=271884/concentrated-silvermoon-health-potion)
- Weapon oil – [Thalassian Phoenix Oil](https://www.wowhead.com/item=243734/thalassian-phoenix-oil)
- Food – [Hearty Harandar Celebration](https://www.wowhead.com/item=266996/hearty-harandar-celebration), or [Hearty Royal Roast](https://www.wowhead.com/item=242747/hearty-royal-roast) on your own.
- Mana water – any of the Cooking teas (Argentleaf, Sanguithorn, Azeroot, Mana Lily or Tranquility Bloom). Buy whichever is cheapest.

#### Racials

Racials make only a small difference, so pick the race you like. If you want to min-max:

- **Night Elf:** [Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984/shadowmeld) can drop certain Mythic+ mechanics, and [Touch of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154748/touch-of-elune) gives Haste inside instances.
- **Dwarf / Dark Iron Dwarf:** [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594/stoneform) and [Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221/fireblood) remove bleeds. Stoneform's damage reduction makes Dwarf popular for high keys.
- **Mag'har Orc:** [Ancestral Call](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274738/ancestral-call) now lines up with [Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322118/invoke-yulon-the-jade-serpent) for extra Haste in raid.

#### Professions

Professions have little effect on your healing. **Pick whatever makes your play time easier and more enjoyable.** Leatherworking can craft armor close to best-in-slot, and Alchemy or Enchanting save you trips to the Auction House.

#### WoWAnalyzer

[WoWAnalyzer](https://wowanalyzer.com/) reviews your logs automatically.

## Addons and UI

Midnight limits what addons can do and builds much more into the default interface, including the Cooldown Manager. Set that up first, then add only what you miss.

- **Cooldown Manager and full UI imports:** Wowhead's [Mistweaver UI & Macros page](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/monk/mistweaver/addons-macro-ui-imports) has ready-made Mistweaver layouts.
- **Clique** for click-casting, and **Grid2** for lighter, more flexible raid frames.
- **Better Cooldown Manager** to customise the Cooldown Manager.
- **Plater** for nameplates.
- **Boss mods and meters:** BigWigs with LittleWigs, or Deadly Boss Mods, plus Details.

## Macros

**Mouseover Macro Example:**

```
#showtooltip
/cast [@mouseover,nodead,help] [] Vivify
```

**Detox on Mouseover:**

```
#showtooltip Detox
/cast [@mouseover,help,nodead] Detox; Detox
```

**Talent Swapping Macro Example (Uses Revival / Restoral):**

```
#showtooltip [known:Revival] Revival; [known:Restoral] Restoral
/cast [known:Revival] Revival; [known:Restoral] Restoral
```

If you don't use the English client, use the spell ID in the [known:] conditional, like this:

```
#showtooltip [known:325197]Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane; [known:322118]Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent
/cast [known:325197]Invoke Chi-Ji, the Red Crane; [known:322118]Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent
```
