  intro: >-
    ### Instructions


    Windwalkers generally prioritize our abilities by how much damage they do
    and the resources (Chi, energy, execution time) that they cost. There are a
    couple of general goals while playing Windwalker that should help with the
    bigger picture before diving into the priority lists below:


    - **Manage Energy and Chi -** Outside of big Haste cooldowns, Windwalker's
    damage is restricted by how many resources we can generate and spend. This
    means that sitting at the Energy cap or generating more Chi than you have
    space for typically results in lost damage potential. Your focus is
    balancing having enough Chi for spenders and using [Blackout
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) just enough to
    prevent overcapping without using it too much and not having the Chi you
    need when a bigger spender becomes available.
      - During major cooldowns, you will often have more Energy and Chi than you can use due to the GCD and cooldowns of Chi spenders, making avoiding the caps less impactful, but still something to be aware of.
    - **Keep Abilities on cooldown -** With most of our Chi spenders having
    their own cooldowns, it is important to reduce the amount of time that these
    harder-hitting abilities are sitting off cooldown without being used. There
    will, inevitably, be overlaps where you'll have to choose what to press
    based on the priority list. A key part of playing Windwalker recognizing
    that one of these situations is approaching and trying to have enough Chi
    for what you need to do.

    - **React to procs -** Windwalker has several procs that you'll need to
    manage in order to maximize damage potential. The main ones are [Dance of
    Chi-Ji](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325201/dance-of-chi-ji) and [Blackout
    Kick!](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116768/blackout-kick). Both of these
    can stack to 2 so that you don't need to immediately use them. However, you
    will want to avoid risking overstacking, as that is lost damage potential.
    These procs can play an integral part in helping you balance your resources
    while still benefitting from [Mastery: Combo
    Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes). They
    can also throw off what you were planning, so being able to react
    intelligently and quickly is important.

    - **Do not break
    [mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes).**
    Simple as that, do not repeat the same ability twice in a row. For a full
    list of what counts for mastery, see
    [here](/df/windwalker/pve-guide/references/). There are currently no
    situations where it is worth it to break mastery as there have been in the
    past.


    Following these rules is the largest part of playing Windwalker. You may
    notice that "always be casting" is not one of our core tenets, as you are
    only pressing something if it aligns with these rules (something comes off
    cooldown, you are close to capping energy, etc). This means that as a
    natural consequence of following these, you may not always fill every GCD.
    **WINDWALKER IS A SPEC WITH NATURAL DOWNTIME**. This is not an indication
    that you are playing incorrectly or that you need more Haste (which will not
    really affect this). To execute these rules in the most efficient way,
    follow the priorities below with the goal of never using something lower in
    the list if it will delay something higher in the list.


    ### Cooldowns


    As said above, the safest bet is to use things as close to their cooldown as
    possible. However, if you can use most cooldowns at the same time without
    losing a cast of them, you'll get the most out of them. You can read more
    about it in [this article by
    Talby](/2021/07/31/windwalker-approaching-cooldowns-and-desyncs/).

    Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit)**


    [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit)
    is a powerful ability to use, but the most powerful aspect of it is that it
    adds the [Heart of the Jade

    Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) or
    [Whirling Dragon
    Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch), you need
    to stagger these uses for maximum benefit. This generally means using
    [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit)
    spaced out between casts of [Strike of the
    Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) or
    [Whirling Dragon
    Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch). This
    attempts to insure maximum uptime and value.


    The instance of [Heart of the Jade
    Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent)
    that is triggered from using
    [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) stacks with the
    version of [Heart of the Jade
    Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent)
    that comes from [Celestial
    Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit), [Strike of
    the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord),

    Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) does not

  fortifyingbrew: >-
    **[Fortifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew)**


    [Fortifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew)
    increases your maximum health, so it also increases the damage that [Touch
    of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) deals and
    that [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma)
    can absorb. Because of this, using [Fortifying
    Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew) before you use
    [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) or
    [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) is a
    damage increase. This can be used to further improve your defense in a time
    of need, but it also has the secondary effect of increasing the amount of
    damage that is redirected back to the target.


    If you are not going to need the defensive value of [Fortifying
    Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew) on its own, like
    if large damage intake is more frequent and you need to stagger your
    defensive cooldowns, then you can stack them together for added benefit.
    Just be sure to use [Fortifying
    Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew) BEFORE using
    [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) or
    [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) as
    both snapshot on use.


    A real good way to use this is to find a way to use [Fortifying
    Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew) then [Touch of
    Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) at a time when
    you'll also be able to use [Touch of
    Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) during the
    duration of [Fortifying
    Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew). This gets you
    both benefits for one use. If you're already going to use it for DPS
    purposes, then you might as well be efficient about it.

  touchofdeath: >-
    **[Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death)**


    [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) seems
    like a straightforward ability to use but attempting to min/max it can lead
    to losing damage rather than gaining it. With the change to [Fatal
    Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=394123/fatal-touch) in Midnight, it no
    longer provides a damage increase after using it.


    While this generally means that you'll want to use it as soon as its
    available and you have space to press it without delaying another ability,
    it also means that you CAN try to use it right before the target dies when
    your target has less health than your maximum health so that it deals 100%
    of your health rather than just the 35% of your maximum health that it deals
    if you use it on a target that is at 15% health or lower.


    This is a very small window and [Touch of
    Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) can sometimes
    have a delay before being usable if you just swap to a target to try and hit
    it with [Touch of
    Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death). This delay can
    be the difference between making or missing the very small window. Missing
    the window generally means that you don't get [Touch of
    Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death) off and thus
    lose damage that you would have done if you had cast it sooner.


    In Mythic+ trash you generally want to use this ASAP as the window to do so
    may be small. On bosses in Raid and Mythic+ you typically have a longer
    window to use it, so you want to prioritize other abilities with shorter
    cooldowns like [Fists of
    Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) as long as you
    cast [Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109/touch-of-death)
    before the fight is over.
  touchofkarma: >-
    **[Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma)**


    [Touch of Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) is,
    first and foremost, a defensive ability. Its primary use is to absorb some
    damage in order to lessen the damage that you are dealt. However, the
    secondary function of [Touch of
    Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) is that some of
    the damage that the shield absorbs is redirected back to the target that you
    cast the spell on. This means that [Touch of
    Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) can be used
    offensively if you do not need its defensive value.


    Most fights in most content have times where you will be taking damage and
    can cleanly use [Touch of
    Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) for both its
    defensive and offensive value. However, sometimes the damage you are taking
    isn't enough to necessitate a defensive cooldown like [Touch of
    Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma). In these
    situations it is, technically, optimal to find some damage that you can then
    absorb and redirect. This can be done through standing in some damage effect
    on the ground, or in extreme situations, casting
    [Provoke](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115546/provoke) on a target and
    taking a few smacks.


    Needing to [Provoke](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115546/provoke) a target
    to get the benefit from [Touch of
    Karma](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122470/touch-of-karma) should be used
    as a last resort and only ever be done with the express permission of your
    tanks and healers as you're often likely to get killed once the shield ends
    without direct intervention. **Do this at your own risk.**
  zenith: >+
    **[Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith)**


    [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) doesn't provide any
    direct damage increase without the addition of additional talents like the
    Apex Talents of [Tigereye
    Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1261703/tigereye-brew). However, it does
    reduce the cost of all of your Chi spenders by 1 and increases the cooldown
    reduction provided by [Blackout
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) by an additional
    second. When you press the button it resets the cooldown of [Rising Sun
    Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and provides you
    an additional 2 Chi to use right away.


    Key takeaways are:


    - While [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) is active,
    [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) becomes

### Ability Usage Notes

- **[Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=261916/blackout-kick)** – Generally used between other abilities to "dump" excess resources.
  - **[Teachings of the Monastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116645/teachings-of-the-monastery)** – Has currently no impact on the priority. May be relevant in another patch again.
- **[Blackout Kick!](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116768/blackout-kick) (Free Proc)** – Used before you would potentially overcap on procs (you can hold 2 at a time).
- **[Crackling Jade Lightning](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning)** – Not used anymore.
- **[Dance of Chi-Ji](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=286587/dance-of-chi-ji)** – Simply use the proc on [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick) (according to priority, and even in single target) and spend it quickly enough to avoid overcapping on procs (you can hold 2 at a time).
- **[Hit Combo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo) and [Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115636/mastery-combo-strikes)** – Generally, any ability that directly deals damage both benefits from and continues Mastery and [Hit Combo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196740/hit-combo), as well as a few that don't directly deal damage. For a full list, see [here](/df/windwalker/pve-guide/references/). Currently, **there are no cases where we want to break Mastery** that would outweigh its damage bonus.
- **[Heart of the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent?spellModifier=137025)** – This is a buff that is applied by various abilities when running Conduit of the Celestials. It reduces the cooldown of several rotational abilities, and greatly increases the channeling speed of [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury). The buff is applied by [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) or [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) after first unlocking the hero talent, but also after unlocking [Unity Within](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=443589/unity-within), a more powerful version is also applied by [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) (as one of the "assists" of the Celestials). **These two versions do not stack with each other.** An additional version of the buff comes from [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=1249625/zenith). This version **does** stack with the other two.
- **[Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick?spellModifier=137025) then [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch)** – This term is used to describe the [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick?spellModifier=137025) that enables the [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) and the [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) itself. This line, when used, does not apply to [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick?spellModifier=137025) unless [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) will be used before [Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick?spellModifier=137025) will be ready again. However, they don't have to be cast in direct succession.
- **[Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)** – It is finally possible again to cast abilities when [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick)'s global has passed and before its channel has finished. This applies to any ability that has no cast or channel time. [Vivify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116670/vivify) buffed by [Viviacious Vivification](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388812/vivacious-vivification) will still interrupt [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=101546/spinning-crane-kick).
- **[Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) & [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch)** – Usage depends on the current build.
  - **Shado-Pan builds** use [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) and [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) on cooldown unless a damage amplifier is in the near future or you plan on using Zenith soon. (3 seconds at max)
  - **Conduit of the Celestial builds** rely a lot on stacking [Heart of the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent?spellModifier=137025) windows. Holding [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) and [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is not recommended unless you have a damage amplifier starting within the next few seconds. It should generally be followed by casting [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=1249625/zenith) to overlap their [Heart of the Jade Serpent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443294/heart-of-the-jade-serpent?spellModifier=137025) windows to multiply the effects. Additionally, a proper big burst window is crucial for Conduit of the Celestial builds. Delaying [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) or [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) is recommended when the next big burst window with [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) and [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) will start within the next 10 seconds. You will instead delay [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) if it comes off cooldown while [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) or [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) will come off cooldown in the next 10 seconds.
- **[Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272696/zenith-stomp)** – This appears during [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) and has two charges to use and you want to use both charges before [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) runs out. It can be cast during [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr-2/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) without stopping its channel. You generally want to use it after any ability with a cooldown, and before "spammable" abilities like [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=261916/blackout-kick). The precise place in the priority can be found in that part of the guide.

## Macros

##### Don't Clip Things

```
#showtooltip Tiger Palm
/stopmacro [channeling:Fists of Fury]
/cast Tiger Palm
```

**OR**

```
#showtooltip Tiger Palm
/cast [nochanneling:Fists of Fury]Tiger Palm
```

Change [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) to whatever ability you want. This macro prevents you from cancelling the channel of [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury). With 12.1 cancelling [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit?spellModifier=137025) is now a thing which makes us return to the old format of using a single stopmacro line rather than conditionals that prevent your cast from being executed during specific channels. It is recommended to use this on your main rotational abilities as it's very very unlikely that we'll look to cancel these abilities.

Here is a version of the Don't Clip Things Macro that swaps between [Whirling Dragon Punch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=152175/whirling-dragon-punch) and [Strike of the Windlord](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392983/strike-of-the-windlord) depending on what you have talented.

```
#showtooltip [known:392983] Strike of the Windlord; Whirling Dragon Punch
/stopmacro [channeling:Fists of Fury]
/cast [known:152175] Whirling Dragon Punch
/cast [known:392983] Strike of the Windlord
```

##### Fists of Fury and Unity Within

```
#showtooltip Fists of Fury
/cast Unity Within
/cast Fists of Fury
```

This macro is required due to a slight issue with the execution of things in the game. If you cancel your [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) with a [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) the end of channel happens at the exact time the new channel is started resulting in the application of your Heart of the Jade Serpent window that you get from [Unity Within](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443589/unity-within) happening after your [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) channel has already snapshot the duration of the channel. [Unity Within](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443589/unity-within) is affected by your global cooldown while it doesnt cause one itself. This behaviour requires you to use this macro right after the current global cooldown has finished to execute [Unity Within](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443589/unity-within) properly in time for [Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) to snapshot the duration of the channel properly. There is sadly no other solution for this at the current point.

##### Zenith Stomp and Tiger Palm

```
#showtooltip
/cast Zenith Stomp
/cast Tiger Palm
```

This macro layers [Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272696/zenith-stomp) on top of [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) so you can treat [Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272696/zenith-stomp) as a glorified [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm). It abuses the fact that you unlearn [Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272696/zenith-stomp) as ability once either [Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272696/zenith-stomp) stacks are consumed or [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) ends. It can bug out if you enter any loading screen while you still have stacks remaining so pay attention to that. If you want to add the Don't Clip conditionals feel free to do so.

##### Burst Macro

```
#showtooltip Zenith
/use [pet][known:450615]13
/use [mod:shift,pet][mod:shift,known:450615]item:212971
/use [mod:shift,pet][mod:shift,known:450615]item:212265
/cast Zenith
```

This Macro uses your on-use stat trinket and potions in the correct order to allow your [Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272694/zenith-stomp) from casting [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) to be affected by the stat buffs as well. However, this macro has a few nuances you need to adjust for your personal use, so here is an explanation for all lines and how to change them appropriately.\
**/use [pet][known:450615]13**This line uses your trinket in slot 13 whenever you have an active pet **OR** when you are talented into Shado-Pan by utilizing the very first passive point [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes?spellModifier=137025). For Windwalker the only active pet that triggers this condition is a summoned Xuen from [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) by talenting into Conduit of the Celestials. If you have your on-use stat trinket on slot 14 you need to adjust this line. If you don't use a trinket that should be used that close between Xuen and Zenith or don't have one (example for the first would be a trinket like [Algeth'ar Puzzle Box](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193701/algethar-puzzle-box)) you should delete this line to make sure you don't accidentally misuse the trinket you have.
**/use [mod:shift,pet][mod:shift,known:450615]item:212971**\
**/use [mod:shift,pet][mod:shift,known:450615]item:212265**\
Those two lines allow you to use less binds by using a shift modifier to pop your potion at the same time as your trinket, right before your [Zenith Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1272694/zenith-stomp) from casting [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith). The used IDs are the [Fleeting Tempered Potion](https://www.wowhead.com/item=212971/fleeting-tempered-potion) and the normal [Tempered Potion](https://www.wowhead.com/item=212265/tempered-potion), both at rank 3. By using this order given in the macro, you will first use your existing Fleeting Potions before starting to use your personal property. If you use rank 2 or even rank 1 potions, you can add those lines by using the same format with the IDs you can find on wowhead. If you want to use different modifiers, you can also replace shift by ctrl or alt or even get rid of it fully. Just be aware that you may end up consuming all your precious potions in content you may not want to use them for if you remove the modifier condition.\
**/cast Zenith**Simply casts [Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) as the very last thing inside this macro.

##### Seperating Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger and Celestial Conduit

When you want to seperate [Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=123904/invoke-xuen-the-white-tiger) and [Celestial Conduit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443028/celestial-conduit) as seperate keybinds, just because you are used to it from your time in The War Within you can use the following two macros.\
This is for Xuen:

```
#showtooltip
/cast [nopet]Invoke Xuen, the White Tiger
```

And this is for Celestial Conduit

```
#showtooltip Celestial Conduit
/cast [pet]Celestial Conduit
```

If you want their icons to properly show you have to hard assign the icon through the pre selection of the icon yourself.

##### Focus Cast Macros

Windwalker has a number of spells you may want to target using the /Focus targetting option. You can replace the *sample text*in the macro below with spells such as [Spear Hand Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116705/spear-hand-strike), [Paralysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115078/paralysis), or [Detox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115450/detox) depending on your preference:

```
#showtooltip
/cast [@focus,exists] Sample Text
```

##### Mouseover Macros

In some cases being able to cast spells/heals on targets without having to switch targets is extremely useful. You can use the following macro to make the desired spell cast on the target you are currently mousing over instead of your active target. Replace the *sample text* with the desired spell name:

```
#showtooltip
/cast [@mouseover] Sample Text
```

##### Ring of Peace Placement

The following two macros will place [Ring of Peace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116844/ring-of-peace) without having to use a targeting circle to do so The first will place if at your feet when pressed immediately:

```
#showtooltip
/cast [@player] Ring of Peace
```

And this will place it where your cursor is currently targeting:

```
#showtooltip
/cast [@cursor] Ring of Peace
```
