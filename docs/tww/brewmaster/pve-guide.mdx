  heading: Brewmaster Monk Ability Priority
  intro: |
    ---

    Brewmasters follow a priority system rather than a strict rotation, based on which abilities are coming off cooldown and how much Energy is available. In addition, use of this priority system impacts your unique defensive tools, many of which get reduced cooldowns from your offensive abilities. With this in mind, your main pillars of playing Brewmaster are the following:

    - 🟧 **Blackout Kick and Keg Smash are cast on** **cooldown:** simply put, [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) is **the** most important ability you have as a Brewmaster. It both deals a large amount of your total damage, and gives plenty of defensive benefits. [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) falls into a similar spot, providing both damage and defense on a low cooldown.

    - **🟧 Stagger is not damage reduction by itself:** Although [Stagger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115069/stagger) does not make you take the full amount of damage from a hit right away, if you do not do anything about the damage in your Stagger pool you are still taking the full hit over a period of 10 or 15 seconds. Making proper use of [Purifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119582/purifying-brew) in particular is the biggest difference between an average Brewmaster and a great one.

    - 🟧 **Use the right cooldown for the job:** Brewmasters have four main defensive cooldowns in Midnight: [Fortifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388917/fortifying-brew) (and its modifier talents), [Invoke Niuzao, the Black Ox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=132578/invoke-niuzao-the-black-ox), [Celestial Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew)/[Celestial Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241059/celestial-infusion), and [Exploding Keg](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325153/exploding-keg). Some of these abilities have cooldown timers that are much longer than the others, and some have their cooldowns reduced by your other abilities and talents. While this will make things complicated in judging the "value" of using one, in general you should consider abilities with longer cooldowns as being your "stronger" ones that you should be more judicious about using. In addition, every cooldown except for Fortifying Brew has a distinct offensive bonus, encouraging you to trigger them on cooldown if there isn't a planned defensive use.

    - 🟧 **Customize your mitigation:** Brewmasters have the option to choose certain talents that greatly affect their defensive playstyle. First among these is [Celestial Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew) versus [Celestial Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241059/celestial-infusion). While these two talents are very similar, the key difference between them is that Celestial Brew will absorb *all* damage over a shorter duration, whereas Infusion will absorb only *some* damage taken over a longer time period. This tends to result in a smoother experience for your healers, as some damage is still able to reduce your health and be offset by any passive forms of healing that may be wasted otherwise. Next is [Niuzao's Resolve](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241097/niuzaos-resolve), which converts the healing spheres a Brewmaster generates into a heal-over-time effect rather than an instant one. This, too, creates an easier time for counteracting the damage you receive, although you lose the ability to quickly top yourself off in an emergency with Expel Harm (this usually wastes a **large** amount of healing, however). Overall, **you will usually opt into Celestial Infusion and Niuzao's Resolve,** with more advanced or difficult content occasionally switching to Celestial Brew and regular healing spheres.

    - **🟧 Your Hero Talents are almost entirely passive:** Whether you are a 🟧 Shado-Pan or 🟦 Master of Harmony Brewmaster, your Hero Talent effects will passively occur as you perform the standard rotation. For the most part, there isn't much to worry about in the way of optimizing their use. Just remember that you will need to use [Celestial Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew) to activate [Aspect of Harmony](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450508/aspect-of-harmony?spellModifier=137023) and spend your 🟦 vitality!

    Although these four big guidelines may seem daunting to follow, the Brewmaster ability priority will become second-nature over time, as many of your effects are passively performed by following it correctly. The hard part is getting over the initial learning curve (and number of keybinds). With that in mind, here is your full ability priority as a Brewmaster Monk.

### Brewmaster Monk Apex Talent Usage:

- Bring Me Another has up to four ranks that can be talented; a Brewmaster will always select them all regardless of build
- [Bring Me Another](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265129/bring-me-another) Rank 1 causes your [Purifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119582/purifying-brew?spellModifier=451036) to occasionally replace [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) with an Empty Barrel that will deal more damage and bounce to nearby enemies.
- [Bring Me Another](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265138/bring-me-another?def=141838&rank=2) Ranks 2 and 3 result in Empty Barrel procs resetting the cooldown of [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) and reducing its Energy cost by 50/100%, while also passively increasing all Physical damage you deal by 10/20%.
- [Bring Me Another](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265141/bring-me-another) Rank 4 allows [Celestial Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew?spellModifier=450892)/[Celestial Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241059/celestial-infusion?spellModifier=450892) and [Fortifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115203/fortifying-brew) to guarantee a trigger of Empty Barrel, and also cause Empty Barrel casts to provide you with a strong heal-over-time effect ([Refreshing Drink](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265145/refreshing-drink)).

These Apex Talents will be a largely passive part of your rotation. Throwing your Empty Barrels is a high priority, but not as important as continuing to cast [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) on cooldown. Don't forget that the rank 4 effect means you should still aim to cast [Celestial Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew?spellModifier=450892)/Infusion in low-damage areas for additional triggers!

### Brewmaster Monk Hero Talent Usage:

- If you are playing as a Shado-Pan Brewmaster, your [Flurry Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450615/flurry-strikes) passive will occur as you perform your rotation and follow the priority above. Do **not** bother delaying any casts of Keg Smash to try to trigger more Flurry Strikes at once. Instead, focus on casting [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) and [Breath of Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115181/breath-of-fire) as much as possible while [Invoke Niuzao](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=132578/invoke-niuzao-the-black-ox) is active.
- Master of Harmony Brewmasters passively generate vitality from dealing damage and healing (overhealing does *not* count). By casting [Celestial Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew)/[Celestial Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241059/celestial-infusion?spellModifier=450892), you will spend this vitality to apply a bonus damage over time effect to your enemies. However, you **cannot build vitality in the spending period.**

### Brewmaster Monk Ability Usage:

- [**Keg Smash**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) – Your highest-priority Energy ability; also grants [Shuffle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322120/shuffle) and cooldown reduction to your "Brew" abilities.
- **[Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=261916/blackout-kick)** – Your highest-priority **free** ability; also grants [Shuffle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322120/shuffle) to improve your [Stagger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115069/stagger).
- [**Breath of Fire**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115181/breath-of-fire)– A damage-over-time effect that also reduces damage enemies deal to you. This ability's cooldown does not matter when using the [Sal'salabim's Strength](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383697/salsalabims-strength) talent.
- [**Gift of the Ox**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124502/gift-of-the-ox)/[**Spirit of The Ox**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=400629/spirit-of-the-ox)– You will passively generate a Healing Sphere for every 100% of your maximum health taken as damage (before counting any Armor or mitigation). Additional spheres will also be generated from [Black Ox Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick).
- **[Expel Harm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322101/expel-harm)** – A cheap, low-cooldown heal that will also immediately gather any healing spheres from [Gift of the Ox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124502/gift-of-the-ox)/[Spirit of The Ox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=400629/spirit-of-the-ox). Avoid casting when talented into [Niuzao's Resolve](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241097/niuzaos-resolve).
- **[Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm)/[Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322729/spinning-crane-kick)** – Your main "filler" abilities that also cost Energy. In general, use [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) in single-target and [Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322729/spinning-crane-kick) only when gathering at the start of a pull.
- **[Charred Passions](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=386965/charred-passions)/[Dragonfire Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383994/dragonfire-brew)** – Two competing choice talents that both interact with [Breath of Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115181/breath-of-fire). Due to [Sal'salabim's Strength](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383697/salsalabims-strength), both talents are competitive in single-target, though [Dragonfire Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383994/dragonfire-brew) has an advantage against 2 or more targets.
- **[Rushing Jade Wind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847/rushing-jade-wind)/[Special Delivery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196730/special-delivery)** – Two choices on the same talent node that increase your damage. Mantaining [Rushing Jade Wind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847/rushing-jade-wind)'s buff is your lowest priority if you have it.
- [**Purifying Brew**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119582/purifying-brew) – Used to reduce your [Stagger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115069/stagger)ed damage and trigger [Special Delivery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196730/special-delivery)/[Bring Me Another](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265129/bring-me-another); gets cooldown reduction from [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) and [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm).
- [**Celestial Brew**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507/celestial-brew)/[**Celestial Infusion**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241059/celestial-infusion) – Cast this ability more or less on cooldown, as it offers defensive benefits and gets cooldown reduction from [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) and [Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm). It will also force a trigger of [Bring Me Another](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265141/bring-me-another).
- **[Fortifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388917/fortifying-brew)** – your primary defensive cooldown. Use as appropriate based on how dangerous the damage being taken is; will also force a trigger of [Bring Me Another](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265141/bring-me-another).
- [**Exploding Keg**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=325153/exploding-keg) – You deal additional damage to all targets hit by this ability for 3 seconds based on the number of hits you deal. If anything, make sure to have [Rushing Jade Wind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116847/rushing-jade-wind) active during this time if you have it talented.
- **[Invoke Niuzao, the Black Ox](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=132578/invoke-niuzao-the-black-ox)** – deals substantial damage by [Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=227291/stomp)ing after every use of [Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) while active. In addition, 40% of your [Stagger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115069/stagger) damage is instead taken by him, and he will be healed via [Oxen Fortitude](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242197/oxen-fortitude) when you purify Stagger.
- **[Ox Stance](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=455068/ox-stance) –** If talented, **t**his buff will be generated as you cast [Purifying Brew](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=119582/purifying-brew?spellModifier=383700). It will then automatically be consumed against especially dangerous hits to increase your [Stagger](https://www.wowhead.com/beta/spell=115069/stagger)'s strength against them.
- **[Touch of Death](http://www.wowhead.com/spell=322109)​** – Use when it can be triggered. If you are looking to really maximize its power, you can make sure to only use it when it will kill the target, as this can do a maximum of 100% of your HP as damage instead of the 35% it normally does to boss targets (and only if [Improved Touch of Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322113/improved-touch-of-death) is talented).

## Brewmaster Monk Gearing in Midnight Season 2

---

### Stats

Offense: Agility (item level) > Critical Strike = Versatility > Mastery > Haste

Defense: Item level (Armor/Stamina/Agility) > Mastery = Versatility = Critical Strike > Haste

**The offensive stat priority can change for your character depending on your current gear and stats. Stat weights are largely an outdated thought. Sim the gear you have through [Top Gear](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear). If you're looking for what gear to aim for, use [Droptimizer](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/droptimizer).**

### Dual Wielding vs 2 Handed Weapons

Generally, 2-handed weapons win with full enchants and consumables outside of single-target. It is possible for dual-wielding to pull ahead based on your current gear, however. For specific situations or item level gaps, sim your character's options with [Raidbot’s Top Gear Tool](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear).

### Gems

Use [Raidbot’s Top Gear Tool](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear)for the most specific and accurate results for your character, or default to [Flawless Deadly Lapis](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240914/flawless-deadly-lapis)/[Flawless Versatile Garnet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240910/flawless-versatile-garnet). In general, this will usually follow the stat priority above. For your Eversong Diamond, default to the [Indecipherable Eversong Diamond](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240983/indecipherable-eversong-diamond) for its increased Agility.

### Tier Set

- 2-piece bonus - [Breath of Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115181/breath-of-fire) ignites your next [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash), causing it to explode on contact, dealing additional Flamestrike damage to its primary target and reduced damage to additional targets.
- 4-piece bonus -  Ignited [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash)es cause enemies struck to take 8% increased damage from your Physical abilities, and leave a patch of flames on the ground, dealing Fire damage each second for 5 seconds.

The Venomous Abyss contains the [Guile of the Monkey King](https://www.wowhead.com/item-set=2061/guile-of-the-monkey-king) tier set. This set's bonuses are entirely passive and feature bonus damage to your [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash?spellModifier=322120:383707) by casting [Breath of Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115181/breath-of-fire). In addition, each cast of Keg Smash turned into a [Hot Potato](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1301477/hot-potato) this way will create [Fiery Shrapnel](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1301619/fiery-shrapnel) while setting your enemies [Aflame](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1301418/aflame), leaving them [Scorched](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1301410/scorched) and vulnerable to further Physical damage. Neither of these bonuses will majorly impact your overall rotation, though you will want to use Breath of Fire before entering combat to immediately trigger Hot Potato and Scorched with your initial Keg Smash. Fortunately [Sal'salabim's Strength](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383697/salsalabims-strength) means you will still have it available afterward.

In Season 2 of Midnight, the Catalyst can also be used to create tier pieces with secondary stats matching the original item used for a given slot. While this does create more flexibility, it also results in a theoretical "best" combination, including the possibility of getting Crit/Vers in all five major tier slots! For those that want the very best, no matter how unlikely, seek out the following:

- **Shoulders -** [Snakeskin Spaulders](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=273774)
- **Chest -** [Hide of Pestilence](https://www.wowhead.com/item=251226)
- **Gloves -** [Subjugator's Chilling Grips](https://www.wowhead.com/item=193758?bonus=657:10358:5871:7981)
- **Legs -** [Coiled Hex Legguards](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=268225) (only at 344) or "original" stats ([Venomcursed Relic](https://www.wowhead.com/ptr/item=270919/venomcured-relic))

### "Best-in-Slot" Gear

Sim the gear you have through [Top Gear](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear) to determine your current "best-in-slot" gear. If you're looking for what gear to aim for, use [Droptimizer](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/droptimizer). Otherwise, prioritize equipping gear of a higher item level; Agility, Armor, and Stamina are the biggest impact to your overall survival in gear, along with properly using your abilities. Similarly, for defense, getting "more" secondaries takes priority over getting "better" ones.

## Brewmaster Monk Macros

---

### All-in-One Taunt Macro

```
#showtooltip Provoke
/cast [nomod,@mouseover,harm,nodead] Provoke
/cast [nomod] Provoke
/targetexact [mod:alt] Black Ox Statue
/cast [mod:alt] Provoke
/targetlasttarget [mod:alt,exists]
```

This macro will allow you to use [Provoke](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115546/provoke) in a variety of ways. It can target whatever you are currently mousing over, or if the modifier key is used, target your Black Ox Statue for its aoe-taunt capability. Should alt not be the key you wish to use for statue-taunting, you can modify that section of the macro.

### Passive [Double Barrel](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202335/double-barrel) Macro

```
#showtooltip Keg Smash
/cast Double Barrel
/cast Keg Smash
```

This macro will automatically make use of the [Double Barrel](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202335/double-barrel) Honor Talent if you have it selected and cast it at the same time as your [Keg Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253/keg-smash) whenever the ability is off-cooldown. It will still work even if you do not have this Honor Talent selected and is useful if you would just like to benefit from its passive damage increase and stun effect every 45 seconds while out in the world.
